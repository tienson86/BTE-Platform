# 1. PRODUCT MISSION

BTE Platform exists to transform complex BaZi calculations into
professional, explainable and trustworthy consultation reports.

The mission is not to display astrology data.

The mission is to help people understand themselves,
understand their opportunities,
understand their risks,
and make better life decisions.

Every screen must support this mission.

Nothing may exist merely because it is technically possible.

Every visual element must answer a real user need.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. PRODUCT VISION

BTE aims to become the world's most professional BaZi
analysis and consultation platform.

Unlike traditional BaZi software,
BTE does not stop at calculation.

Unlike AI chat,
BTE does not generate unsupported opinions.

Unlike simple report generators,
BTE explains every important conclusion.

The platform combines:

• Traditional BaZi knowledge

• Rule-based reasoning

• Explainable analysis

• Knowledge references

• Human-readable consultation

into one coherent experience.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Primary Users

• Individuals seeking professional BaZi consultation

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 5. PRODUCT VALUE

Customers do not purchase calculations.

Customers purchase clarity.

Customers do not purchase charts.

Customers purchase understanding.

Customers do not purchase rules.

Customers purchase trustworthy recommendations.

Therefore every screen must answer:

"What does this mean for me?"

before

"How is this calculated?"

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 6. PRODUCT PRINCIPLES

Principle 1

Understanding before Calculation.

Users first understand.

Details come later.

--------------------------------------------------

Principle 2

Conclusion before Evidence.

Readers first see the conclusion.

Evidence follows.

--------------------------------------------------

Principle 3

Evidence before Authority.

Rules support conclusions.

Knowledge supports rules.

Classical references support knowledge.

--------------------------------------------------

Principle 4

Human Reading.

Every report must read naturally.

Never resemble JSON.

Never resemble database tables.

Never resemble debug output.

--------------------------------------------------

Principle 5

Progressive Disclosure.

Only show complexity when users request it.

Do not overload first-time readers.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 7. USER QUESTIONS

The report must answer these questions in order.

Within 3 seconds

Who am I?

Within 10 seconds

Is my chart generally strong or weak?

Within 30 seconds

What are my strengths?

What are my weaknesses?

Within 2 minutes

Why?

Within 5 minutes

What should I do?

Within 10 minutes

What evidence supports these conclusions?

If any answer appears before an earlier question,
the reading flow is incorrect.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 12. SUCCESS CRITERIA

Commercial UI V3 succeeds when:

• Users understand the overall chart within seconds.

• Reading the report feels natural.

• The interface disappears behind the content.

• The customer focuses on meaning,
  not on data structures.

• Every conclusion is understandable.

• Every important conclusion is explainable.

• Every explanation is traceable.

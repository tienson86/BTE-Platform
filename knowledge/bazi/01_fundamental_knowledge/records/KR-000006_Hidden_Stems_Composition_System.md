# 15. Canonical Definition

## 15.1 Official Definition

The **Hidden Stem Composition System** is the canonical relationship system that defines how the canonical Heavenly Stems are structurally contained within the canonical Earthly Branches.

A Hidden Stem is **not an independent entity**.

Instead, it represents the structural participation of an existing Heavenly Stem within the composition of a specific Earthly Branch.

---

## 15.2 Canonical Statement

Exactly twelve Earthly Branches possess standardized Hidden Stem compositions.

Each composition consists exclusively of canonical Heavenly Stems defined in KR-000004.

No Hidden Stem may exist independently from an Earthly Branch.

---

## 15.3 Canonical Characteristics

Every Hidden Stem composition SHALL satisfy all of the following:

- belongs to exactly one Earthly Branch;
- references one or more canonical Heavenly Stems;
- possesses an immutable composition order;
- possesses defined structural roles;
- is machine-readable;
- is compiler traceable;

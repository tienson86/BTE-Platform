# 14. Part 1 Summary

Part 1 establishes the identity, scope, metadata and governance foundation of **KR-000011 — Ten Gods System**.

Unlike previous Knowledge Records that define entities, classifications or resolution systems, KR-000011 defines the canonical derivation framework for the Ten Gods. The system derives semantic relationships between the Day Master and other Heavenly Stems using the foundational principles of Yin Yang and Wu Xing.

The Ten Gods System SHALL function as the exclusive canonical authority for Ten God derivation throughout the BTE Knowledge Canon while maintaining full interoperability with the Mapping Infrastructure defined by KR-000010.

# COMMERCIAL_METRICS

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Program | RC3 Internal Beta |
| Scale | 0–10 |
| Source | Participant form — not lab reviewer rescore |

These six metrics are the official RC3 commercial set.

---

## Definitions

| Metric | Participant question | 10 means |
|--------|----------------------|----------|
| **Trust** | Do I believe this consultant — nothing hidden or invented? | I would let this guide a real decision |
| **Clarity** | Did I understand without a glossary? | I could retell it to a friend |
| **Value** | Was this worth my time / money as shown? | I got a decision or a self-view change |
| **Actionability** | Do I know what to do (or what the parent should do) this week? | Concrete, age-safe next step |
| **Recommendation** | Would I recommend this to someone like me? | Yes, without caveats that kill the product |
| **Purchase Intent** | Would I pay for this as delivered? | Yes at a stated package |

Recommendation and Purchase Intent are **not** the same. A person may recommend but not buy (or the reverse).

---

## How to score the cohort

| Aggregation | Rule |
|-------------|------|
| Per case | Mean of that participant’s six scores (or report all six) |
| Cohort | Mean across completed forms |
| Floor | See [SUCCESS_METRICS.md](SUCCESS_METRICS.md) |
| Do not | Average CASE_0001 8.0 lab score with a 4 to “pass” the cohort |

Lab commercial scores (EPIC-A/B) are **prior evidence**. RC3 metrics are **live humans**.

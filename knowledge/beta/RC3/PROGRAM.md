# PROGRAM

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Name | RC3 Internal Beta |
| Type | Customer discovery |
| Cohort | 10 real charts · 10 personas · 1:1 |
| Audience | Invited internal / known customers — not public launch |

---

## What RC3 is

A structured listening program.

Each participant receives the **customer deliverable appropriate to their context** (adult pass-through or parent/child adaptive delivery) and completes the standard [FEEDBACK_FORM.md](FEEDBACK_FORM.md).

---

## What RC3 is not

| Not | Why |
|-----|-----|
| Public commercial launch | Commercial V1 still requires Product sign-off after discovery |
| Engineering sprint | No code/Knowledge work inside this program |
| Golden Dataset rewrite | Scores and artifacts stay in the laboratory |
| Ten new synthetic charts | Charts must be **real**; personas are recruitment filters |

---

## Cohort design

```text
Persona  →  real chart bind  →  frozen pipeline run
                ↓
        Customer deliverable
                ↓
        Feedback form
                ↓
        Metrics + triage
                ↓
        RC3 accept / hold
```

One persona per case. One form per case. No mixing feedback across charts.

---

## Roles

| Role | Duty |
|------|------|
| Product | Bind remaining charts; invite participants; RC3 accept/hold |
| Validation Owner | Run protocol; store forms; no rewrite of output |
| Customer Reviewer | Independent read of forms (not the composer of the case) |
| Dataset Steward | Protect CASE_0001 freeze |

---

## Deliverable per participant

| Life stage | What they see |
|------------|----------------|
| Adult SELF | Identity + Career + Executive (Package B/C as assigned) |
| Child + PARENT | Development identity + Parent guidance + soft Executive — **no Career Decision** |

Do not show diagnostics, claim keys, Draft labels, or engineering appendices.

---

## Duration

| Phase | Intent |
|-------|--------|
| Recruit / bind | Fill CASE_0004–0010 |
| Session | One sitting per participant (or parent) |
| Capture | Form same day |
| Triage | Weekly until n=10 complete |
| Close | Success metrics scored · Product decision |

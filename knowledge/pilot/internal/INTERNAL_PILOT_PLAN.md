# Internal Pilot Plan

Version: 1.0.0  
Sprint: Beta-5  
Phase: 1 — Internal Pilot  
Cohort: 5–10 users

## Who

Product, engineering, support, ops, and one consultant-advisor if available. Not paying customers.

## Goals

Walk every primary journey. File P0/P1 early. Calibrate severity guide. Prove feedback templates work.

## Activities

1. Kickoff + severity training (`SEVERITY_GUIDE.md`)  
2. Assigned journeys (`USER_JOURNEYS.md` + `TEST_SCENARIOS.md`)  
3. Daily defect dump into templates  
4. Mid-phase triage  
5. Exit review (`EXIT_CRITERIA.md`)

## Duration

5–10 working days.

## Success

Checklist complete (`CHECKLIST.md`), no unknown P0, templates used for 100% of findings.

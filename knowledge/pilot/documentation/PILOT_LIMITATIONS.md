# Pilot Limitations

Version: 1.0.0

- Small N; metrics are directional.  
- Self-selection of experts may skew positivity or harshness.  
- No automated analytics — undercount possible.  
- PDF/Share journeys may be N/A on frozen UI.  
- NPS unreliable below N=20.  
- School diversity will not be fully sampled.  
- Pilot cannot validate every locale, device, or accessibility edge.  
- Legal consent template needs counsel before external use.  
- Success does not authorize engine edits.

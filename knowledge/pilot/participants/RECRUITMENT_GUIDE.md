# Recruitment Guide

Version: 1.0.0  
Sprint: Beta-5

## Sources

Warm professional network, educator lists, waitlist. No cold spam.

## Expert (Phase 2)

Practicing consultant, can give 2–3 hours across two weeks, agrees confidentiality, not seeking a custom engine fork.

## Customer (Phase 3)

Can complete first analysis with the Quick Start (commercial pack, read-only). Mix of new and experienced readers.

## Pitch (honest)

“Invited pilot of BTE v1.0. Frozen platform. We want structured feedback, not a free forever custom build.”

## Exclude

Users demanding guaranteed predictions; anyone unwilling to consent; minors.

## Cap

Stop at phase max (10 / 20 / 50). Waitlist overflow for later.

# Pilot Objectives

Version: 1.0.0  
Sprint: Beta-5

1. Confirm first-time users can complete create → wait → read report without staff sitting beside them (Phase 3 target).
2. Confirm professional consultants judge the report as usable in a real consultation (Phase 2).
3. Confirm internal testers exhaust primary journeys and P0/P1 defects are known (Phase 1).
4. Measure (by observation or survey) completion, reading, Knowledge use, save/PDF, return, support, satisfaction.
5. Establish a feedback lifecycle that Product Owner can turn into roadmap input without breaking freeze.
6. Prove support + legal + commercial packs are usable before wider launch (Phase 4 gate).
7. Keep Architecture Freeze intact through all four phases.

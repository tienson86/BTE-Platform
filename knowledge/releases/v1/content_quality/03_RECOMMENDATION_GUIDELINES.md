# 03 — Recommendation Guidelines

Version: 1.0  
Status: **Release B — Content Quality**  
Date: 2026-08-08  
Scope: Quality standards only — no runtime change

---

## 1. Purpose

Recommendations tell the person **what to prioritize doing**.

They must feel like counsel from an experienced BaZi consultant: specific, actionable, evidence-backed, and professionally written.

This document extends Pack 05 `09_RECOMMENDATION_COMPONENT.md` and Sprint C wording rules.

---

## 2. Quality bar

A commercial recommendation must be:

| Attribute | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| **Specific** | Anchored to this chart’s validated guidance — not a universal slogan |
| **Actionable** | The reader can do something next |
| **Evidence-supported** | Traceable to Interpretation / Evidence (useful god, counsel, action units) |
| **Professionally written** | Calm, respectful, directive-supportive tone |

---

## 3. Required shape

Prefer a three-part commercial shape (aligned with Portal labels already in use):

| Part | Label (VI) | Role |
|------|------------|------|
| Action | Hành động | What to do |
| Reason | Lý do | Why this follows from the analysis |
| Benefit | Lợi ích kỳ vọng | What improves if followed — only if supported |

Priority metadata (Cao / Trung bình / Thấp / Ưu tiên cao) may accompany items. Narrative does not invent a new ranking algorithm beyond source priority.

---

## 4. Must

✓ State a clear action in human language  
✓ Keep action aligned with Observation / Impact meaning  
✓ Keep severity and urgency within source claims  
✓ Prefer one primary recommendation when multiple compete  
✓ Use directive-supportive voice (“Ưu tiên…”, “Nên tập trung…”)  
✓ Preserve analytical meaning — compress, do not invent  

---

## 5. Must not

✗ Repeat Observation as if it were advice  
✗ Repeat scores or metric names as the recommendation  
✗ State unsupported conclusions (“Đổi nghề sang Thủy ngay sẽ giàu”)  
✗ Invent timelines, percentages, or guaranteed outcomes  
✗ Fear-based forcing (“Nếu không làm ngay sẽ thảm họa”)  
✗ Technical procedure text (“Apply rule set…”, `matched_rules`)  
✗ English UI leftovers on Vietnamese commercial surfaces  
✗ Mock / placeholder / “chờ engine” wording  

---

## 6. Good vs bad

### Action

| BAD | GOOD (direction) |
|-----|------------------|
| Bạn nên phát huy điểm mạnh của mình. | Ưu tiên phát huy đúng hướng Thực Thần đã được chỉ ra trong luận giải. |
| strength_score khuyến nghị hành động. | Chọn công việc cần chủ động tạo giá trị và giữ nhịp thực hiện ổn định. |
| Observation: thân vượng. | (Not a recommendation — keep in Observation) |

### Reason

| BAD | GOOD (direction) |
|-----|------------------|
| Vì điểm số cao. | Vì cục diện đang hỗ trợ hướng chủ động tạo kết quả. |
| Rule X matched. | Vì các yếu tố đã kiểm chứng trong luận giải cùng hướng tới ưu tiên này. |

### Benefit

| BAD | GOOD (direction) |
|-----|------------------|
| Bạn sẽ thành công 100%. | Giúp duy trì đúng hướng và giảm lệch nhịp không cần thiết. |
| (empty marketing promise) | Chỉ state benefit if Interpretation/Evidence supports the expectation |

If benefit is unsupported → omit benefit; do not invent.

---

## 7. Specificity checklist

A recommendation is **specific enough** when:

1. A reader can tell it belongs to **this** analysis, not any random chart.  
2. The action names a direction, behavior, or focus already present in sources.  
3. The reason cites meaning — not a raw score.  
4. It does not merely restate “you have X pattern.”  

A recommendation is **too generic** when:

- It could be pasted onto any result unchanged.  
- It only says “phát huy điểm mạnh / hạn chế điểm yếu.”  
- It echoes the Observation sentence with an imperative verb stuck on front.

---

## 8. Relationship to Warning and Executive Summary

| Surface | Role |
|---------|------|
| Recommendation | What to do |
| Warning | What to watch / avoid |
| Executive Summary priority / next action | Compressed view of the primary recommendation |

Do not merge Warning into Recommendation.  
Do not use Recommendation to introduce new risks.

---

## 9. Insufficient evidence

If no action-class / useful-god / counsel evidence exists:

- Emit approved insufficient narrative.  
- Do not invent “safe generic tips” to look complete.  

Honest emptiness > polished fabrication.

---

## 10. Review questions

1. Is the action something a person can attempt?  
2. Is the reason grounded in this analysis?  
3. Does any sentence repeat Observation or dump a score?  
4. Would a senior consultant say this without embarrassment?  
5. Is priority coherent with Executive Summary next action?

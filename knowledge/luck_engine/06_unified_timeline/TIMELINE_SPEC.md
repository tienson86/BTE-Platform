# 1. Purpose

The Unified Timeline Module is responsible for combining every Fortune Context
generated by the BTE Platform into one immutable Timeline Context.

The module performs no fortune calculation.

Its responsibility is aggregation, normalization, synchronization,
validation and construction of TimelineContext.

The Unified Timeline SHALL become the single source of truth for all
downstream engines.

---

# 2. Design Goals

The module SHALL

- Produce deterministic outputs
- Produce immutable TimelineContext
- Preserve every Fortune Layer
- Preserve metadata
- Be independent from Rule Database
- Be independent from Calendar calculations
- Be independent from AI
- Support future Timeline extensions

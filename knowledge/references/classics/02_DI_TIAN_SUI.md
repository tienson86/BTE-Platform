# 1. Executive Summary

*Di Tian Sui* (滴天髓) is one of the most influential classical works in the Zi Ping (BaZi) tradition. Rather than serving as an encyclopedic manual, it presents the philosophical foundations and governing principles of destiny analysis through concise poetic verses accompanied by later commentaries.

Within the BTE Knowledge Platform, *Di Tian Sui* is designated as the primary reference for high-level principles governing Yin-Yang balance, Five Elements dynamics, Day Master evaluation, Qi flow, and methodological reasoning. Its role is complementary to *Yuan Hai Zi Ping*: while the latter provides extensive practical classifications and case-oriented knowledge, *Di Tian Sui* emphasizes universal principles that guide interpretation.

- Different editions may vary in wording and commentary; BTE should record the specific edition used when extracting knowledge.

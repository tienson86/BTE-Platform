# 1. Executive Summary

*San Ming Tong Hui* (三命通會) is one of the most comprehensive classical encyclopedic works in the field of Chinese destiny studies. Compiled during the Ming Dynasty by Wan Minying (萬民英), it systematically integrates theories from Zi Ping, Na Yin, Shen Sha, Five Elements, Heavenly Stems, Earthly Branches, Luck Cycles, and numerous earlier classics.

Within the BTE Knowledge Platform, *San Ming Tong Hui* serves as the principal encyclopedic reference, providing extensive coverage of practical applications, classifications, historical interpretations, and comparative viewpoints across different schools. It complements the philosophical depth of *Di Tian Sui* and the foundational structure of *Yuan Hai Zi Ping*.

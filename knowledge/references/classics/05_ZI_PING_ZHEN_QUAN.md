# 1. Executive Summary

*Zi Ping Zhen Quan* (子平真诠) is one of the most influential methodological texts within the Zi Ping tradition. It emphasizes systematic reasoning in chart interpretation, especially Pattern (格局), Ten Gods (十神), Useful God (用神), and the logical relationships among chart components.

Within the BTE Knowledge Platform, this work is designated as the primary methodological reference for Pattern Engine, Rule Engine, and Interpretation Engine.

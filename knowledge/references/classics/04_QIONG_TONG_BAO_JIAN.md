# 1. Executive Summary

*Qiong Tong Bao Jian* (穷通宝鉴) is regarded as the authoritative classical work on seasonal Qi regulation (调候), climate balance, and the selection of Useful God (用神) according to seasonal conditions. Unlike encyclopedic works, it focuses on the interaction between Heavenly Stems, seasonal climate, and the dynamic balance of Cold, Heat, Dryness, and Dampness.

Within the BTE Knowledge Platform, this work is designated as the primary canonical reference for Temperature Analysis, Seasonal Analysis, Climate Regulation, and Useful God determination.

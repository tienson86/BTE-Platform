## 1. Purpose

Design Golden Cases that prove Domain 01 consulting quality once P0/P1 units exist.

Cases are **profiles** (structure × work intent). Product may bind anonymized real charts later.

---

## 2. Design principles

1. Cover **structure**: strong, weak, mixed.  
2. Cover **roles**: employee, manager, founder, partner-seeker.  
3. Cover **decisions**: select, change, promote, found, partner.  
4. Honor Wave 1.1: mitigate-first when WK fires.  
5. Domain units must **improve** Rec/Exec vs Wave 1.1-only baseline.  
6. No PII in docs.

## 1. Purpose

Define the official scope of the **Career & Business** commercial knowledge domain: what BTE will consult on, what it will not invent, and how subdomains compose.

---

## 2. Domain thesis

Career & Business consulting turns chart structure (especially **Dụng thần**, pattern, strength/limits, officer/wealth/output signals, and timing) into **work and enterprise decisions** the customer can act on — without calculator dumps or guaranteed outcomes.

```
Who am I at work?  (role fit)
        ↓
What should I do next?  (change / stay / advance / found)
        ↓
How do I lead / partner / grow?  (leadership, team, development)
        ↓
What must I not rush?  (risk → mitigation → opportunity)
```

Wave 1.1 supplies the structural spine. Domain 01 supplies **work-specific** meaning and actions.

## 3. In-scope subdomains

| Subdomain | Code | Customer focus |
|-----------|------|----------------|

| **Management** | CA-MGT | Managing people / delivery vs IC work |

These nine are the **official Domain 01 subdomain set** for V1 modeling.

## 4. Subdomain definitions

### 4.1 Career Selection (CA-SEL)

| Field | Definition |
|-------|------------|
| Purpose | Name fit directions from structure (not job-title oracle) |
| Typical ask | “Công việc nào hợp tôi?” |
| Outputs | Role themes; environments to prefer/avoid; next exploration step |
| Ethics | No absolute “must be X profession” claims |

### 4.2 Career Change (CA-CHG)

| Field | Definition |
|-------|------------|
| Purpose | Decide stay / switch / stage with buffer |
| Typical ask | “Có nên đổi việc?” |
| Outputs | Decision posture; transition action; reckless-switch risk + mitigation |

### 4.3 Promotion (CA-PRO)

| Field | Definition |
|-------|------------|
| Purpose | Advise advancement timing and readiness |
| Typical ask | “Có nên nhận / xin thăng tiến?” |
| Outputs | Advance / prepare / defer; opportunity window language when signals support |

### 4.4 Leadership (CA-LED)

| Field | Definition |
|-------|------------|
| Purpose | Clarify how the person leads and when authority costs energy |
| Typical ask | “Tôi có hợp làm lãnh đạo không?” |
| Outputs | Leadership style notes; strain risks; mitigation habits |

### 4.5 Management (CA-MGT)

| Field | Definition |
|-------|------------|
| Purpose | Distinguish people-management vs individual-contributor fit |
| Typical ask | “Có nên làm quản lý?” |
| Outputs | Manager vs IC posture; conditions that make management sustainable |

### 4.6 Entrepreneurship (BU-ENP)

| Field | Definition |
|-------|------------|
| Purpose | Advise founding / self-directed enterprise vs employment |
| Typical ask | “Có nên khởi nghiệp / làm chủ?” |
| Outputs | Founder posture; pilot / defer / launch framing; runway ethics |

### 4.7 Business Partnership (BU-PTR)

| Field | Definition |
|-------|------------|
| Purpose | Solo vs partner; role and trust clarity |
| Typical ask | “Có nên hợp tác kinh doanh?” |
| Outputs | Partnership posture; clash/trust risks; mitigation (role clarity) |

### 4.8 Team Management (BU-TEM)

| Field | Definition |
|-------|------------|
| Purpose | How the person works with / through teams |
| Typical ask | “Tôi quản đội nhóm thế nào cho bền?” |
| Outputs | Delegation / conflict / pace guidance tied to structure |

### 4.9 Professional Development (CA-DEV)

| Field | Definition |
|-------|------------|
| Purpose | Skill and capability bets that serve useful-god direction |
| Typical ask | “Tôi nên phát triển nghề nghiệp ra sao?” |
| Outputs | Learning priorities; 2–4 week capability next step |

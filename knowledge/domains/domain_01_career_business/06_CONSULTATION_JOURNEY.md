## 1. Purpose

Define the end-to-end **consultation journey** for a Career & Business customer — from entry question to memorable conclusion.

---

## 2. Journey map

```
Entry question (Q-*)
        ↓
Scenario / decision routing (CS-* / D-*)
        ↓
Analysis evidence
        ↓
Interpretation (unchanged engine)
        ↓
Wave 1.1 structural bundle
        ↓
Domain 01 Career & Business enrichment
        ↓
Narrative composition (Exec → … → Conclusion)
        ↓
Customer understanding → decision posture → next step
```

---

## 3. Stage detail

### Stage 1 — Entry

| Item | Behavior |
|------|----------|
| Customer | Asks a Domain 01 question (`02`) |
| Product | Routes CS-CA / CS-CC / CS-PR / CS-BU / CS-ST / CS-ENP / CS-LE or default+light |
| Fail-soft | If unclear, ask clarifiers (`02` §4) |

### Stage 2 — Structural truth (Wave 1.1)

| Item | Behavior |
|------|----------|
| Always | Identity, strength/weakness honesty, useful-god compass, core action |
| Gate | If WK active → mitigate-first before domain Advance |

### Stage 3 — Domain enrichment

| Item | Behavior |
|------|----------|
| P0 | Work-direction + role-fit next step (+ light leadership/independence) |
| P1 | Decision-specific change/promote/found/partner packs |
| Missing unit | Honest structural advice only — no invented domain claims |

### Stage 4 — Narrative delivery

| Order | Component | Domain job |
|------:|-----------|------------|
| 1 | Executive Summary | Who you are at work + posture |
| 2 | Observation / Reasoning | Why structure supports that posture |
| 3 | Impact | What it means for role/load/enterprise |
| 4 | Recommendation | One primary work action |
| 5 | Warning | Only if risk evidence |
| 6 | Conclusion | One sentence the customer remembers |

### Stage 5 — Customer exit

Customer should leave able to answer:

1. What work posture fits me now?  
2. What do I do in the next 2–4 weeks?  
3. What must I not rush?

## 4. Journey variants

| Variant | Path emphasis |
|---------|---------------|
| Employee growth | CA-SEL → CA-DEV → (optional) CA-PRO |
| Job change | CA-SEL check → CA-CHG (Go/stage/defer) |
| Manager track | CA-MGT → CA-LED → load MT |
| Founder | BU-ENP → pilot/defer → (optional) BU-PTR |
| Partnership | BU-PTR RK/MT before equity/commit |

---

## 5. Trust rules along the journey

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| No guarantees | Income, promotion, startup success |
| No medicalization | Stress → pacing/load, not diagnosis |
| No Wave 1.1 contradiction | Domain may specialize, not reverse structural truth |
| Traceability | Decision → evidence → KU → narrative preserved |

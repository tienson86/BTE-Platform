# 1. Identity

## 1.1 Definition

**Day Master** is the semantic role assumed by the Heavenly Stem that occupies the stem position of the Day Pillar.

The occupying object is a Heavenly Stem entity.

The role is Day Master.

The role does not create a new entity.

The role does not change the identity of the Heavenly Stem.

Official names:

- Day Master
- Nhật Chủ
- 日主
- Nhật Can
- 日干

Accepted aliases:

- Day Stem
- Rì Zhǔ
- Rì Gān

Aliases SHALL NOT replace the Canonical Name in official specifications.

---

## 1.2 What Day Master Is

Day Master is:

- the comparison axis of the chart
- the reference Heavenly Stem for relational derivation
- the object whose standing is evaluated as Strength
- the structural center of interpretation

Day Master is the stem of the Day Pillar only.

The Day Branch is not the Day Master.

The Day Pillar as a whole is not the Day Master.

---

## 1.3 What Day Master Is Not

Day Master is not:

- a Ten God
- Companion / Friend / 比肩
- a new Heavenly Stem
- a personality type
- a fortune result
- a Useful God
- a Pattern
- a Shen Sha
- the entire natal chart
- the Day Branch (配偶宫 / spouse palace is a branch position, not the Day Master role)

When the Day Stem is compared with itself, the derived Ten God is Companion.

That derivation does not convert Day Master into Companion.

Day Master remains the axis.

Companion is a relation produced from the axis.

---

## 1.4 Purpose

Day Master exists to give the Four Pillars a single reference point.

Without a Day Master:

- Ten Gods cannot be derived
- Strength has no object
- Pattern has no subject relative to month command
- Useful God has no condition to serve
- Luck has no natal core to interact with
- Narrative has no stable “who” inside the chart

The purpose of Day Master is orientation, not prediction.

---

They do not create separate Day Masters.

These are positional frames.

They are not life-event scripts.

They are not biographies.

The Month Branch is the primary seasonal command.

The Day Stem is the reference core.

The two must not be swapped.

## 1.7 Why Day Master is the center of interpretation

Interpretation requires a subject.

Day Master is that subject.

Reasons:

1. **Relational origin.** Ten Gods are derived from Day Master plus a target stem. There is no Ten God without a Day Master.
2. **Standing origin.** Strength describes whether the Day Master can hold independent qi. Strength is not a free-floating chart score.
3. **Pattern origin.** Pattern is established from month command relative to Day Master, not from month command alone.
4. **Need origin.** Useful God is the chart’s operational need given the Day Master’s condition. It is not a lucky star detached from the core.
5. **Time origin.** Luck modifies the operating environment of the same Day Master. Luck does not replace the natal axis.
6. **Narrative origin.** Reports must speak from one center. Day Master is that center.

The center is structural.

It is not mystical.

It is not a claim that the person “is” the stem name in a folk sense.

# 2. Mechanism

This section explains the operating mechanics of Day Master as a concept.

It does not prescribe formulas.

It does not assign weights.

It does not define engine thresholds.

---

## 2.1 Operating Model

Day Master qi is assessed through five interacting conditions:

```text
Season
Root
Support
Drain
Control
```

These five produce a standing:

```text
Balance / Strength classification
```

Standing then becomes input to later systems.

The later systems do not redefine Day Master.

---

## 2.2 Season

Season is the environmental command surrounding the Day Master.

Primary seasonal carrier: Month Branch.

Season answers:

Does the month qi nourish, peer, drain, or restrain the Day Master’s element?

Classical attainment name: **Đắc Lệnh / 得令 / De Ling**.

Đắc Lệnh means the Day Master obtains seasonal command advantage.

It does not mean the Day Master is automatically strong.

It does not mean the chart is auspicious.

Season is context.

Season is not Strength.

Season is not Useful God.

Season is not Pattern, though Pattern uses month command as its establishment mark.

Seasonal conflict exists when month qi opposes the Day Master’s elemental need.

Seasonal support exists when month qi generates or peers with the Day Master.

Details of solar terms, climate types, and seasonal qi registries belong to Temperature Canon and Seasonal Context records.

This Canon only states that Day Master standing cannot be read without season.

---

## 2.3 Root

Root is the Day Master’s connection into Earthly Branches through hidden stems.

Classical names:

- **Đắc Địa / 得地 / De Di** — earthly/rooting advantage
- **Thông Căn / 通根 / Tong Gen** — root connection

Root answers:

Does the Day Master have a place to stand in the branches?

Visible Day Stem without root is exposed qi.

Root without visible peer support is latent capacity.

Hidden-stem root and visible-stem appearance are different layers.

They SHALL NOT be summed as if they were the same object.

Root depth, branch quality, and void/clash modification belong to Strength Canon.

This Canon only states that Day Master stability depends on root.

---

## 2.4 Support

Support is qi that helps the Day Master hold standing.

Two primary support channels:

1. **Peer support** — same-element stems and hidden stems (Companion class)
2. **Resource support** — generating-element stems and hidden stems (Resource class)

Classical attainment name: **Đắc Thế / 得势 / De Shi**.

Đắc Thế means the Day Master obtains compositional momentum.

Support is useful when the Day Master needs to be raised.

Support is excess when the Day Master already holds surplus standing.

Support is not automatically beneficial.

The same generating stem can be:

- necessary foundation, or
- additional weight on an already overloaded core

That distinction is a Strength / Useful God judgment.

It is not a property of the stem itself.

---

## 2.5 Drain

Drain is qi leaving the Day Master through the generating-outward relation.

Primary drain channel: Output class (Eating God / Hurting Officer).

Drain answers:

Is the Day Master’s qi being expressed outward rather than retained?

Drain is useful when the Day Master has surplus standing and needs an outlet.

Drain is damaging when the Day Master is already thin.

Drain is not “talent” as a fortune slogan.

Drain is an energy-direction mechanism.

Wealth is downstream of output in Five-Element sequence.

That sequence is Ten Gods / Useful God material.

This Canon only states that Day Master can be drained.

---

## 2.6 Control

Control is qi that restrains the Day Master.

Primary control channel: Officer / Killing class.

Control answers:

Is the Day Master being checked, structured, or suppressed?

Control is useful when the Day Master’s standing is excessive and requires discipline.

Control is damaging when the Day Master cannot bear restraint.

Control is not “career” as a fortune slogan.

Control is a restraint mechanism.

---

## 2.7 Balance

Balance is the operational band of Day Master standing.

It is not a Useful God.

It is not a Pattern.

It is not a moral quality.

Three conceptual standings:

| Standing | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| Strong | Day Master holds surplus or independent qi |
| Weak | Day Master lacks sufficient qi to stand independently |
| Balanced | Day Master stands inside the operational band |

Balanced does not mean “good luck.”

Strong does not mean “fortunate.”

Weak does not mean “unfortunate.”

Balance is a condition of the core.

Later systems decide what the chart needs **given** that condition.

Do not use balance language to select Useful God.

Do not use Useful God language to redefine balance.

---

## 2.8 Strength

Strength is the classified standing of the Day Master.

Strength evaluates the Day Master.

Strength does not evaluate “the chart’s luck.”

Strength consumes:

- Season

Strength does not consume:

Stem nature belongs to later Stem Canons.

---

## 3.1 Identity

Day Master symbolizes the chart’s reference identity.

It is the “who” inside the structure.

It is not a passport description.

It is not a psychological diagnosis.

It is the operational self of the chart: the qi from which relations are measured.

---

## 3.2 Nature

Nature of a Day Master is inherited from the occupying Heavenly Stem:

- Yin or Yang
- one Wu Xing class

This Canon records that inheritance.

It does not describe the ten stem natures.

Reading Day Master nature without the occupying stem is empty.

Reading the occupying stem without the Day Master role is entity description, not chart orientation.

Both are required.

Neither substitutes for the other.

---

## 3.3 Core Energy

Day Master is the core energy the rest of the chart organizes around.

Core energy is:

- retained when support and root hold
- expressed when drain is open
- shaped when control is present
- inflated when season and peer support stack
- depleted when season, drain, and control stack against a thin root

Core energy is a structural description.

It is not a spiritual substance.

---

## 3.4 Behavioral Expression

Behavioral expression is how the core tends to operate under its standing.

General, non-stem-specific:

| Condition | Expression tendency |
|-----------|---------------------|
| Rooted and in season | stable initiative |
| Rooted but out of season | capacity exists; environment does not feed it |
| In season but unrooted | apparent force without stable base |
| Supported when thin | recovery / dependence on generating or peer sources |
| Drained when surplus | output, articulation, expenditure |
| Drained when thin | depletion, overextension |
| Controlled when surplus | discipline, usable structure |
| Controlled when thin | suppression, constraint |

These are operational tendencies.

They are not character verdicts.

They are not event predictions.

---

## 3.5 Growth

Growth is the Day Master’s capacity to increase usable standing.

Growth sources:

- seasonal alignment
- root establishment
- resource generation
- peer reinforcement when the core is thin
- luck that supplies the missing channel

Growth is not automatic with age.

Luck may feed or starve the same core.

Growth language SHALL remain conditional on evidence.

---

## 3.6 Adaptation

Adaptation is how the Day Master operates when the environment does not match its nature.

Two legitimate adaptive modes:

1. **Restore independent standing** — support, root, and season are used to rebuild the core.
2. **Follow dominant qi** — the core cannot hold independence, so the chart is read as following the prevailing force.

The second mode is Follow Pattern.

It is not a casual metaphor for “weak Day Master.”

It requires Pattern authority.

Adaptation is a structural response.

It is not a moral quality such as “flexibility.”

---

## 3.7 Decision Style

Decision style, at Canon level, is the core’s way of using qi:

- retain
- generate outward
- seize / manage (wealth relation)
- submit to / be structured by (officer relation)
- draw from (resource relation)
- peer with (companion relation)

The style is relational.

It is not a personality inventory.

Ten Gods Canon owns the five relation classes.

This Canon only states that Day Master is the agent of those decisions.

Strength classifies whether the Day Master stands strong, weak, or balanced.

Strength Canon SHALL reference this Canon for the object definition.

Strength Canon SHALL NOT redefine Day Master as a score, a vibe, or a life outcome.

## 4.2 Pattern

Pattern is the chart’s structural frame.

Pattern is typically established from month command relative to Day Master.

Day Master is the **subject** of Pattern.

Month command is the **establishment mark**.

Month command is not Useful God.

Pattern is not a profession.

Follow Pattern is a special structural state in which Day Master cannot hold independent standing, so the frame follows the dominant qi.

Follow Pattern does not delete Day Master.

It changes how the chart is read around Day Master.

Pattern Canon SHALL reference this Canon.

It SHALL NOT invent a second axis.

---

## 4.3 Useful God

Useful God is the chart’s operational need.

The need is relative to Day Master condition, Pattern, and Temperature.

Typical conceptual orientations:

| Day Master condition | Conceptual need |
|----------------------|-----------------|
| Thin / unrooted | support, generation, root restoration |
| Surplus / overloaded | drain, control, reduction of peer-resource stacking |
| Climate-distorted | warming, cooling, moistening, drying as Temperature Canon defines |
| Follow Pattern | follow and protect the dominant qi; do not force restoration of independent Day Master standing |

Useful God is not the Day Master.

Useful God is not “whatever is missing in the Five Elements.”

Useful God Canon SHALL consume Day Master standing as evidence.

It SHALL NOT redefine Day Master.

---

Ten Gods are semantic relations derived from:

```text
Day Master
+
Target Heavenly Stem
→
exactly one Ten God
```

Day Master is the **axis**.

Target stem is the **compared entity**.

Ten God is the **derived relation**.

Day Master is not one of the ten relations.

A same-stem comparison yields Companion.

That is a relation, not a renaming of the axis.

Hidden stems also derive Ten Gods relative to the same Day Master.

Visible and hidden are layers of the same axis.

They are not two Day Masters.

Ten Gods Canon SHALL reference this Canon as the exclusive axis definition.

Luck does not replace the natal Day Master.

A luck stem is not a new natal identity.

# 5. Manifestation

Manifestation is how Day Master standing and relations tend to appear in life domains.

These are general operational mappings.

They are not predictions.

They are not stem-specific portraits.

They require evidence from Strength, Ten Gods, Pattern, Useful God, and Luck before any domain statement is issued.

If evidence is missing, manifestation SHALL be withheld.

---

## 5.1 Career

Career manifestation is how the core applies force in work.

- Surplus standing with no drain or control: force without usable channel.
- Thin standing under heavy control: work as constraint rather than agency.
- Supportive resource/peer when thin: reliance on backing, credentials, or allies.
- Open drain when surplus: work through production, expression, or skill output.

Career is not identical to Officer.

Officer is one relation that may structure career.

Output, wealth, and resource may also structure career.

The Day Master is the agent.

The relation is the channel.

---

## 5.2 Finance

Finance manifestation is how the core relates to resources it can manage.

Wealth, in Ten God terms, is what the Day Master controls.

That control is usable only if the Day Master has standing to exercise it, or if Follow Pattern makes wealth the dominant qi being followed.

Thin Day Master facing heavy wealth qi is load, not automatically “money.”

Surplus Day Master with no wealth relation may retain force without a management object.

Finance statements require Ten Gods and Useful God evidence.

They SHALL NOT be issued from Day Master identity alone.

---

## 5.3 Relationship

Relationship manifestation is how the core stands in pairing and close alliance.

The Day Branch is a structural palace.

It is not the Day Master.

Companion may indicate peer alliance or rivalry.

Officer/wealth relations may indicate structured pairing channels.

These mappings belong to Ten Gods and palace theory.

This Canon only states that relationship reading still uses Day Master as the subject, not the Day Branch as a second self.

---

## 5.4 Health

Health manifestation is how core qi holds, depletes, or overheats.

- Missing root: unstable base.
- Excess drain: expenditure beyond replenishment.
- Excess control: suppression.
- Excess support in already surplus standing: congestion.
- Climate conflict: seasonal/temperature distress around the same core.

Health language at Canon level is systemic, not medical diagnosis.

No organ-by-stem catalog belongs here.

That catalog, if ever authorized, belongs to Stem Canons or a medical-correspondence record.

---

## 5.5 Learning

Learning manifestation is how the core absorbs and emits knowledge.

Resource feeds the Day Master.

Output emits from the Day Master.

Thin core with strong resource: intake without equivalent emission.

Surplus core with strong output: emission that may outrun foundation.

Learning is not identical to Resource.

Resource is one feeding channel.

---

## 5.6 Leadership

Leadership manifestation is how the core holds center under other qi.

Leadership requires usable standing **or** a Follow Pattern in which the person operates by aligning with a dominant field rather than by holding independent center.

Control of a surplus core can produce disciplined command.

Control of a thin core produces being commanded.

Peer support can produce coalition or contest for the same center.

Leadership is not a Shen Sha.

Leadership is not “strong Day Master = leader.”

---

## 5.7 Business

Business manifestation is the core’s capacity to hold, produce, and exchange.

It combines:

- standing (can the core hold)
- output (can the core produce)
- wealth (can the core manage exchange)
- officer (can the core bear regulation and structure)
- resource (can the core draw backing)

No single relation equals “business success.”

Day Master identity alone does not equal entrepreneurship.

---

## 5.8 Growth

Growth manifestation is change in usable standing over time.

Natal Day Master does not change identity.

Luck and structural activation change operating conditions.

Growth is visible when missing support/root/climate arrives.

Regression is visible when remaining support/root is clashed, voided, drained, or controlled beyond capacity.

Growth is temporal.

It belongs with Luck evidence.

It is not a natal slogan such as “this Day Master always grows.”

---

## 5.9 Manifestation Rule

Every manifestation claim SHALL name:

1. Day Master identity (stem role only)
2. Standing evidence
3. At least one relational or climate evidence
4. The domain being spoken about

Claims that skip these are not Canon-compliant.

# 6. Edge Cases

Edge cases are conceptual states.

They are not detection algorithms.

Classification authority remains with the owning domain Canon / engine.

This Canon explains what the state means for Day Master.

---

## 6.1 Extremely Strong

The Day Master holds surplus qi from stacked season, root, and support.

Typical conceptual picture:

- in season
- rooted
- peer and/or resource abundant
- drain and control insufficient

Consequences:

- additional support increases congestion
- drain and/or control become the operational need
- Companion is no longer “help”; it is rivalry for the same center
- Useful God orientation shifts toward expenditure or restraint, not feeding

Extremely Strong is not “extremely lucky.”

It is a load-bearing problem of excess.

---

## 6.2 Extremely Weak

The Day Master cannot hold independent qi.

Typical conceptual picture:

- out of season
- little or no root
- drain and/or control present
- support absent or ineffective

Consequences:

- additional drain or control deepens collapse
- support and root restoration become the operational need
- **unless** Pattern authority classifies Follow Pattern

Extremely Weak is not automatically Follow Pattern.

Weakness is a standing.

Follow is a structural override.

They are not synonyms.

---

## 6.3 Follow Pattern

Follow Pattern is a Pattern state.

It applies when Day Master cannot retain independent standing and the chart is organized around dominant qi.

Conceptual types exist (follow wealth, officer/killing, output, resource, or surplus peer field).

Taxonomy belongs to Pattern Canon.

Rules for this Canon:

- Day Master is not deleted
- Day Master remains the natal axis for Ten God derivation
- interpretation follows the dominant field instead of forcing restoration
- Useful God must not blindly “help the weak Day Master” against a verified Follow Pattern
- month command is not erased by Follow language

Knowledge layers explain Follow only after Pattern Engine / Pattern Canon has classified it.

This Canon forbids inferring Follow from weakness alone.

---

## 6.4 Contradictory Signals

Common contradictions:

| Signal A | Signal B | Meaning |
|----------|----------|---------|
| In season | No root | Environmental feed without base |
| Rooted | Out of season | Base exists; month does not feed |
| Visible support | Hidden drain | Appearance vs latent expenditure |
| Visible drain | Hidden root | Expenditure over a buried base |
| Strong stems | Clashed or voided branches | Apparent force with damaged availability |
| Peer support | Officer control | Help and restraint at once |

Contradiction SHALL be reported as contradiction.

It SHALL NOT be collapsed into a single adjective for convenience.

Strength Canon may resolve a standing.

Until that resolution exists, Day Master interpretation stays at mechanism level.

---

## 6.5 Missing Support

Missing support means peer and resource channels are absent or ineffective, and root does not compensate.

Consequences:

- Day Master depends more on season alone
- if season also fails, standing thins rapidly
- output and officer become heavier loads
- restoration, if Pattern is not Follow, requires generating or peer qi from luck or remaining natal residues

Missing support is evidence.

It is not a moral lack.

---

## 6.6 Season Conflicts

Season conflict means month qi does not nourish the Day Master’s elemental class.

Forms:

- month drains the Day Master
- month controls the Day Master
- month is climatically hostile (Temperature layer)

Season conflict does not by itself equal Weak.

Root and support may still hold standing against a hostile month.

Hostile month plus missing root plus drain/control is a stacked weakness picture.

Season conflict also feeds Temperature analysis.

Do not treat season conflict as a Useful God decision by itself.

---

## 6.7 Edge-Case Rule

Edge cases change how Day Master is **used** by later systems.

They do not change what Day Master **is**.

Before any interpretive statement about Day Master standing or manifestation, the following classes of evidence SHOULD be present:

| Evidence class | Source authority | Question answered |
|----------------|------------------|-------------------|

Shen Sha evidence is optional and never sufficient.

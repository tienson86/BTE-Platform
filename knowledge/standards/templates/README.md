# Purpose

This directory contains all official templates used for creating, reviewing, validating, and publishing Knowledge Records within the BTE Knowledge Canon.

These templates ensure that every Knowledge Record follows a consistent academic, structural, and computational standard.

Templates SHALL be used throughout the entire knowledge authoring lifecycle.

---

# Scope

The templates defined in this directory apply to every knowledge domain, including but not limited to:

- BaZi
- Feng Shui
- Qi Men Dun Jia
- Liu Yao
- I Ching
- Traditional Calendar
- Terminology
- Future Domains

# Design Principles

Templates SHALL:

- follow PART_03_KNOWLEDGE_RECORD_STANDARD
- remain domain-independent
- remain machine-readable
- remain compiler-friendly
- support long-term governance

Templates SHALL NOT:

- redefine academic standards
- introduce implementation logic
- duplicate Knowledge Canon rules

---

# Version Management

Templates follow Semantic Versioning.

Major updates require academic approval.

Minor updates require technical review.

Patch updates correct non-semantic issues only.

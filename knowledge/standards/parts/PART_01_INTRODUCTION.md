# 1. Purpose

This document defines the vision, mission, objectives, scope, and guiding principles of the BTE Knowledge Canon.

It serves as the foundational charter governing the design, development, validation, maintenance, and evolution of all knowledge assets within the BTE Platform.

This document is domain-independent and applies equally to BaZi, Feng Shui, Qi Men Dun Jia, Liu Yao, I Ching, and any future knowledge domains incorporated into the platform.

---

# 2. Vision

To build the most comprehensive, academically rigorous, computationally structured, and machine-readable Knowledge Canon for traditional Chinese metaphysics.

The Knowledge Canon shall provide a unified foundation for human experts, software systems, artificial intelligence, and future research.

---

# 3. Mission

The mission of the BTE Knowledge Canon is to transform traditional metaphysical knowledge into a standardized, verifiable, reusable, and computationally compatible knowledge system.

The Knowledge Canon separates:

- Academic Knowledge
- Computational Knowledge
- Executable Rules
- Interpretation Logic

Each layer has clearly defined responsibilities and boundaries.

---

# 4. Objectives

The Knowledge Canon is designed to achieve the following objectives:

1. Preserve traditional academic knowledge.
2. Standardize terminology across all domains.
3. Eliminate ambiguity and duplication.
4. Support computational reasoning.
5. Enable rule-based inference.
6. Improve interpretation consistency.
7. Facilitate future expansion.
8. Maintain long-term governance.

---

# 5. Scope

The Knowledge Canon governs every knowledge asset contained within the BTE Platform.

This includes, but is not limited to:

- BaZi
- Feng Shui
- Qi Men Dun Jia
- Liu Yao
- I Ching
- Traditional Calendar
- Terminology
- References
- Academic Sources
- Relationships
- Knowledge Records
- Knowledge Graph

---

# 6. Out of Scope

The Knowledge Canon does NOT define:

- executable business logic
- scoring algorithms
- inference algorithms
- report rendering
- user interface behavior
- application workflows
- API implementations

These belong to other platform components.

# 13. Design Philosophy

The BTE Knowledge Canon is governed by the following design philosophies.

---

## Philosophy 1 — Knowledge Before Algorithm

Academic knowledge is the foundation of every computational process.

Algorithms SHALL consume standardized knowledge rather than define knowledge.

Knowledge always precedes implementation.

---

## Philosophy 2 — Canonical Before Implementation

The Knowledge Canon is the authoritative source of truth.

All software components SHALL conform to the Knowledge Canon.

Implementation SHALL NOT redefine academic concepts.

---

## Philosophy 3 — Data Over Code

Whenever possible, domain knowledge SHALL be represented as structured data rather than hard-coded logic.

Knowledge evolves faster than software.

Data-driven systems are easier to validate, maintain, audit, and extend.

---

## Philosophy 4 — Explainable by Design

Every analytical conclusion SHALL be explainable.

Every conclusion SHOULD be traceable through:

Knowledge Record
↓

Rule

↓

Analysis

↓

Interpretation

↓

Report

No "black box" reasoning is permitted.

---

## Philosophy 5 — Separation of Concerns

The platform separates responsibilities into independent layers.

Knowledge Canon

≠

Rule Database

≠

Analysis Engine

≠

Interpretation Engine

≠

Presentation Layer

Each layer SHALL remain independent.

---

## Philosophy 6 — Single Source of Truth

Each academic concept SHALL have exactly one canonical definition.

Duplicate definitions are prohibited.

Other modules SHALL reference the canonical concept rather than redefine it.

---

## Philosophy 7 — Academic Integrity

Academic correctness takes precedence over implementation convenience.

Conflicting interpretations SHALL be resolved through documented academic review.

Every accepted definition SHALL be supported by authoritative sources.

---

## Philosophy 8 — Machine Readability

Knowledge SHALL be designed for both human understanding and machine processing.

Every Knowledge Record SHALL be structured to support:

- Rule Engines
- AI Systems
- Knowledge Graphs
- Validation
- Automated Reasoning

---

## Philosophy 9 — Domain Independence

The Knowledge Canon architecture SHALL support multiple domains without architectural changes.

Examples include:

- BaZi
- Feng Shui
- Qi Men Dun Jia
- Liu Yao
- I Ching
- Future domains

The architecture is domain-agnostic.

---

## Philosophy 10 — Evolution Through Governance

The Knowledge Canon is designed to evolve.

All modifications SHALL follow an approved governance process.

Changes SHALL be:

- reviewed
- validated
- versioned
- documented

No uncontrolled modifications are permitted.

---

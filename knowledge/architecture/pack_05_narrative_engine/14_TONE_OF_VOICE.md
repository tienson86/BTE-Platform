# 1. Purpose

This document defines BTE Narrative **tone** and **voice**.

Tone = attitude in a given component.

Voice = stable identity across all components.

---

# 2. Voice (Constant)

BTE Narrative voice is always:

| Voice trait | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| Consultant | Professional, composed, human |
| Clear | Easy to follow without losing depth |
| Authoritative | Confident without arrogance |
| Respectful | Client dignity first |
| Grounded | Tied to evidence, not spectacle |

Voice is never:

| Anti-voice | Why forbidden |
|------------|---------------|
| Calculator | Lists numbers without meaning |
| Mystical hype | Absolute fate marketing |
| Cold clinical jargon | Alienates the customer |
| Casual meme / slang | Breaks trust |
| Fear sales | Manipulates through panic |

---

# 3. Tone by Component

| Component | Tone | Intensity |
|-----------|------|-----------|
| Executive Summary | Briefing, decisive | Medium |
| Observation | Neutral, factual | Low–medium |
| Reasoning | Explanatory, patient | Medium |
| Impact | Empathic, concrete | Medium |
| Recommendation | Directive, supportive | Medium–high |
| Warning | Cautionary, calm | Medium (never hysterical) |
| Conclusion | Settling, integrative | Medium |

Tone may shift by component.

Voice must not.

---

# 4. Tone Dimensions

| Dimension | Prefer | Avoid |
|-----------|--------|-------|
| Distance | Professional closeness | Over-familiar OR icy distance |
| Certainty | Evidence-matched | Overclaim / underclaim for drama |
| Warmth | Steady respect | Flattery / harsh blame |
| Energy | Calm forward motion | Hype / fatalism |
| Directness | Clear statements | Vague mystique |

---

# 5. Person and Address

| Rule | Guidance |
|------|----------|
| Address | Prefer second-person customer address when narrative speaks to the chart owner |
| Agency | Frame tendencies and priorities — not moral failure |
| Collective “we” | Optional for consultant framing (“Chúng ta cần lưu ý…”) — do not overuse |
| System “I/AI” | Forbidden as the narrator identity |

---

# 6. Emotional Safety

Narrative may discuss risk.

Narrative must not:

- shame the person  
- threaten inevitable doom  
- promise guaranteed success  
- mock traditional or modern beliefs  

When evidence is insufficient, tone stays honest and calm — not apologetic theater and not false confidence.

---

# 7. Brand Alignment Checks

Before accepting tone:

1. Does this sound like a consultant?  
2. Would removing brand chrome still feel like BTE (clear / trusted / actionable)?  
3. Does it support trust → understanding → action?  

If “sounds like a scoring API log” → fail tone.

---

# 8. Forbidden Tone Patterns

✗ “Kích hoạt khi…” rule-engine tone  
✗ “Mock / placeholder / chờ engine” product-dev tone  
✗ “Bạn chắc chắn sẽ…” absolute prophecy tone  
✗ “Điểm số chứng minh bạn kém…” shaming calculator tone  
✗ “PACK_06 / Presentation Layer…” internal-doc tone

# 1. How to use this file

Edge knowledge tells the consultant **what the life feels like when the standing is not clean**.

It does not tell the engine how to decide the class.

If the engine published a class, keep it. Use this file to speak honestly about the weather around it.

If the engine published nothing, do not use this file to invent a class.

---

# 2. Borderline — almost the neighbor

**What it feels like.** The person has the main standing, and also a weekly experience of the neighbor. A Strong person who is tired like Weak every Sunday. A Weak person who can look Strong for a short, well-fed sprint.

**So what.** Do not give them two lives and two manuals. Give them **one standing plus a qualifier**: this is not extreme; the other weather is real on the margin.

**What to say.** Name the main operating system. Name the leak or the extra feed. Do not say “maybe the chart is wrong”.

**What not to say.** Percentages. “You are both Strong and Weak.” A second personality for the weekend.

---

# 3. Season feeds, root does not (or the reverse)

**What it feels like.** “I should be fine — I was born in the right climate — but I have nowhere to stand.” Or: “I have ground, but the weather of my life never feels like mine.”

**So what.** Climate without floor is confidence that does not last. Floor without climate is endurance that never feels in season.

**What to say.** You are not imagining the split. One part of you starts easily; another part cannot sit. Career and marriage advice should buy the missing piece (ground, or climate), not more of the piece you already have.

**What not to say.** A glossary of Đắc Lệnh and Thông Căn without the split.

---

# 4. Support and drain both large

**What it feels like.** Productive and thinning. Helpful and resentful. The reliable one who is somehow always empty.

**So what.** This is the classic “I work so much, why am I not Strong?” story. Work was the drain. Backup may also be real. Net is the life.

**What to say.** Your effort is not fake. The leak is also not fake. Pacing is not a personality upgrade. It is how this structure stays usable.

**What not to say.** “Try harder.” “Rest more” without naming the leak.

---

# 5. Special structure — ordinary reading is not enough

**What it feels like.** The person does not match the simple Strong/Weak stereotype. Relatives say “but you don’t look weak”. Or “you’re not as unstoppable as the reading sounds”.

**So what.** Some charts do not behave like a still object. A piece is merged, shaken, emptied, or overridden. The customer is not crazy.

**What to say.** Your standing is still the published class. The way it **arrives** in life is less linear. We will not pretend you are a textbook example.

**What not to say.** A Pattern name stolen from another pack. A secret extra class.

---

# 6. Strong in the world, empty at home (or the reverse)

**What it feels like.** Public surplus, private deficit — or the opposite.

**So what.** Strength class is natal fuel, not a mask. Masks are personality and role. If the customer lives a split, ask which room is drain. Often the “Strong” room is the job, and the leak is the unpaid family battery — or the reverse.

**What to say.** The class is not cancelled by a role. The role may be spending the class. Change the spend.

**What not to say.** That they were misclassified because they cried once.

---

# 7. Supportive luck on surplus; emptying luck on deficit

These are not edge bugs. They are **intensifiers**. See `10_LUCK.md`.

The edge is **misreading**:

- Supportive luck on Very Strong read as “the universe wants me to take everything”
- Emptying luck on Very Weak read as “I am being punished”

**What to say.** Weather is weather. Surplus plus wind needs a smaller sail. Deficit plus storm needs a harbor, not a character trial.

---

# 8. Confusing luck support with Useful God

**What it feels like.** A good decade. Someone says “your Useful God has arrived”.

**So what.** Luck that feeds the Day Master makes the **standing** easier or heavier. Useful God is a later, different question: what the chart **needs**. Those can agree. They can also fight. A Strong Day Master can have luck that feeds them and still need a drain direction in life strategy.

**What to say in this pack.** This decade changes how much force you feel. What you should **cultivate as useful** is not decided here.

**What not to say.** An element to wear, a career industry from a color, a guarantee.

---

# 9. Missing hour, missing luck

**What it feels like.** The customer wants a complete life manual.

**So what.** Natal standing may still be tellable. Time chapters may not.

**What to say.** We will not invent the hour to make the story pretty. Here is what the available pillars support. Here is what we will not claim.

**What not to say.** A guessed Very Strong/Very Weak from a dramatic biography.

---

# 10. When the biography seems to contradict the class

A Weak Day Master who built a company. A Very Strong Day Master who looks shy.

**So what.** Biography is strategy plus luck plus other structures. Weak can build if the environment paid. Very Strong can look quiet if Pattern or upbringing sealed the mouth. Strength is fuel, not theatre.

**What to say.** I believe your life. The class describes the tank, not the press release. Let us look at how expensive the press release was.

**What not to say.** The customer is lying. The engine is always the whole person.

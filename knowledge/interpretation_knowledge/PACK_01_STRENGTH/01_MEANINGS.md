# 1. How to use this file

This file answers: **what is it like to live this standing?**

It does not answer why the chart produced it.

It does not list jobs.

It does not give a to-do list.

If a paragraph could sit in Career or Recommendations without change, it does not belong here.

---

# 2. Shared truth for all five classes

Strength is not a grade.

It is how much inner fuel the Day Master has relative to the chart’s weather.

Surplus is not virtue.

Deficit is not failure.

Equilibrium is not emptiness.

The commercial question is never “Are you a winner?”

The commercial question is: **how should this person spend, protect, or steer their force?**

# 3. Very Strong — Thân Cực Vượng

## Lived meaning

You walk into rooms already carrying more force than the room asked for.

People feel you before you speak. Work yields when you lean. Resistance looks small from the inside.

So what: your default problem is not “how do I get enough energy?” Your default problem is **where the surplus goes when nobody puts a brake on it**.

## Core characteristics

- Recovery is fast. A setback is an irritant, not a collapse.
- You can override delay. Waiting feels like waste.
- You occupy space. Other people’s pace can feel like obstruction.
- Help looks optional. You can do the thing yourself, so you often do.

## Natural tendencies

- Take the load before it is assigned.
- Raise volume instead of changing method.
- Treat friction as a test of will.
- Assume that if you are not tired, the system is fine.

Consultant phrasing (VI):

> Bạn không thiếu lực. Bạn đang mang lực dư. Điều cần luận không phải “có mạnh hay không”, mà lực dư này đang nâng bạn hay đang đè người khác — kể cả chính bạn.

# 4. Strong — Thân Vượng

## Lived meaning

You start most days with a full tank. You can persist. You can lead. You can return to a task after it knocks you.

You are not overflowing in every hour. You are **sufficient**. That is different from extremity.

So what: you can carry responsibility that would empty someone else. The hidden cost is believing that stamina is the same thing as being right.

## Core characteristics

- Initiative comes easier than rest.
- Pressure is familiar. You do not panic at load.
- You prefer to decide and move.
- You can hold a position long enough for it to work — or long enough for it to become stubbornness.

## Natural tendencies

- Say yes to hard work because you can.
- Hear correction as optional commentary.
- Use endurance as proof you do not need a different strategy.
- Recover in private, then return as if nothing happened.

Consultant phrasing (VI):

> Bạn vào việc với nội lực sẵn có. Người khác thấy bạn chịu được áp lực. Chính bạn dễ chủ quan rằng mình không cần điều chỉnh.

# 5. Balanced — Trung Hòa

## Lived meaning

You are not empty and you are not overflowing. You can lean both ways: push when the room needs push, yield when the room needs yield.

People may call you “easy to work with” and also “hard to read”.

So what: your gift is range. Your risk is **waiting for the perfect middle** while a real choice expires.

## Core characteristics

- You notice both sides of a conflict.
- You can change register without a personality crisis.
- You dislike being forced into a single identity (“the strong one” / “the weak one”).
- Stillness can look like wisdom, and also like delay.

## Natural tendencies

- Collect more information before committing.
- Mediate before taking a side.
- Soften your own preference so the group stays intact.
- Under-use force because using it feels like losing balance.

Consultant phrasing (VI):

> Bạn không phải người “không có gì nổi bật”. Bạn đứng gần điểm cân. Điểm mạnh là chỉnh được nhịp. Điểm mù là chờ cân bằng hoàn hảo rồi mới chọn.

# 6. Weak — Thân Nhược

## Lived meaning

You do not start from surplus. Solo pushing costs you more than it costs a Strong Day Master. That is not a verdict on talent. It is a design of fuel.

You often do your best work when the environment feeds you: the right people, the right pace, the right structure.

So what: your winning move is **not proving you need no one**. Your winning move is choosing rooms that refill you, then using that refill well.

## Core characteristics

- You feel the room. Atmosphere is data.
- Collaboration is not a slogan. It is how force arrives.
- Depletion shows earlier. You get the warning before collapse if you listen.
- Self-assertion can arrive late, after you have already given too much.

## Natural tendencies

- Accommodate first, name your need second.
- Work harder in the wrong room instead of leaving it.
- Take feedback personally because you already doubt the tank.
- Grow quickly when support is real — faster than people expect from the word “weak”.

Consultant phrasing (VI):

> Bạn làm được nhiều hơn khi có chỗ dựa, chứ không phải khi cố gắng một mình để chứng minh. Thân nhược không có nghĩa là kém. Nó có nghĩa là nhiên liệu phải được chọn, không phải bị lãng phí.

# 7. Very Weak — Thân Cực Nhược

## Lived meaning

Energy is a scarce resource. The chart asks you to treat force as something to protect, not something to spend for proof.

This is a **design constraint**, not a statement that you are less of a person.

So what: life improves when structure is strong around you — clear roles, fewer fake emergencies, people who do not require you to perform surplus you do not have.

## Core characteristics

- Small extra load is not “a little more”. It is a different equation.
- You may look calm while running close to empty.
- Recovery needs real time, not a motivational sentence.
- The wrong environment does not merely tire you. It erases the week.

## Natural tendencies

- Hide depletion so you are not a burden.
- Accept terms that a surplus person would refuse.
- Shine in a protected niche, then get pulled into a wider arena too soon.
- Interpret exhaustion as character failure.

Consultant phrasing (VI):

> Lá số đang nói về bảo vệ năng lượng và chọn môi trường, không phải về giá trị con người. Đừng sống như thể bạn đang mang lực dư.

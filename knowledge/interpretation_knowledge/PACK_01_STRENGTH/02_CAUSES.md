# 1. How to use this file

This file answers: **why does this standing exist in the chart, in consultant language?**

It explains season, root, support, drain, control, and combination as **life-relevant structure**.

It does not give formulas.

It does not give thresholds.

If the engine has not published a cause, do not narrate that cause.

---

# 2. What “cause” means here

A cause is a way the chart feeds, holds, empties, or presses the Day Master.

The customer does not need the classical name first.

They need the lived mechanism:

| Structure | Lived mechanism |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Season (Đắc Lệnh) | The climate of birth feeds you — or does not |
| Root (Đắc Địa / Thông Căn) | You have ground to stand on — or you float |
| Support (Đắc Thế) | Backup of the same nature stands with you |
| Drain | Effort leaves you faster than it returns |
| Control | Something sits on you and limits free movement |
| Combination / clash / void | Support is merged, shaken, or emptied |

So what: the class is not a mood. It is the **net of these weathers**.

---

# 3. Season — being fed by the climate

When the month’s climate agrees with the Day Master, you do not start empty. The year itself is already on your side.

So what: confidence in the first move often comes from this. You feel “in season” even when the task is hard.

When the month’s climate disagrees, you work against the weather. The same effort costs more.

So what: you may be competent and still feel late, cold, or unheard in rooms that reward a different pace.

Season is not luck of the decade. Season is the **natal climate**. Do not tell the customer that a good month of birth means every year will be easy.

---

# 4. Root — having ground

Root is whether the Day Master has earth under the feet: branches that hold the same nature, openly or hidden.

With root, you can take a hit and still be someone. Identity has a floor.

So what: recovery is possible. You can leave a job, a city, an argument, and still know who is walking away.

Without root, talent can still exist, but it has nowhere to sit. Praise feels good and does not last. Crisis feels like vanishing.

So what: this person needs **places and people that act as ground** — not more slogans about grit.

Deep root plus surplus season is how Very Strong often feels from the inside: hard to empty.

Thin root plus drain is how Weak often feels: the floor is close.

---

# 5. Support — backup in the chart

Support is allies of the same nature: visible companions, hidden help, or generating friends.

With support, you are not the only one holding the line. Work can be shared. Identity is echoed.

So what: you can lead a team without inventing the whole machine. You can also become blind to how much the machine is carrying you.

Without support, every task is a solo climb. Independence looks heroic and is expensive.

So what: this person should stop treating help as a debt of shame.

Support is not “you have many friends in real life”. It is **chart backup**. Real-life friendship still has to be built. The chart only says whether backup is a native theme.

---

# 6. Drain — output without refill

Drain is the chart asking the Day Master to produce, express, or feed others faster than it is fed.

So what: the person can look productive while getting thinner. Busyness is not proof of strength.

Mild drain on a Strong Day Master is often the useful brake — it gives the surplus somewhere to go.

Heavy drain on a Weak Day Master is the typical exhaustion story: they are the reliable one, and the tank is the price.

Do not moralize drain as “you are too kind”. Name it as **a leak in the fuel system**. Kindness may be how the leak appears. The leak is still a leak.

---

# 7. Control — pressure sitting on you

Control is restriction: something in the chart checks the Day Master.

So what: life includes authority, rules, deadlines, or inner self-criticism that does not turn off.

On surplus, control can be the missing steering wheel. It makes Strong usable in institutions.

On deficit, control can feel like a hand on the back of the neck. The person complies, then disappears in private.

Do not tell a Weak Day Master that more discipline will create fuel. Discipline without refill is just a tighter grip.

---

# 8. Combination, clash, and void — support that will not sit still

Combination can merge forces so the Day Master is no longer standing alone — or so a needed piece is pulled into another story.

Clash can shake the ground. What looked like root becomes weather.

Void can empty a piece that looked present on paper.

So what: the customer may feel “I should be Strong, but I don’t feel it” or “I should be fragile, but I keep surviving”. The chart is not lying. **A piece is not behaving as a still object.**

This is not a Pattern lecture. This is Strength weather becoming unstable.

If combination / clash / void is not published for the case, do not narrate it.

---

# 9. How causes cluster into classes

These are consulting pictures, not recipes.

## Very Strong

Season feeds, root holds, support stands close, and drain/control are too small to spend the surplus.

So what: the person has fuel, floor, and backup at once. The story is excess, not competence.

## Strong

Enough of season, root, or support is present that the Day Master is not fighting alone. Drain exists but does not win.

So what: the person can carry load. They still have a weather. They are not a machine.

## Balanced

Feed and empty are both real. Neither side owns the chart.

So what: the person can be Strong-in-the-room on Tuesday and empty on Friday without being two different souls. The work is choosing, not waiting for the chart to pick for them.

## Weak

Drain, control, or hostile season outweighs root and support. The Day Master spends more than it holds.

So what: effort is real. The return is smaller unless the environment pays the difference.

## Very Weak

The floor is thin, the climate is against, backup is scarce, and output or pressure is heavy.

So what: this is not a motivational gap. This is a **fuel architecture** that requires protection and structure.

---

# 10. What not to do with causes

- Do not list classical terms as if the customer asked for a glossary.
- Do not say “because the score is high”.
- Do not say “because rule X fired”.
- Do not invent a cause that the case does not show.
- Do not use Useful God language (“you should use Water”) as a Strength cause.
- Do not treat gender, luck, or a single Ten God as the whole Strength story.

---

# 11. Customer Why — knowledge intent

When Cause knowledge is composed into Customer Mode “Why”, keep only the causes that are present, in human weather:

- You are not standing alone (season / root / support).
- You are standing alone (missing those).
- Effort leaks (drain).
- Something sits on you (control).
- The floor moves (combination / clash / void).

That is enough.

The rest is Validation Mode.

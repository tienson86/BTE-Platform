# 1. How to use this file

Recommendations are the **steering wheel**.

They must follow from meaning and challenges, not from a slogan.

Each class has:

- Do
- Avoid
- Environment
- Habits
- Career direction (load, not job title)
- Learning
- Lifestyle

Do not paste Career or Health chapters here. Point the action.

Customer Mode should pick a short set, not print this entire file.

---

# 2. Very Strong

**Do.** Install a brake: a deputy, a partner, a written “no” list, a review that can stop you. Put surplus into one main build. Practice asking a question before you close a decision.

**Avoid.** Taking every load. Treating disagreement as incompetence. Using speed as a substitute for a map. Performing inexhaustibility for people who will not invoice your body.

**Environment.** Arenas that need a closer. Not rooms whose only virtue is endless process.

**Habits.** Hard training **and** a downshift that is scheduled, not earned. Sleep as a performance part.

**Career direction.** Command of a real problem. Not a title that only inflates the ego.

**Learning.** Strategy and listening as skills, not as insults. You already know how to push.

**Lifestyle.** Fewer simultaneous wars. One recovery ritual that cannot be skipped because a new mountain appeared.

---

# 3. Strong

**Do.** Choose the hard thing that is also the right thing. Put rest on the calendar. Invite one person who is allowed to change your method.

**Avoid.** Collecting difficulty as identity. Hearing feedback without revising. Becoming the family’s only battery.

**Environment.** Places that reward finishing and still permit a revised plan.

**Habits.** Automatic recovery: same shutdown hour, a walk that is not another metric.

**Career direction.** Ownership with a succession path so you are not irreplaceable-and-trapped.

**Learning.** Reframing practice. Apprenticeship is native; unlearning is the growth edge.

**Lifestyle.** Maintenance over heroics. The boring week is a feature.

---

# 4. Balanced

**Do.** Decide on a clock. Write the preference before you collect the tenth opinion. Run campaigns with an end date.

**Avoid.** Calling delay fairness. Softening until no one can follow. Waiting for inner stillness that means “never”.

**Environment.** Mixed teams, translator roles, partnerships with a closer or a specialist.

**Habits.** A weekly decision hour. A note: “what I actually want”.

**Career direction.** Integrator with authority to close, not only to host.

**Learning.** Time-boxed research. Depth in one method per season, not five methods forever.

**Lifestyle.** Alternate effort and air on purpose so the swing does not become chaos.

---

# 5. Weak

**Do.** Choose feeding rooms. Ask directly. Price the work. Leave emptying rooms on a date, not after a collapse. Take support without treating it as a character stain.

**Avoid.** Proving you need no one. Over-accommodating. Using a supportive luck window to imitate a Very Strong calendar. Staying for the identity of being kind.

**Environment.** Craft plus cover. Mentors, sane teams, clients who do not eat people.

**Habits.** Refill in the same week as output. A sentence you can say: “I cannot take that on.”

**Career direction.** Specialist value in a held structure. Not lone-wolf grind as morality.

**Learning.** With people. Feedback-rich, not humiliation-rich.

**Lifestyle.** Protect sleep and noise. The “soft” conditions are how the standing works.

---

# 6. Very Weak

**Do.** Build frame first: calendar, help, niche, money floor. Convert any good luck into structure that remains. Report depletion early to people who have earned it.

**Avoid.** Auditioning for surplus life. Ambush debates. Large spans of management. Heroic bets. Shame about needing a small, well-built life.

**Environment.** Quiet, high-trust, specialist. Administrative armor if you must face the market.

**Habits.** Same sleep window. Pre-decided cuts. One-to-one communication for hard topics.

**Career direction.** Protected depth. Counsel, not command.

**Learning.** Slow mastery in a held space. Not open-plan urgency.

**Lifestyle.** Reduce surface area. Fewer inputs. Treat energy as the professional tool it is.

# 8. Pairing rule

Every “avoid” should be the overuse of the same structure as a “do”.

If the pair is random (“do lead / avoid the color red”), it is not Strength knowledge.

# 1. How to use this file

These examples teach tone and coverage.

They are **not** sentence-library records.

They are **not** facts about a real case.

Do not paste them unchanged into a report. A real person needs the causes that are actually present.

Each vignette shows Conclusion → Why → So what → One life domain → Do / Avoid, compressed.

---

# 2. Very Strong — the closer who never stops

A founder who finishes other people’s companies and then cannot sit in the company they finished.

**Say.** Your Day Master belongs to the Very Strong category. You do not start empty. Season and ground are both with you, so surplus is the native weather. That is why you can close. It is also why a finished room feels like a problem: the engine is still hot. In partnership, people stop bringing you the truth because the truth gets run over. Put a brake in the org chart. Do not take the next mountain because the last one ended.

**Do not say.** You are extremely powerful. Rule IDs. A guaranteed IPO year.

---

# 3. Strong — the backbone who cannot hear the mechanic

A delivery director, trusted, always there on Friday, three years into a method that is now the bottleneck.

**Say.** You belong to the Strong category. You have enough inner fuel to own a hard piece and not drop it. People sleep because you do not. The cost is treating endurance as proof that the method is right. In learning, you add hours before you change the frame. Keep the ownership. Add one person who is allowed to revise the plan. Avoid collecting difficulty as identity.

**Do not say.** You are strong, therefore you should never rest. A dictionary of Thân Vượng.

---

# 4. Balanced — the integrator who missed the window

A product lead who can hear engineering and sales, and who shipped six months late because both sides were still a little bit right.

**Say.** You stand near balance. You can live in more than one tempo. That is why mixed teams work with you. It is also why a window can close while you are still being fair. In decision making, fairness without a clock is delay. Set a decision hour. Write what you actually want before the tenth opinion. Avoid calling the delay wisdom.

**Do not say.** You are average. You have no personality.

---

# 5. Weak — the excellent specialist in the wrong room

A designer whose work is precise, whose open-plan sales culture calls them “not hungry”, who goes home empty and calls it a character flaw.

**Say.** Your Day Master belongs to the Weak category. That does not mean your craft is small. It means you do not start from surplus, so a room that never refills you will look like incompetence when it is actually a leak. You read atmosphere early — that is radar, not fragility. Choose a held specialist seat. Ask for the project directly. Avoid proving you need no one. Avoid staying to remain the kind one.

**Do not say.** You are weak, so you will fail. Wear a lucky color and become Strong.

---

# 6. Very Weak — the thinker who was promoted into a stadium

A researcher whose niche papers are excellent, promoted to run a noisy department, now mistaken for lazy because the calendar is a confiscation of thought.

**Say.** This standing is Very Weak: energy is a scarce tool, not a moral grade. The niche worked because the frame was small and high-trust. The stadium is a different machine. Leadership for you is counsel, not command of a crowd. Build administrative armor or step back to protected depth. Do not audition for a surplus personality. Convert any good period into structure that remains when the weather cools.

**Do not say.** Fate is poor. Push through. This is your growth edge.

---

# 7. Conflict edge — season without root

A customer who “should feel lucky” by birth month and still feels like they vanish in a crisis.

**Say.** Part of you is fed by climate: first moves come. Another part has little floor: when the hit lands, there is nowhere to sit. That split is the story, not a broken reading. In career, buy ground — a place, a contract, a team that still exists after a bad week — not more first-move theatre. In health, recovery is identity work, not a spa slogan.

**Do not say.** Ignore the engine class. Invent a second class. Explain Đắc Lệnh as a textbook chapter.

---

# 8. Luck edge — Weak natal, supportive decade

A Weak Day Master in a decade that finally feeds them, about to say yes to three roles because “it’s my time”.

**Say.** The standing did not become Very Strong. The weather started paying the difference. This is a growth window. Use it to install platform, craft, and a money floor. Do not use it to copy a Very Strong calendar. When the weather cools, you will want the structure, not the memory of having been busy.

**Do not say.** Your Useful God has arrived (unless that pack has spoken). You will be rich this decade.

---

# 9. Quality bar for any future example

A new vignette is allowed only if it:

1. Names one class
2. Gives a cause in weather language, not scores
3. Answers So what in one life domain that is not a repeat of the meaning
4. Gives one do and one avoid that are the same structure
5. Contains no Rule IDs, percents, or dumps
6. Does not shame Weak or worship Very Strong

If it fails the bar, it is not knowledge. It is decoration.

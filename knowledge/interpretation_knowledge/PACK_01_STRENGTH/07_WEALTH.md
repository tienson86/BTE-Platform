# 1. How to use this file

Strength does not say “you will be rich”.

It says **how this fuel system behaves around money**.

Ten Gods and Useful God will later add which wealth methods are native. This file must not steal that job.

---

# 2. Very Strong

**Behavior.** Money is another object that can be pushed. You make large moves. You hate feeling small in a transaction.

**Saving.** Saving feels like idling the engine. You can save when saving is framed as ammunition, not as fear.

**Investment.** You overweight conviction. You double down because you can tolerate the heat. That wins in a true edge and destroys in a wrong thesis.

**Risk.** You under-feel ruin. Ruin is a story that happens to people with smaller tanks. Until it happens to you.

**Opportunity.** You catch opportunities that require nerve and speed. You miss opportunities that require waiting while looking inactive.

So what: put a **written loss limit** in front of the surplus. The tank will not do that job for you.

---

# 3. Strong

**Behavior.** You earn by carrying. Income often tracks load. That is a strength and a trap: you may only feel legitimate when you are overworking for the invoice.

**Saving.** You can save through routine. Automatic systems fit you better than mood-based thrift.

**Investment.** You like assets you can work: a practice, a building, a long position you “hold through”. Trading as entertainment is usually a leak, not a talent.

**Risk.** You tolerate operational risk (hard work, delayed payoff). You may under-see financial concentration: one client, one property, one bet that is really your identity.

**Opportunity.** You win the long build. You lose the moment that required selling, walking away, or taking a smaller sure thing.

So what: separate **self-worth from billable stamina**, or money will always demand another shift.

---

# 4. Balanced

**Behavior.** You can both spend on coherence and hold for safety. The split can look mature. It can also look like never fully committing to a wealth strategy.

**Saving.** You save when the story is balanced — emergency fund plus a life that still feels like a life. Extreme austerity will not last. Extreme display will embarrass you.

**Investment.** You see more than one scenario. That protects you from cult stocks. It also delays the buy until the move is gone.

**Risk.** You prefer diversified, explainable risk. Black-box thrill is not your native language.

**Opportunity.** You do well in partnerships and mixed portfolios. You do poorly when a window requires an ugly, one-sided bet and you are still listing the other side.

So what: pick a wealth policy on a calendar, not when the inner weather is perfectly still.

---

# 5. Weak

**Behavior.** Money is safety and proof. You may undercharge because asking feels like a fight you cannot afford. Or you may cling because the tank already feels low.

**Saving.** Saving is medicine. A buffer is not greed. It is how a low natal tank survives a bad month. Shame about needing a buffer is expensive.

**Investment.** You do better with guided, boring, structurally sound vehicles than with lone-wolf conviction plays. A trusted advisor or a simple rule-set is part of the fuel system.

**Risk.** You feel risk early. That can save you. It can also keep you in cash while a Strong partner calls you small. Neither of you is the whole truth.

**Opportunity.** You catch opportunities that arrive through people: introductions, employment, a client who trusts you. You miss opportunities that require cold, unsupported push.

So what: price your work as if support were a cost of goods. Do not donate the tank.

---

# 6. Very Weak

**Behavior.** Money must buy frame: time, help, a quieter room. Status spending that increases performance demand is a bad trade.

**Saving.** A floor is existential. Without it, every decision is panic. Build the floor before the story.

**Investment.** Complexity is an enemy. Few, understood, low-maintenance holdings. If you cannot sleep, it is too much, even if a Strong friend is excited.

**Risk.** Ruin is close in experience even when it is not close in numbers. Respect that signal. Also do not let it forbid every income skill that fits the niche.

**Opportunity.** Small, repeated, protected income is better than a heroic bet. A single “this will change everything” pitch is usually a predator of this standing.

So what: wealth strategy is **armor for energy**, not a personality upgrade.

---

# 7. Composition rule

Do not promise returns.

Do not name specific stocks, property tips, or “lucky industries” from Strength alone.

If the customer is in financial distress, this knowledge stays behavioral. It is not a substitute for licensed advice.

# 1. How to use this file

Strength health knowledge is about **how this fuel system wears**.

It is not a doctor.

It must not name diseases as destiny.

It must not sell supplements.

When in doubt, send the customer to care, not to a metaphor.

---

# 2. Very Strong

**Energy.** High output is native. You notice other people’s tiredness before your own. That delay is the health risk.

**Stress.** You metabolize acute stress as action. Unspent stress becomes heat: jaw, sleep that is short and hard, a body that does not downshift.

**Lifestyle.** You under-use recovery because recovery looks like losing. Build recovery as a performance part, like a second training block, or you will not do it.

**Balance.** The useful balance is cyclic load — hard, then actually off. A life that is always “on” will not warn you politely.

So what: the body is not an infinite proof of the class. Surplus still ends. The ending is sharper if you never practiced stopping.

---

# 3. Strong

**Energy.** Reliable. You can work a long season. The danger is the long season with no off-season.

**Stress.** You carry chronic load well until you do not. The break, when it comes, surprises people who thought you were the healthy one.

**Lifestyle.** Maintenance boredom is your friend: sleep timing, walking, a practice that is not another competition. You will skip it if it is framed as softness.

**Balance.** Put rest on the same calendar as delivery. If rest is “when everything is done”, it will not occur.

So what: health for you is **scheduled downshift**, not a motivational weekend.

---

# 4. Balanced

**Energy.** Variable. You have more than one gear. The gift is you can feel the gear change. The risk is ignoring a low gear because last week you were fine.

**Stress.** You do better with mixed stress than with one extreme: not only fire, not only emptiness. Monotony of either kind is the strain.

**Lifestyle.** You need both effort and air. A program that is only punishment, or only spa, will not hold.

**Balance.** Track the week, not the identity. “I am a balanced person so I must be fine” is how you miss a bad month.

So what: your health skill is listening to the swing, then adjusting before the swing becomes a story.

---

# 5. Weak

**Energy.** The budget is real. A social week plus a work crisis is not “the same week with extra events”. It is a different equation.

**Stress.** You feel stress early. That is protective if you honor it. If you override it to look Strong, you pay twice.

**Lifestyle.** Regular meals, sleep, and a room that is not always loud are not lifestyle brands. They are how the standing functions. Choose work and city with this in mind.

**Balance.** Balance means refill in the same week as output. Delayed holidays do not repay a quarter of leak.

So what: treating rest as optional is how this class becomes the sudden illness that “doesn’t run in the family”.

---

# 6. Very Weak

**Energy.** Scarce. Protect it like a professional tool. People with surplus will not understand. They do not have to. You still have to protect it.

**Stress.** Small stressors stack. Three mild demands can equal one crisis. Design the week with stack in mind, not with each demand looking innocent.

**Lifestyle.** Fewer inputs: less chaotic travel, less obligatory nightlife, more control of morning and night. This is not fragility theatre. It is operations.

**Balance.** A strong external routine is medicine: same sleep window, same food rhythm, same people who do not create fake emergency.

So what: health here is **the frame that keeps intelligence online**. Without the frame, advice about “mindset” is noise.

---

# 7. Composition rule

Stay at energy, stress, and lifestyle.

Do not map Five Elements to organs in Customer Mode as if it were a lab result.

Do not leak the token `hot` as an internal temperature dump. If temperature is a published fact from another pack, that pack may speak. This file does not.

If the customer reports symptoms, the sentence is: see a clinician. Then, if useful, add the pacing knowledge above.

# 1. How to use this file

Natal Strength is the standing.

Luck is weather over that standing.

This file answers: **what changes in lived life when the decade or year supports or weakens the Day Master?**

It does not compute luck.

If luck is not published, do not use these units. Say the luck chapter cannot be told yet.

Supportive luck is **not** Useful God.

Unfavorable luck is **not** a curse.

---

# 2. The interaction, in one picture

```text
Natal surplus + luck that feeds you  →  overdrive
Natal surplus + luck that empties you →  correction, not identity loss
Natal deficit + luck that feeds you  →  growth window
Natal deficit + luck that empties you →  conservation window
Natal balance + either weather       →  a lean, then a choice
```

Transition years are when the weather changes. The person is not a new soul. The budget changed.

---

# 3. Very Strong natal

**Supportive luck.** The surplus gets a tailwind. This is a period of visible impact — titles, builds, fights you can win. It is also the easiest time to take three lives’ worth of load.

So what: expand **one** main line, not every line. The avoid is omnipotence.

**Unfavorable luck.** You meet friction you cannot bully. This is not you becoming Weak as a person. It is the decade teaching the brake you would not install.

So what: use the period to learn pacing and listening. Do not spend it proving the weather is wrong.

**Transition.** When support arrives after a dry luck, the temptation is revenge-work: take everything. When support leaves, the temptation is to push harder. Both are the same error — using surplus as the only tool.

---

# 4. Strong natal

**Supportive luck.** A harvest period for the long build. Responsibility increases. People hand you more.

So what: negotiate recovery as the load grows. Promotion without rest is just a heavier Strong.

**Unfavorable luck.** Delivery gets harder. You may still finish, at a higher price. The mistake is ignoring the price until the body or the marriage invoices you.

So what: keep the craft, reduce the optional wars.

**Transition.** Into support: choose the project that deserves the extra tank. Into unfavor: keep identity, change volume.

---

# 5. Balanced natal

**Supportive luck.** You can lean Strong for a while without becoming a different character. Good for a defined campaign: a launch, a degree, a move.

So what: set an end date for the lean. Balanced people forget they were borrowing surplus.

**Unfavorable luck.** You lean Weak. Sensitivity rises. You need more frame. This is information, not failure.

So what: simplify. One priority. More sleep. Fewer simultaneous identities.

**Transition.** This class feels transitions clearly. Use that. Do not wait for the inner weather to be perfectly still before you change the outer plan. The weather *is* the plan.

---

# 6. Weak natal

**Supportive luck.** The environment starts paying the difference. Mentors, platforms, kinder rooms, a partner who feeds. Work done in this window compounds.

So what: say yes to **feeding** opportunities. Say no to using the window to imitate a Very Strong calendar.

**Unfavorable luck.** The leak widens. This is a conservation decade, not a proof decade.

So what: protect the craft at a smaller scale. Do not interpret the weather as “I was never any good”.

**Transition.** Into support: build the platform you will need when the weather cools. Into unfavor: spend the savings of structure you built, not the last of the nerves.

---

# 7. Very Weak natal

**Supportive luck.** A rare open window. Use it to install frame: the right job, the right city, the right help, the right medical or therapeutic support if needed. Do not use it to audition for a surplus life.

So what: convert luck into **structure that outlasts the luck**.

**Unfavorable luck.** The smallest extra demand is expensive. This is not the time to prove toughness to relatives.

So what: reduce surface area. Keep the niche. Let stronger people take the public war.

**Transition.** Into support: move carefully; even good luck can overstimulate. Into unfavor: pre-decide the cuts so you are not negotiating them in a crash.

---

# 8. What this file must never say

- A year of marriage, death, bankruptcy, or promotion as a guarantee
- “Luck supports you, therefore use Water” (Useful God theft)
- Rule IDs or scores
- Content when luck data is missing

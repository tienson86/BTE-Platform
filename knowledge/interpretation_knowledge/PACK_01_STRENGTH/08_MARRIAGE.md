# 1. How to use this file

Strength in relationship answers: **how much room does this person’s fuel take, and what does the other person have to live next to?**

It does not say who they should marry.

It does not say they will be alone.

A later Marriage / Ten Gods pack may add spouse structure. This file must not pretend to be that pack.

---

# 2. Very Strong

**Bond.** The relationship is a field you enter at full volume. Love can be loyal and still feel like weather to the partner.

**Partner.** A partner who cannot name a need will disappear. A partner who only fights will create a house of two wars. The workable partner can stay present **and** stop you.

**Communication.** You speak as if the point is obvious. Intimacy needs you to slow the first sentence. Ask one real question before you solve.

**Family.** You become the spine of the family whether you applied or not. Aging parents, siblings in crisis, children in trouble — you pick it up. Resentment grows if nobody is allowed to pick you up.

So what: marriage is not a second company to dominate. It is the one room where surplus must sit down.

---

# 3. Strong

**Bond.** You show love by carrying. Bills, logistics, the hard conversation with the school. The partner may be cared for and still feel unseen.

**Partner.** Someone who respects load, and who will still say “that method is hurting us”. A purely yielding partner trains your blind spot.

**Communication.** You report facts. You under-report inner weather. The partner learns the weather from your silence or your extra shifts.

**Family.** You are dependable. The risk is becoming the unpaid infrastructure. Dependable people get used, then called cold when they are empty.

So what: let the partner see the tank, not only the work the tank did.

---

# 4. Balanced

**Bond.** You can meet a Strong partner without vanishing and a Weak partner without crushing them — if you choose a position. Without a position, both feel you slipping.

**Partner.** Often another person with range, or a clearer extremist plus your integrator function. Name the function. Unnamed, you become the household’s shock absorber with no title and no rest.

**Communication.** You are good at hearing. You must also declare. “I could go either way” is peace in a meeting and fog in a marriage.

**Family.** You keep factions from exploding. That is a gift. It is also how your own preference never gets a vote.

So what: intimacy needs a preference on the table, not only a fair process.

---

# 5. Weak

**Bond.** You attach through attunement. You know the partner’s mood early. You may know it earlier than your own.

**Partner.** A partner who feeds the room — emotionally steady, not extractive — is not luxury. A partner who needs you to be their only battery will recreate the natal leak.

**Communication.** You hint. You hope they will read you as well as you read them. Many decent people will not. Direct asking is a skill, not a personality transplant.

**Family.** You keep peace. You absorb. Then you go far away inside. Family members say “we never fight”. That is not always health.

So what: a good marriage for this standing is a **refill**, not a second unpaid job.

---

# 6. Very Weak

**Bond.** The relationship must be a held space. Chaos-as-passion is a medical event for the nervous system, not a romantic style.

**Partner.** Look for respect of limits, not a savior and not a manager. Savior creates debt. Manager creates a child. An adult frame creates a life.

**Communication.** You need time. Ambush talks after midnight will not produce your true sentence. Schedule the hard conversation like you would schedule a specialist — because for you, it is.

**Family.** Large, noisy, obligation-heavy family systems can erase you. Boundaries will look selfish to people who run on surplus. They are how you stay in the family at all.

So what: the question is not “are you lovable?” The question is **“does this bond protect the little fuel there is?”**

---

# 7. Composition rule

Speak to the customer’s side of the bond.

Do not insult the partner who is not in the room.

Do not predict cheating, infertility, or divorce from Strength class.

If luck or another pack has not published relationship timing, do not invent a wedding year.

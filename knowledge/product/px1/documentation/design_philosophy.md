# Design Philosophy — Result Experience V2

Version: 2.0.0  
Status: **OFFICIAL — Design Only**  
Sprint: Phase X · PX-1  
Depends on: Product Manifesto · Experience Principles · Brand Language

---

## 1. Why this page exists

BTE exists so understanding creates better decisions.

The Result Page is the moment that promise becomes tangible.

It must transform analytical truth into a guided consultation the user can trust, understand, and act on.

---

## 2. What the page is

A written session with an experienced consultant:

- confirms identity  
- states the situation  
- recommends with reasons  
- warns where needed  
- walks life domains  
- shows evidence on request  
- teaches only if invited  

---

## 3. What the page is not

Not a dashboard of equal widgets.  
Not an admin console.  
Not a developer inspector.  
Not a fortune-telling stage.  
Not a marketing landing.

Calculator aesthetics — charts, IDs, schema, timestamps — are apparatus.  
Apparatus serves the consultant. It does not lead.

---

## 4. Human before technology

Technology amplifies expertise.  
It does not replace judgment in the voice of the page.

Users should feel guided by wisdom, not by an algorithm dump.

---

## 5. Transparency without noise

Explain how conclusions are reached — inside recommendations (Vì sao) and later evidence (charts, optional technical).

Do not confuse transparency with exposing every internal field in the first viewport.

---

## 6. Eastern knowledge, modern clarity

Traditional terms may appear with human gloss.  
Mystery is not a brand asset.  
Respect for tradition and commitment to clarity coexist.

---

## 7. Quality bar

A Result Experience is complete only when it is:

- analytically faithful (engines unchanged by this sprint)  
- visually calm  
- easy to understand  
- consistent with the Manifesto  
- useful for a real decision  

Working software that feels like a console fails this philosophy.

---

## 8. Stop line

Philosophy here binds PX-1 and all future Result Experience V2 work.

END

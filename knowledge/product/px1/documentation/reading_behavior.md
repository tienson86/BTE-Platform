# Reading Behavior — Result Experience V2

Version: 2.0.0  
Status: **OFFICIAL — Design Only**  
Sprint: Phase X · PX-1

---

## 1. How people actually read reports

First-time users:

1. Check “is this mine?”  
2. Hunt a conclusion  
3. Hunt what to do  
4. Only then inspect proof  
5. Rarely finish every appendix  

Power users / practitioners:

- May jump to a domain or technical panel  
- Still need identity confirmation  
- Must not be forced through Knowledge  

PX-1 serves both without splitting into two products.

---

## 2. Stop-ability

The experience succeeds if the user can stop after:

**Hero + Tóm tắt tư vấn + Định hướng chính**

and still leave with understanding and a next action.

Everything after is guided depth.

---

## 3. Attention decay

Visual and verbal energy must front-load P1.

If charts or technical blocks appear early, attention is spent on apparatus and advice feels like a footnote. That is the failure mode V2 removes.

---

## 4. Re-reading

Return visits use TOC.

Behavior rules:

- Same order  
- Collapsed stays collapsed  
- Hero still confirms identity  
- Deep links do not skip trust  

---

## 5. Cross-device reading

Mobile users read the same story in one column.

They do not receive a “lite calculator” or a “different chapter order.”

They may expand less. Defaults must therefore keep advice visible without expand.

---

## 6. Cognitive load controls

| Load source | Control |
|-------------|---------|
| Too many bullets | Cap 5 in summary |
| Too many CTAs | One Primary |
| Too many terms | Vietnamese + gloss; engine terms hidden |
| Too many charts | After advice; quiet |
| Too much theory | Knowledge collapsed |

---

## 7. Emotional checkpoints

| Checkpoint | Desired state |
|------------|---------------|
| After Hero | Recognized |
| After Tóm tắt | Oriented |
| After Định hướng | Directed |
| After Lưu ý | Cautious, not afraid |
| After domains | Understood in life terms |
| After charts | Confirmed |
| After appendix | Complete |

---

## 8. Stop line

Design for how consultations are read, not how engines emit fields.

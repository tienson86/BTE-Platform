# Commercial Report — Result Experience V2

Version: 2.0.0  
Status: **OFFICIAL — Design Only**  
Sprint: Phase X · PX-1

---

## 1. Why V2 is a commercial artifact

Customers pay for a consultation they can use.

They do not pay to admire a correct pipeline.

Commercial value appears when the user can say:

- Tôi hiểu tình huống của mình  
- Tôi biết việc nên làm  
- Tôi tin lời khuyên có căn cứ  

PX-1 is the experience architecture that makes those sentences possible.

---

## 2. Commercial reading vs engine completeness

| Engine completeness | Commercial completeness |
|---------------------|-------------------------|
| All fields present | Right fields in the right order |
| Charts accurate | Charts after advice |
| IDs traceable | IDs hidden until asked |
| Knowledge attached | Knowledge optional |

A technically complete page can still be commercially unreadable.

---

## 3. Life domains as commercial chapters

V2 treats five life areas as first-class consulting chapters:

Sự nghiệp · Tài chính · Quan hệ · Sức khỏe · Vận trình

Career remains the default visual lead when capabilities compete (Commercial V1 posture), but the page is no longer a single-capability dump. Empty domains stay honest.

---

## 4. Conversion without theatre

Primary CTA sits with Định hướng chính.

No urgency countdown.  
No offer wall.  
No English growth-hacking labels.

Conversion is commitment to the stated direction — after understanding.

---

## 5. Trust economics

Trust is spent when:

- Hero shows developer metadata  
- Charts outshout summary  
- Copy mixes English chips  
- Errors look like stack traces  

Trust is earned when the page behaves like a senior consultant.

That trust is the commercial moat.

---

Commercial V1 release status is unchanged by this design sprint.

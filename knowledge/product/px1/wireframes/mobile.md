## Mobile rules

- Strict single column  
- No horizontal rec carousel  
- Primary CTA sits with Định hướng chính — not a covering FAB that hides text  
- Expand hit areas remain usable  
- Page does not require horizontal pan  
- Jump list (if any) uses the same Vietnamese titles in the same order  
- First viewport: full Hero + Tóm tắt beginning

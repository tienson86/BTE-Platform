---

## 1. Purpose

The Result Page speaks like a senior consultant presenting a written report.

Not a fortune teller.  
Not a chatbot.  
Not a log file.

---

## 2. Voice

| Quality | Practice |
|---------|----------|
| Calm | No urgency, no hype |
| Precise | One idea per sentence in P1 |
| Respectful | User decides |
| Evidence-based | Vì sao precedes Việc cần làm |
| Confident | Clear direction without fake certainty |
| Educational | Teach only when it serves the decision |

Avoid: sensational, mystical, absolute, playful, aggressive.

---

## 3. Explain → recommend

Every recommendation block follows:

```
Vì sao
  ↓
Kết quả kỳ vọng
  ↓
Việc cần làm
```

Never open a rec with a command and hide the reason after expand.

---

## 4. Tóm tắt tư vấn

- Maximum 5 bullets  
- One sentence each  
- Action-oriented  
- Whole-consultation altitude — not a domain dump  

Good intent:

> Nên ưu tiên môi trường làm việc ổn định trước khi mở rộng trách nhiệm quản lý.

Bad intent:

> Score SEL = 0.82; pattern CLUSTER_A detected.

---

## 5. Hero copy

| Part | Rule |
|------|------|
| Headline | One professional line · no metric parade |
| One-liner | Essence of the session · one sentence |
| Status | Human Vietnamese |

Hero does not narrate the entire report.

---

## 6. Domain copy

Each domain answers one question.

Keep intro short.  
Let recommendation cards carry action.  
Analysis preview explains; it does not repeat the rec twice.

---

## 7. Warnings

Honest and bounded.

Pair risk with mitigation posture when possible.  
Do not write horror.  
Do not write empty legal fog.

---

## 8. Charts and knowledge

Chart caption: what it shows + why it matters to advice already given.

Knowledge: Định nghĩa → Giải thích → Tham chiếu.  
Knowledge never sells.

---

## 9. Certainty language

Prefer:

- nên  
- phù hợp hơn  
- trong giai đoạn này  
- nếu… thì…  

Avoid:

- chắc chắn  
- định mệnh  
- phải làm ngay kẻo mất lộc  

Manifesto: understanding precedes action. Copy must not bully.

---

## 10. Length targets (density, not engine truncation)

| Surface | Target |
|---------|--------|
| Hero one-liner | 1 sentence |
| Summary bullet | 1 sentence |
| Vì sao | 1–2 short sentences |
| Kết quả kỳ vọng | 1 sentence |
| Việc cần làm | 1 sentence or ≤5 short bullets |
| Analysis preview | Short; rest expands |
| Knowledge teaser | Short; rest via Đọc thêm |

---

## 11. Stop line

Copy serves trust → understanding → action.

## 1. Purpose

Rank every Result component by **commercial consulting value**.

Higher priority must win attention, space, and reading order. Lower priority may be dense, collapsed, or deferred.

---

## 2. Priority ranks

### Priority 1 — Decide with me

| Component | Role |
|-----------|------|
| **Executive Summary** | Consulting conclusion in human language |
| **Identity / Context** | Who this report is for (trust anchor) |
| **Career** | Primary commercial direction (Career Selection Assessment) |
| **Recommendation** | What to do next (What / Why / How / When / Expected outcome) |

**Why Priority 1:** A consulting session opens with who you are, what matters, and what to do. If these fail, charts cannot save the product. Commercial conversion and trust live here.

### Priority 2 — Believe me

| Component | Role |
|-----------|------|
| **Evidence** | Grounding for claims (interpretation anchors, capability reasoning) |
| **Strength** | What the customer can lean on |
| **Useful God / support axis** | Commercial framing of support direction (customer language first) |

**Why Priority 2:** Customers act when they trust the advice. Strength and support explain *why* the Recommendation is theirs — without requiring technical literacy. Evidence sits here, not in Priority 1, so advice is not buried under proof.

### Priority 3 — Inspect if needed

| Component | Role |
|-----------|------|
| **Charts** | Visual analytical confirmation |
| **Tables** | Structured analytical breakdown |
| **Technical data** | Stem/branch, raw indicators, expert-depth fields |

**Why Priority 3:** Necessary for credibility and power users; fatal if they dominate first viewport. Calculator posture begins when Priority 3 outranks Priority 1.

---

## 4. Conflict rules

| Conflict | Winner |
|----------|--------|
| Chart visual drama vs Exec clarity | Exec |
| Dual Capability plans competing | Career Strategy primary · Promotion secondary |
| Technical term vs commercial term | Commercial term in P1/P2 surfaces |
| Long evidence vs short action | Action (evidence collapses) |
| Knowledge depth vs first-session clarity | Clarity |

---

## 5. Success criteria

- First scroll answers Identity + Exec + Career direction + primary Rec.  
- Charts never appear before the customer knows what to do.  
- Promotion never visually outranks Career Strategy on Commercial V1 default journey.

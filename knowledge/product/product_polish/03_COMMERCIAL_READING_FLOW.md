# 03 — Commercial Reading Flow

Version: 1.0.0  
Status: **OFFICIAL — Design Reference**  
Date: 2026-08-08  
Sprint: Product Polish V1 · Sprint A  

---

## 1. Purpose

Design the **ideal reading journey** for a commercial consulting Result — the path a first-time customer should feel, regardless of which frozen zone renders each beat.

---

## 2. Ideal journey

```
Customer opens Result
        ↓
Who am I
        ↓
Strengths
        ↓
Challenges
        ↓
Career direction
        ↓
Actions
        ↓
Evidence
        ↓
Details
```

---

## 3. Beat definitions

| Beat | Customer question | Product obligation | Typical surface (frozen) |
|------|-------------------|--------------------|--------------------------|
| **Open** | Is this for me? | Instant identity + calm consulting tone | Context / Identity |
| **Who am I** | What is my posture / profile in plain language? | One clear identity statement | Executive Summary (central) |
| **Strengths** | What can I rely on? | 1–3 strength lines, not a catalog | Exec supporting · Analysis strength |
| **Challenges** | What must I watch? | Bounded risks + mitigation posture | Exec / Analysis risk |
| **Career direction** | Where should I aim? | Named Career Selection Assessment outcome | Career projection · Exec |
| **Actions** | What do I do next? | Primary Rec: What / Why / How / When / Expected outcome | Recommendation zone |
| **Evidence** | Why should I trust this? | Short proof chain linked to advice | Interpretation anchors · capability reasoning |
| **Details** | Show me the analysis | Charts, tables, technical depth | Visualization · Knowledge |

Secondary beat (Commercial V1): **Promotion readiness** as a *milestone under Career Strategy* — after primary Actions, not instead of them.

---

## 4. Journey principles

1. **Advice before apparatus** — Actions precede Details.  
2. **One emotional arc** — trust → understanding → direction → action → proof.  
3. **No early calculator** — charts are confirmation, not opening.  
4. **Capability names are product** — customer should recognize Career Selection / Promotion Readiness as named value, without new routes.  
5. **Progressive depth** — Details invite expansion; they do not demand first read.

---

## 5. Anti-patterns (forbidden journey shapes)

| Anti-pattern | Why it fails |
|--------------|--------------|
| Chart → table → advice | Feels like a tool, not a consultant |
| Dual equal Recommendations | Customer freezes; commercial primary unclear |
| Strengths without Actions | Insight tourism |
| Actions without Who am I | Generic advice |
| Evidence wall before Career direction | Proof without destination |

---

## 6. Mapping to PACK_06 order (constraint)

PACK_06 row order remains frozen:

`Context → Executive Summary → Core Analysis → Charts → Recommendations → Interpretation → Knowledge`

Product Experience must **realize the commercial journey inside this constraint** via:

- Exec carrying Who am I + Strengths/Challenges seed + Career direction  
- Recommendation carrying Actions (Career primary)  
- Analysis/Interpretation carrying Evidence  
- Charts/Knowledge treated as Details (Priority 3)

Where frozen order conflicts with ideal beats (Charts before Recommendations), Presentation polish must **de-emphasize** Charts (density, collapse, quieter hierarchy) so Actions still *feel* primary — without reordering zones.

---

## 7. Success criteria

- Customer can narrate the seven beats after one pass.  
- Career direction is unmistakable before deep charts.  
- Actions are reachable without hunting.

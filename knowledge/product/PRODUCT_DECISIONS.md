This register records frozen product decisions.
It is not a changelog of code.

Status **FROZEN** means reversal requires a new Product Owner decision.

---

## PD-001 — Narrative Composer adopted

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Date | 2026-08-16 |
| Status | **FROZEN** |
| Reason | Customer sentences must be composed once, from Decision, State, Relationship, and Knowledge. A second composer would split the product voice. |
| Impact | Narrative Composer V2 is the production composer. Compatibility fallback is not a second product. |

---

## PD-002 — Published Narrative adopted

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Date | 2026-08-16 |
| Status | **FROZEN** |
| Reason | Not every composed sentence may leave the consultation. Publication policy (admit, drop, appendix) is a product control, not an editorial afterthought. |
| Impact | Published Narrative sits after composition and before edition. Glossary and unmatched catalogues do not enter the consultation by default. |

---

## PD-003 — Professional Publisher adopted

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Date | 2026-08-17 |
| Status | **FROZEN** |
| Reason | Depth of reading is an edition of the same truth, not a second analysis. Customers buy Executive or Professional consultation, not a different chart. |
| Impact | Editions are executive (default), professional, and appendix. No fourth edition without Product Owner approval. Publishing may not compose new sentences or recalculate truth. |

---

## PD-004 — Editorial Standard adopted

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Date | 2026-08-16 |
| Status | **FROZEN** |
| Reason | Technical correctness does not decide whether a sentence may reach a paying customer. That question has one constitution: Editorial Standard V1. |
| Impact | ES-V1 governs customer-facing language. Editorial FAIL blocks release regardless of tests. |

---

## PD-005 — Artifact First adopted

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Date | 2026-08-17 |
| Status | **FROZEN** |
| Reason | Completion reports and passing tests have repeatedly been mistaken for product completion. The customer receives a PDF and a consultation, not a memorandum. |
| Impact | Done requires Artifact → Editorial Review → Product Review → Product Owner approval. |

---

## PD-006 — No framework expansion during Beta

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Date | 2026-08-17 |
| Status | **FROZEN** |
| Reason | Beta must improve the consultation on a frozen platform. New engines, frameworks, matrices, publishers, composers, canons, layers, or runtime components would restart platform design. |
| Impact | Those additions require Product Owner approval before work starts. Allowed Beta work is Bug, Editorial, Knowledge, Engine (within owner), and Product improvement. |

---

## PD-007 — One source of analytical truth

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Date | 2026-08-09 |
| Status | **FROZEN** |
| Reason | Dual calculation produces dual consultations. The product cannot be trusted if narrative or PDF can invent a different chart. |
| Impact | Each analytical domain has one owner. Downstream layers copy, select, or refuse. They do not recalculate. |

---

## PD-008 — Golden Dataset is real cases only

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Date | 2026-08-16 |
| Status | **FROZEN** |
| Reason | Synthetic and anonymous fixtures cannot prove a commercial consultation. Product quality is judged on named, bound, real charts. |
| Impact | Placeholders are not cases. Synthetic subjects are excluded. CASE-0001 is the laboratory golden. Editorial cases EV-0001–EV-0010 are the current validated production set. |

---

## PD-009 — Default production edition is Executive

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Date | 2026-08-17 |
| Status | **FROZEN** |
| Reason | The commercial default must be the readable consultation, not the longest one. Professional is an edition, not the unmarked path. |
| Impact | Production remains Executive unless the request explicitly selects another approved edition. |

---

## PD-010 — Consultant product, not calculator product

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Date | 2026-08-08 |
| Status | **FROZEN** |
| Reason | Commercial packaging fails if the customer is sold modules, scores, or engine access. |
| Impact | Packaging, reports, and portal must read as consultation. Engine language in customer prose is a release failure. |

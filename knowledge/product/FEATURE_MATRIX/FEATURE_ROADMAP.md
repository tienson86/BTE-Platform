---

## Principle

Production waves exist to unlock **customer questions**, not to “finish engines.”

Technical dependency is respected (cannot interpret without engine facts), but **sequence follows willingness-to-pay**.

---

## Why this order (value rationale)

1. **Ten Gods first (W01)** — Highest density of “how I operate / pressure / roles / relationships / career style” questions after basic strength exists. Wave already started.
2. **Pattern second (W02)** — Answers “how is my life organized / long path” — completes identity triangle with Strength + Ten Gods.
3. **Useful God third (W03)** — Converts understanding into **what to do** (balance) — critical for paid actionability.
4. **Executive depth (W04)** — Packaging is a product feature (Q47–Q48); do after domain claims exist.
5. **Strength freeze (W05)** — Engine/composition exists; freeze knowledge raises commercial quality of entire spine.
6. **Luck (W06)** — Extremely high willingness-to-pay but must wait for honest publish + interpretation; do not block MVP.
7. **ShenSha (W07)** — Sticky but narrower question set.
8. **Fine timing (W08)** — Differentiator after timeline trust is earned.

---

## Anti-patterns

| Anti-pattern | Why rejected |
|--------------|--------------|
| Wave by “easiest engine left” | Ignores customer value |
| Ship Luck before Useful God | Timing without balance advice under-delivers actionability |
| Executive-only without domains | Empty packaging |
| ShenSha before Ten Gods | External color without operating system |

## Package A — Quick Insight

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Need | Fast self-recognition |

| Not for | Full career strategy, life timeline, advisor tooling |

- Identity Report (Feature 01)

## Package C — Master Consulting

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|

| Core questions | Who am I? What should I prioritize? What should I avoid? |

| Not for | Professional advisors needing validation traces |

**Includes:**

- Identity Report

**Excludes:**

## Package D — Advisor Edition

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Need | Professionals who advise others |
| Core questions | What can I use with clients — and what is evidence-bound? |
| Promise | Customer deliverables **plus** validation-oriented views for responsible advising |
| Not for | Casual first-time curiosity buyers |

**Includes:**

- Everything in Master Consulting (customer-safe outputs)
- Advisor viewing mode: structure of claims, missing-data, domain availability
- Re-run / determinism assurance for client cases
- Export suitable for client handoff + advisor notes workspace (product intent)

**Excludes:**

- Exposing raw rule IDs / engine dumps to the end client
- Replacing human professional judgment
- Unlimited multi-client enterprise terms (commercial ops later)

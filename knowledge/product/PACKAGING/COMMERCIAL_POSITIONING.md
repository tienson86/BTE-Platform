## Why the line exists

BTE is a **consulting product**, not a feature checklist.

Customers buy clarity at different depths. Packaging must match **depth of need**, not number of engines.

---

## Why each package exists

| Package | Exists because… | Competes against… | Wins when… |
|---------|------------------|-------------------|------------|
| **A Quick Insight** | Many people only need recognition first | Free chart sites / personality quizzes | Clearer, calmer, more honest “this is me” |
| **B Professional Consulting** | Work decisions drive willingness to pay | Generic career tests + astrology job lists | Direction + environment + risks + actions without fake timing |
| **C Master Consulting** | Buyers want one consultant, one story | Multi-module “report packs” | Integrated voice, priorities, trust |
| **D Advisor Edition** | Practitioners need client-ready + responsible depth | DIY tools that overclaim | Customer clarity + advisor-safe limits |

---

## Positioning statements

**A:** The fastest credible answer to “Who am I?”  

**B:** The practical answer to “Where should my career go?” grounded in who you are.  

**C:** The full BTE consulting experience — for people who want guidance, not modules.  

**D:** BTE for professionals who advise — clarity for clients, structure for advisors.

---

## Brand constraint

Every package must feel like a **consultant product**.

If a package looks like “unlock more engines,” packaging has failed.

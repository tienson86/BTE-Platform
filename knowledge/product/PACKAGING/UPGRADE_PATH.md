---

## Primary ladder

```
A Quick Insight
      ↓  (asks about work / next steps)
B Professional Consulting
      ↓  (wants full priorities & one consultant voice)
C Master Consulting
      ↓  (advises others / needs client practice tools)
D Advisor Edition
```

---

## Upgrade triggers

| From → To | Customer signal | Offer |
|-----------|-----------------|-------|
| A → B | “What job fits me?” / “Should I change roles?” | Add Career Report; keep Identity |
| B → C | “I want the full conclusion / priorities” | Add Executive + Master synthesis + full PDF |
| C → D | “I use this with clients” / practice use | Unlock Advisor View + notes export |
| A → C | High intent life consulting (skip B) | Allowed if buyer wants full package immediately |
| B → D | Rare; prefer B→C→D unless already a practitioner | Soft-gate with segment check |

---

## Upgrade principles

1. **Credit prior purchase** — Identity from A carries into B/C; don’t force rebuy of same feature.  
2. **No fake scarcity** — don’t hide honesty/limits behind upgrades.  
3. **Preserve completeness** — upgraded package should feel additive, not “you were tricked.”  
4. **One-click narrative** — “Continue to Career Direction” / “Unlock Full Consulting.”

---

## Downgrade / re-purchase

- Re-runs of same package: product trust (determinism), not forced upsell.  
- Downgrade not required; archives remain according to commercial ops policy (later).

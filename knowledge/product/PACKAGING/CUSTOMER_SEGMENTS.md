## Segment overview

---

## Segment detail

### Casual user

- Trigger: “I want to understand myself quickly.”
- Fear: jargon, long reports, being sold a fortune-telling forecast
- Success: finishes Identity Report; can answer “Who am I?”
- Upgrade cue: asks about work / what to do next → B

### Career seeker

- Trigger: role change, burnout at work, leadership vs specialist doubt
- Fear: vague personality fluff; fake “lucky year to quit”
- Success: clear environment fit, risks, actions this month
- Upgrade cue: wants full priorities / life framing → C

### Life consulting buyer

- Trigger: “I want one serious report, not modules.”
- Fear: calculator feel; incomplete honesty
- Success: one consultant voice; priorities + avoids; PDF they keep
- Upgrade cue: uses reports with clients / practice → D

### Professional consultant

- Trigger: needs consistent, explainable client materials
- Fear: black-box claims; overclaim risk to their reputation
- Success: customer-safe PDF + advisor visibility into limits/availability
- Upgrade cue: team / multi-seat (future commercial ops)

## Inclusion matrix

| Feature | A Quick Insight | B Professional | C Master Consulting | D Advisor Edition |
|---------|-----------------|----------------|---------------------|-------------------|
| Identity | **Included** | Included | Included | Included |
| Career | — | **Included** | Included | Included |

| Master | — | — | **Included** (customer synthesis) | Included + advisor framing |
| Honesty / limits | Included | Included | Included | Included + advisor visibility |
| Validation mode | — | — | — | **Included** (advisor only) |

Legend: **Included** = part of package promise · — = not in package · optional = may appear as teaser, not sold as full feature.

---

## Mapping rules

1. A never pretends to be Career or Master.  
2. B must include Identity + Career as first-class deliverables.  
3. C’s differentiator is **Executive + integrated Master consulting voice**, not more jargon.  
4. D never replaces customer-safe outputs with internals; internals stay advisor-side.

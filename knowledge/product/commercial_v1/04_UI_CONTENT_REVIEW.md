## 2. Content surfaces

### Summary / Executive

- Adapter preference: Career direction → else Promotion readiness → else narrative identity.  
- Risk: Exec becomes a long commercial paragraph, not a crisp identity + one insight.  
- Risk: Promotion appended into strength reasoning upstream → even denser narrative before Portal.

### Recommendation / Actions

- Adapter preference: **Promotion plan → Career plan → narrative recommendations**.  
- Primary next-action ownership is Promotion-biased on default production path.  
- No visible “Capability: Promotion Readiness” label in UI content model.

### Strengths / Warnings

- Career strengths/risks preferred; Promotion used as fallback.  
- When Career fires, Promotion risks may not appear in the same warning list — asymmetric visibility.

### Analysis / Visualization

- Still chart-truth oriented — correct analytically.  
- Experience tension: commercial Exec above calculator-like analysis without bridging sentence.

### Knowledge / End

- Knowledge zone can reintroduce technical vocabulary.  
- End-of-report lacks a strong “your next conversation / revisit in 90 days” commercial close (content gap).

## 1. Journey map

| Step | Surface | Customer question | Audit finding |
|------|---------|-------------------|---------------|
| 1 Analyze | Input / submit | “Will this understand me?” | Out of Result polish scope; assume analyze succeeds |
| 2 Loading | Status gate | “Is something happening?” | Skeleton + “Đang tải kết quả…” — functional; not consultant-framed |
| 3 Executive Summary | Context + Summary zones | “Who am I / what matters?” | Often filled with **Career direction** (or Promotion readiness fallback); may not feel like a calm Exec opener |
| 4 Analysis | Analysis zone | “What is the chart saying?” | Analytical cards remain; risk of calculator feel if Exec already commercial-dense |
| 5 Visualization | Visualization zone | “Can I see structure?” | Supports trust if Exec was clear; weak if Exec was jargon-heavy |
| 6 Recommendations | Recommendation zone | “What should I do next?” | **Promotion 90-day plan preferred** on production path when both caps fire |
| 7 Career Selection | (no dedicated zone) | “What about my career path?” | Projected into Exec / strengths / actions — **easy to miss as a product** |
| 8 Promotion Readiness | (no dedicated zone) | “Am I ready to advance?” | Same slot competition; Rec favors Promotion |
| 9 Knowledge | Interpretation + Knowledge | “Why should I trust this?” | Depth available; may feel technical / long after dense Exec+Rec |
| 10 End of Report | Footer / knowledge | “What now / who produced this?” | Continuity / next-step framing not audited as a strong close |

## 2. Trust → Understanding → Action

| Principle | Observed |
|-----------|----------|
| Trust | Analytical zones + ethics hedges help; unlabeled dual capabilities hurt trust (“which advice is for me?”) |
| Understanding | Units explain well in isolation; merge reduces understanding |
| Action | 90-day plans are actionable in content; **wrong plan may win** for career-intent users |
| Reflection | Weak — journey ends without a clear “keep / revisit” consulting close |

## 3. First-time customer walkthrough (synthetic)

**Persona:** First-time customer, career questions, strong chart, useful god present.

1. Loading — OK, generic.  
2. Opens Summary — sees career-family language with Dụng thần / pattern labels — may need glossary.  
3. Skims Analysis / charts — feels more “engine.”  
4. Hits Recommendation — receives **Promotion** 90-day plan (not Career Selection plan).  
5. Never sees a titled “Career Selection Assessment” or “Promotion Readiness Assessment” block.  
6. Leaves unsure whether the product answered career selection or promotion.

**Result:** Journey fails the “find next action for *my* intent” test.

---

## 4. Scenario notes

### Strong

- Both capabilities complete → maximum density.  
- Highest collision risk.

### Weak

- Mitigate-first language present in plans — good.  
- Readiness still offers tiến/chuẩn bị/trì hoãn — needs sharper “prepare first” emphasis in *experience* (content orchestration, not new Domain).

### Mixed

- Strengths + risks both visible — good honesty.  
- Risk of warning fatigue if career + promotion risks both surface.

### Career-oriented

- Direction may appear in Exec.  
- Primary Rec often **not** career plan → **P0**.

### Promotion-oriented

- Rec alignment better.  
- Still lacks named product framing and clean separation from Career.

This is the official product vision for BTE V1.
It does not describe implementation.

---

## 1. Mission

BTE exists so a person can understand themselves clearly enough to decide.

Traditional BaZi is a serious analytical tradition.
Most digital presentations either mystify it or dump it.

BTE’s mission is to turn that tradition into a **professional consultation**: structured, explainable, bounded, and usable.

BTE does not exist to predict fate.
BTE exists to help a person see pattern, evaluate options, and act with more honesty.

---

## 2. Target customer

Primary customer: an adult who must make a consequential life or work decision and wants a consultant-grade reading of their chart — not entertainment and not an astrology class.

Secondary customer: a practitioner who advises others and needs a client-ready consultation that does not dump internals onto the client.

The product is not designed for:

- users seeking guaranteed luck, titles, income, or medical outcomes
- users seeking a quiz or novelty chart
- internal engineering as the audience of the report

---

## 3. Commercial positioning

BTE is a **consulting product**.

Customers pay for depth of understanding, not for the number of analytical modules.

The commercial line is:

| Offer | Customer need |
|-------|----------------|
| Quick Insight | Recognition: who am I, without noise |
| Professional Consulting | Direction: how to steer work from that identity |
| Master Consulting | One consultation, one story, priorities and conclusion |
| Advisor Edition | Client clarity plus the structure a practitioner needs to advise responsibly |

If a package looks like “unlock more calculation,” positioning has failed.

---

## 4. Core value

The customer should leave with three things:

1. **Recognition** — a truthful picture of operating style, not a label dump.
2. **Understanding** — why that picture holds, in language a non-specialist can follow.
3. **Action** — a small number of ranked, non-duplicated recommendations that respect the customer’s ability to decide.

Value is measured by quality of understanding, not by volume of content.

---

## 5. Long-term vision

BTE V1 establishes a trusted professional consultation on a frozen analytical foundation.

Over time, BTE should remain the same product in identity:

- consultant, not calculator
- explanation before recommendation
- tradition respected, not simplified into slogan
- the customer decides; BTE informs

Later versions may deepen domains and consultation quality.
They must not replace consultation with a larger glossary, a new platform, or a second source of truth.

---

## 6. Success definition

BTE V1 succeeds when all of the following are true:

- A named real case produces a consultation a paying customer can read without translation.
- Analytical truth is stable across reruns of the same birth data.
- Editorial Standard admits the prose.
- Commercial review accepts the artifact as consultant-grade.
- The Product Owner signs the release.
- The customer can answer: who I am, what this means for me now, what I should do next.

Tests passing, documents complete, and engines correct are necessary conditions.
They are not success.

---

## 7. Out of scope

Out of scope for this vision, and for V1 Beta:

- Fortune-telling, entertainment astrology, or superstition as a product category
- Guaranteed dates, income, titles, fertility, legal, or medical outcomes
- Selling engine internals, score dumps, or encyclopedias as the consultation
- Synthetic charts as proof of commercial quality
- New analytical or narrative systems during Beta without Product Owner approval
- Replacing human judgment with automated destiny

If a request sits in this list, it is not a BTE V1 product request.

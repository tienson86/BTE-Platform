---

## Current position

BTE V1 is in **Beta0**: architecture, analytical truth, narrative, publishing, editorial ownership, and Golden Dataset are frozen.

The product job from here is to make the consultation commercially acceptable.
It is not to redesign the platform.

---

## Phases

### Research

The product asks what a professional consultation must be.

Outcome: identity, principles, and refusal list.
Not a shippable consultation.

### Development

The product can produce a full reading from real birth data.

Outcome: a working consultation path exists.
Not permission to sell.

### Beta0

The product locks the platform so later work cannot quietly become a new product.

Outcome: frozen architecture, frozen truth, frozen narrative and publishing path, frozen Golden Dataset, Artifact First as official Done.
Not customer-ready by itself.

Editorial baseline at freeze: named real cases exist; none are approved for paying customers.

### Beta

The product improves the consultation on the frozen platform.

Outcome: Executive and Professional artifacts on real cases that can pass editorial and commercial review.
Still not general sale.

Allowed: defect repair, editorial quality, knowledge quality, engine correctness inside frozen owners, consultation quality.

Forbidden without Product Owner approval: new product systems.

### Release Candidate

The product is the intended commercial consultation.

Outcome: Golden Dataset regenerated, Executive and Professional PDFs reviewed, editorial PASS, commercial PASS, Product Owner ready to approve a production candidate.

Human consulting review of named cases is required.
A laboratory score is not enough.

### Production

The product is sold.

Outcome: paying customers receive the approved consultation.
Change is controlled.
Regression is artifact-first.

### Post-release

The product earns trust in use.

Outcome: defects and editorial drift are caught on real artifacts.
Packaging may deepen (insight → professional → master → advisor) without changing what BTE is.

No new analytical identity.
No glossary expansion sold as “more consulting.”

### Future V2

V2 is a later product generation, not a Beta escape hatch.

Outcome, if ever authorised: deeper consultation quality and domain coverage on a **new** product decision, with explicit Product Owner approval.

V2 is not:

- a new engine invented during V1 Beta
- a second narrative system
- a rewrite of frozen V1 truth

Until that decision exists, V2 is out of scope.

---

## Evolution the customer should feel

| From | To |
|------|----|
| Calculated chart | Recognised person |
| Complete dump | Readable consultation |
| Many recommendations | Ranked, non-duplicated advice |
| Engine language | Consultant language |
| Report that exists | Report a customer would pay for |

If a phase does not move the product along this line, it is not a product phase.
It is platform work, and it requires a separate decision.

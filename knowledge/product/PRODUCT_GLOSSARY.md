These terms are product vocabulary.
They describe what the customer and the company mean.
They are not implementation names.

---

## Artifact

The customer-facing object under review: usually a PDF consultation, sometimes the live consultation surface.
An internal memorandum is not an artifact.

## Case

One real person with bound birth data, engine truth, consultation artifact, editorial review, and (where published) commercial score.
A placeholder slot is not a case.
A synthetic subject is not a case.

## Consultation

The product the customer buys: a professional reading that helps them recognise themselves, understand, and decide.
Not a textbook. Not a calculation printout.

## Domain

A life area the consultation may speak to (for example identity, career, finance), only when the reading has something specific to say.
A domain is not a licence to reprint the whole chart.

## Editorial

The discipline that decides whether a sentence may reach a paying customer.
Editorial does not decide the chart.
Editorial does not own the platform.

## Executive Report

The default consultation edition: short, readable, sufficient for recognition, understanding, and ranked action.
Not a summary of a longer hidden report unless that longer report was actually produced as Professional.

## Golden Dataset

The frozen set of real cases used to judge whether the product still is the product.
Quality is judged on these consultations, not on test counts alone.

## Impact

What the reading means for how the person operates now.
Impact is not a prediction of events.
Impact is not a restatement of a score.

## Knowledge

Admissible traditional and domain content that may inform the reading.
Knowledge is not customer prose.
Knowledge is not a second chart.

## Narrative

The composed consultation language produced from truth, state, relationship, and knowledge.
Narrative explains.
Narrative does not calculate.

## Professional Report

The deeper consultation edition of the **same** truth: more already-composed evidence, still a reading, still not an encyclopedia.
Not a different analysis.
Not the unmarked default.

## Published Narrative

The consultation after publication policy has admitted, dropped, or sent material to appendix.
What is published is what the customer may see in that edition.

## Reasoning

Why the consultation says what it says, in customer language.
Reasoning is not a trace of internal steps.
Reasoning is not a glossary entry.

## Recommendation

A ranked, specific next action the person can take or refuse.
Recommendations do not repeat.
Recommendations do not replace the customer’s decision.

## Truth

The analytical facts of the chart, owned once, upstream.
Truth is copied into the consultation.
Truth is not rewritten to make prose easier.

## Additional product terms

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Customer** | The person who receives the consultation, or the practitioner receiving Advisor Edition for a client |
| **Product Owner** | The role that may freeze, accept, and release |
| **Release** | Permission for a build to leave the company at a stated state |
| **Beta0** | The freeze in which V1 stopped being a platform project and became a product-stabilization project |
| **Customer-ready** | Product Owner claim after acceptance gates. Not a laboratory label. |

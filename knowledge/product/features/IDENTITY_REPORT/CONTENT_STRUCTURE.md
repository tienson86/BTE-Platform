## Section contract

| # | Title | Customer job | Primary sources (interpretation) |
|---|-------|--------------|----------------------------------|
| 1 | Who you are | Name the identity in plain language | Strength + Structure + Consulting |
| 2 | Your natural operating style | How you run day to day | Ten Gods + Consulting |
| 3 | Your greatest strengths | What to lean on | Strength + Ten Gods + Structure |
| 4 | Your biggest blind spots | What usually costs you | Ten Gods + Balance + Consulting |
| 5 | How you respond under pressure | Stress signature | Strength + Ten Gods |
| 6 | What environment helps you thrive | Fit / context | Structure + Ten Gods + Balance |
| 7 | Your most important life lesson | One memorable insight | Consulting + Balance |
| 8 | Three practical actions | Start today / this week | Balance + Consulting |
| 9 | Identity Summary | 5–7 sentences, portable | All (compressed) |

## Length guidance

| Section | Target |
|---------|--------|
| 1–7 | 1–3 short paragraphs each |
| 8 | Exactly 3 actions |
| 9 | 5–7 sentences only |

## Value rule

Each section must add **new** customer value. No repeating Section 1 inside Section 3 with synonyms.

## Terminology policy

Prefer life language:

| Prefer | Avoid unless necessary |
|--------|------------------------|
| endurance, responsibility, standards, output | Thân Vượng, Thất Sát, Chính Ấn, Thực Thần as labels |
| long-term frame | Cách cục jargon |
| release pressure through creation | “tiết khí” without explanation |

If a traditional term appears, it must be immediately translated into lived meaning.

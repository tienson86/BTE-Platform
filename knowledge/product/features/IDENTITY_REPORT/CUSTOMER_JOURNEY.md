## Journey map

```
Enter birth data
      ↓
Click Analyze
      ↓
Receive Identity Report (Feature 01)
      ↓
Recognize self (Who am I?)
      ↓
See strengths + blind spots
      ↓
Know environment fit
      ↓
Take 3 practical actions
      ↓
(Optional later) Full Core Consulting / Timeline packages
```

## Emotional arc

| Stage | Customer feeling | Report job |
|-------|------------------|------------|
| Open | “Is this about me or a template?” | Section 1 lands personally |
| Middle | “I see my pattern” | Sections 2–6 create recognition |
| Insight | “That explains it” | Section 7 — life lesson |
| Close | “I know what to do” | Sections 8–9 — actions + summary |

## Trust rules in journey

- Never open with scores or jargon.
- Never hide limits (e.g. no full life timeline claimed here).
- Never flatten the person into “strong = good.”

## Exit promise

After reading only Identity Report, customer should feel:

> I understand who I am in practice — and I know what to start this week.

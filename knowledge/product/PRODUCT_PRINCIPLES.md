These principles are frozen for BTE V1.
When a feature, sentence, or release conflicts with a principle, the principle wins.

---

## 1. Truth before prose

Analytical truth is decided upstream.
Prose may explain it.
Prose may not invent, soften, or replace it.

If the chart and the sentence disagree, the sentence is wrong.

---

## 2. Artifact First

Work is not finished when a report of work exists.
Work is finished when a customer-facing artifact exists, has been reviewed, and has been approved.

A Completion Report is not an artifact.
A test log is not an artifact.

---

## 3. Customer before implementation

The first question is what the customer receives.
The second question is how the platform produces it.

Implementation elegance that does not improve the consultation is not a product improvement.

---

## 4. One source of truth

Each fact has one owner.
Calendar, chart, strength, pattern, useful god, ten gods, shen sha matches, luck, temperature, and five elements are not recalculated in narrative, publishing, or the PDF.

If two layers can produce the same fact, the product has a defect.

---

## 5. Editorial before expansion

No new page, domain, or edition expands until existing customer language meets Editorial Standard.

Volume is not progress.
Admission is progress.

---

## 6. Commercial value over technical elegance

Ship the consultation a customer will pay for.
Do not ship a more complete machine that the customer cannot use.

Technical quality is required.
It is not the product.

---

## 7. Professional consultation over glossary

The customer buys a reading, not a textbook.

Glossary, encyclopedia, and unmatched catalogues belong in appendix or advisor material.
They do not belong in the consultation.

---

## 8. Do not duplicate knowledge

The same meaning is said once.
Recommendations are not restated across pages.
Identity is not reprinted as career, then as finance, then as summary.

If two paragraphs teach the same point, one is waste.

---

## 9. Readable before complete

A shorter true consultation beats a complete unreadable one.

Completeness is a knowledge and architecture concern.
Readability is a product concern.
Product wins the customer-facing conflict.

---

## Standing application

These principles apply to features, releases, editorial decisions, and commercial packaging.
They do not require a new system to be followed.
They require refusal when a proposal violates them.

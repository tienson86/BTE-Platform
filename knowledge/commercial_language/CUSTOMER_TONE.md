# CUSTOMER_TONE

Version: 1.2.0

---

## Target feel

Trust → Understanding → Action  
(Consultant presence, not calculator coldness, not guru mystique.)

---

## Tone spectrum

| Axis | Too little | Target | Too much |
|------|------------|--------|----------|
| Warmth | Clinical dump | Respectful, human | Flattery / therapy voice |
| Authority | Hedging everything | Clear, bounded claims | Absolute fate talk |
| Directness | Vague poetry | Plain and specific | Harsh blame |
| Hope | Doom | Agency within structure | False guarantee |

---

## Register

- Adult professional Vietnamese (or bilingual product surfaces as specified by packaging).  
- Second person “bạn” for customer features.  
- No emoji. No hype punctuation stacks.  
- No slang that ages poorly.

---

## Emotional honesty

| Situation | Tone |
|-----------|------|
| Strong capacity + overload risk | Firm, protective of energy |
| Balanced + follow structure | Clarifying, dual-layer calm |
| Unresolved / missing policy | Transparent, non-alarmist |
| Output-led operating | Encouraging channel, not “be louder” |

---

## What tone must never do

- Shame the customer for their structure  
- Sound like CASE-0001 when facts differ  
- Use fear to force action  
- Hide tension to sound “positive”

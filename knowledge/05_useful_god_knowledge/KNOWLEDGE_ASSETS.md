# 4. Dependency on Fundamental Knowledge

Useful God Knowledge references, and does not redefine:

- Yin Yang
- Wu Xing
- Heavenly Stems
- Earthly Branches
- Hidden Stems
- Ten Gods relationship identities
- Season / Climate definition frames where fundamental

# 5. Upstream Analytical Dependencies

Useful God Knowledge may declare dependency concepts on published:

- Strength classifications
- Temperature / climate classifications
- Pattern identities

These are evidence dependencies, not ownership of those domains.

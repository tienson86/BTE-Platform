# Executive Summary Standard

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Document | EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY_STANDARD |
| Pack | PACK-01 Strength |
| Version | 1.0.0 |
| Status | DESIGN ONLY |

---

# 1. Purpose

The Executive Summary is the customer’s memorable close for Strength.

It is 5 to 8 lines only.

It is not a second report.

It is not a dump of all nine sections.

---

# 2. Position

Authored last.

May be displayed first in Report / Portal.

Same text in both places.

Never two different summaries.

---

# 3. Length

| Rule | Value |
|------|-------|
| Minimum | 5 lines |
| Maximum | 8 lines |
| Line | One sentence, or two very short clauses that still form one idea |
| Paragraphs | One block |

If it needs 12 lines, it is not a summary.

If it has 3 lines, it is not complete.

---

# 4. Mandatory Line Jobs

The 5–8 lines MUST cover these jobs. Some jobs may share a line only if still clear.

| Job | Customer question |
|-----|-------------------|
| Standing | What is my Strength class? |
| Why (compressed) | Why is that true? |
| So what | What does it mean for me? |
| Cost | What should I watch? |
| Do | What should I do? |
| Avoid | What should I avoid? |
| Time (if luck present) | How does luck change this? |
| Close | What is the takeaway? |

If luck data is missing, omit the luck job rather than invent it. Stay within 5–8 lines with the remaining jobs.

---

# 5. Source Rule

Every summary line MUST be a compression of an earlier Customer Mode section.

Forbidden:

- new classification
- new domain claim
- new luck event
- new recommendation not already given
- any Validation Mode field

The summary is a lossless-meaning, lossy-detail projection of Mode B.

---

# 6. Voice

Consultant. Calm. Direct.

Not a teaser.

Not a cliffhanger.

Not a slogan.

Wrong:

> Discover the secret of your Strong Metal destiny.

Wrong:

> Strength level: strong. Confidence: 92%. Rules: 7.

Better shape (illustrative, not a template):

> Nhật chủ của bạn thuộc nhóm Thân Vượng.
> Bạn có lực để gánh việc, vì được nâng đỡ từ mùa và gốc.
> Điều này giúp bạn chịu tải và dẫn dắt, nhưng dễ ít lắng nghe khi bị góp ý.
> Hãy dùng sức bền vào việc đúng nhịp, và chừa chỗ cho phản hồi.
> Tránh lấy nội lực làm lý do bỏ qua dấu hiệu mệt hoặc ý kiến khác.
> Khi vận nâng thêm, hãy kiểm soát việc nhận quá nhiều.
> Điểm then chốt: mạnh không có nghĩa là không cần điều chỉnh.

That block is an example of **jobs**, not production copy for every Strong chart.

---

# 7. Anti-Repetition

The summary may restates standing once.

It must not:

- repeat Advantages list in full
- repeat every life domain
- repeat the whole Why paragraph

One compressed Why line is enough.

---

# 8. Leak Ban

The Executive Summary is Customer Mode.

All Customer Mode leak bans apply.

Especially forbidden here because summaries attract metric dumps:

- percentages
- scores
- rule counts
- “high confidence”
- engine names

---

# 9. Insufficient Data

If natal class itself cannot be mapped, the summary is an honest incomplete block, still 5–8 lines, stating:

- what cannot be concluded
- which Strength sections remain usable
- what data would complete the reading
- no invented class

Do not pad with generic personality text.

---

# 10. Future Pack Reuse

Every later pack’s Executive Summary MUST be 5–8 lines, cover the Question Framework, add no new claims, and leak nothing.

A platform-level combined summary (all packs) is a later composition problem.

This pack only defines the Strength summary.

A combined report must not mash Strength, Pattern, and Useful God into one 8-line block that invents a new conclusion.

---

---

# 1. Purpose

Customer Mode is the commercial Strength interpretation.

It must read like a senior consultant speaking to one person.

It must never read like a calculator, a rule engine, or a glossary.

---

# 2. Commercial Promise

After reading Strength Customer Mode, the customer must know:

1. Where their Day Master stands (Strong / Weak / Balanced / Very Strong / Very Weak) in human language
2. Why that is true for **this** chart
3. What that means in lived life
4. What it helps
5. What it costs
6. How it shows up in career, wealth, marriage, health, personality, learning, leadership, and decision making
7. How luck cycles can support or weaken that standing
8. What to do and what to avoid
9. A short takeaway they can remember

If the text only restates “You are Strong”, it has failed.

---

# 3. Required Sections

Customer Mode contains exactly these nine sections, in this order.

Executive Summary is last in the authored body so it can synthesize without adding new claims.

Report / Portal may visually place a short summary first. That display copy must still be the same Executive Summary, not a second invented summary.

---

## 3.1 Conclusion

State the class in natural language.

Allowed pattern:

> Nhật chủ của bạn thuộc nhóm Thân Vượng.

or

> Your Day Master belongs to the Strong category.

Rules:

- One class. No hedging into a second class unless Alternative Analysis in Mode A is so close that honesty requires a bounded qualifier — and even then, do not print percentages.
- Do not dump the enum `strong`.
- Do not add Rule IDs.
- Do not explain the entire chart here.

This section answers: **What is the standing?**

It does not yet answer So What.

---

## 3.2 Why

Explain WHY, in natural language, using this person’s chart structure.

Use consultant language for the dimensions that actually fired:

- seasonal command
- rooting
- visible support
- hidden support
- drain / control
- special structure when relevant

Do not list Rule IDs.

Do not recite a textbook of Đắc Lệnh.

Wrong:

> You have Strong Metal.

Still wrong:

> STR-000001 activated because month command is prosperous.

Better:

> Bạn được mùa và có gốc nâng đỡ, nên Nhật chủ không phải đang chống đỡ một mình. Đó là lý do sức mạnh này đứng vững, chứ không phải vì một yếu tố lẻ.

This section answers: **WHY?**

---

## 3.3 Meaning

Explain what this class means **for this person**.

Not the dictionary definition of Thân Vượng.

Wrong:

> Thân Vượng means the Day Master is strong.

Better:

> Với cấu trúc này, bạn thường vào việc với nội lực sẵn có. Người khác thấy bạn chịu được áp lực, nhưng chính bạn dễ chủ quan rằng mình không cần điều chỉnh.

This section answers: **SO WHAT?** at the identity level.

---

## 3.4 Advantages

What this standing helps the person do well.

Each advantage must be:

- caused by this Strength class in this chart
- useful in life
- not a repeat of Conclusion or Why

Typical Strength advantage families (use only those evidenced):

| Class | Advantage family (illustrative) |
|-------|----------------------------------|
| Very Strong / Strong | Endurance, initiative, leadership presence, recovery after setback |
| Balanced | Adaptability, mediation, less swing between overreach and collapse |
| Weak / Very Weak | Sensitivity to environment, willingness to collaborate, ability to grow through support |

Do not invent talents the chart does not support.

Do not praise for flattery.

---

## 3.5 Challenges

What this standing makes harder.

Each challenge must create value the Advantages section did not already give.

Typical challenge families (use only those evidenced):

| Class | Challenge family (illustrative) |
|-------|----------------------------------|
| Very Strong / Strong | Over-control, low receptivity to criticism, forcing timing |
| Balanced | Indecision if waiting for perfect equilibrium |
| Weak / Very Weak | Depletion in unsupported environments, over-accommodation, delayed self-assertion |

Challenges are not moral defects.

Challenges are operating costs of the structure.

---

## 3.6 Influence

How Strength shows up in life domains.

Required domain slots for PACK-01:

1. Career
2. Wealth
3. Marriage
4. Health
5. Personality
6. Learning
7. Leadership
8. Decision making

Rules:

- Each domain gets **new** information.
- Do not copy Advantages into Career and call it done.
- Do not define Ten Gods, Useful God, or Pattern here.
- If a domain cannot be supported by Strength evidence alone, say that this Strength reading cannot specify that domain — do not borrow Pattern/Useful God conclusions.
- Never print internal tokens.

Each domain answers: **HOW DOES IT AFFECT LIFE?** for that area.

Illustrative quality (not templates):

| Domain | Weak customer text | Better customer text |
|--------|--------------------|----------------------|
| Career | You have Strong Metal. | Bạn bền trong vai trò cần chịu tải. Môi trường thiếu phản hồi sẽ làm bạn đẩy quá đà. |
| Wealth | Strong people get rich. | Sức mạnh này giúp bạn theo đuổi mục tiêu tiền bạc, nhưng dễ giữ quá chặt hoặc mở quá rộng nếu không có nhịp dừng. |
| Marriage | Strong means dominant. | Bạn mang lực vào quan hệ. Điều đó tạo chỗ dựa, và cũng có thể làm đối phương thấy ít khoảng trống. |
| Health | Strong means healthy. | Nội lực tốt không có nghĩa là được phép bỏ qua dấu hiệu mệt. Thân vượng vẫn cạn nếu không có nhịp phục hồi. |

---

## 3.7 Influence during Luck Cycles

Explain how Strength **changes in experience** when luck supports or weakens the Day Master.

This section consumes Luck Engine facts when present.

It does not recompute luck.

It does not recompute strength.

It explains interaction:

```text
Natal Strength
    ×
Luck that supports the Day Master
    →
lived surplus / overdrive risk

Natal Strength
    ×
Luck that weakens the Day Master
    →
correction / depletion / needed support
```

If luck data is missing, this section uses Insufficient Data language.

It must not invent decade names, years, or outcomes.

Illustrative quality:

> Khi vận nâng Nhật chủ, bạn sẽ thấy sức bật tăng — đây là lúc dễ nhận trách nhiệm lớn, và cũng dễ nhận quá mức. Khi vận làm giảm lực, bạn không mất bản chất, nhưng sẽ cần chỗ dựa và nhịp chậm hơn.

---

## 3.8 Recommendations

Two mandatory halves:

1. What the person should do
2. What the person should avoid

Recommendations must follow from Meaning, Challenges, Influence, and Luck — not from a generic Strong/Weak slogan.

Rules:

- Actionable
- Respectful
- Non-absolute
- No fear sales
- No career command invented from Strength alone (“quit and become a sailor”)
- No guaranteed results

This section answers:

- **WHAT SHOULD THE CUSTOMER DO?**
- **WHAT SHOULD THE CUSTOMER AVOID?**

# 4. Reading Flow

Customer Mode follows the consulting conversation:

```text
Name the standing        → Conclusion
Explain why              → Why
Say what it means        → Meaning
Give the upside          → Advantages
Give the operating cost  → Challenges
Show life domains        → Influence
Show time                → Luck Cycles
Advise                   → Recommendations
Close                    → Executive Summary
```

Do not jump to advice before meaning.

Do not repeat the standing in every section.

---

# 5. Absolute Leak Ban

Customer Mode MUST NEVER contain:

| Banned | Examples |
|--------|----------|
| Rule IDs | `STR-000001`, `STR-018`, `RUL-…` |
| Confidence | `92%`, `confidence`, `canonical` |
| Scores | `0.72`, `season_score`, `strength_score` |
| Raw enums | `male`, `strong`, `success`, `hot`, `very_strong` as code |
| Dumps | `{'name':'...','index':23}` |
| Engine talk | Strength Engine, Pack 04, matcher, priority 105 |
| Developer talk | TODO, mock, placeholder, Validation Mode |
| Dictionary lectures | “Official Officer is a Ten God that means…” |

Natural-language class names are allowed:

- Thân Vượng / Strong category

Raw runtime tokens are not.

---

# 6. Insufficient Data in Customer Mode

When Mode A Missing Data blocks a section:

- Keep the section shell
- Speak honestly in customer language
- Do not speculate to look complete

Allowed pattern:

> Phần này chưa đủ dữ liệu để kết luận chắc về vận trình. Phần còn lại của luận giải thân vượng/nhược vẫn giữ nguyên.

Forbidden pattern:

> Dù thiếu giờ sinh, có thể khẳng định bạn sẽ thăng chức năm sau.

---

# 7. Strength-Specific Customer Rules

1. Do not treat Strong as morally better than Weak.
2. Do not treat Weak as misfortune.
3. Do not treat Balanced as “no personality”.
4. Do not collapse Very Strong into Strong if the engine class is Very Strong.
5. Do not use Useful God theory as a hidden second conclusion.
6. Do not say “you should use Water” unless Useful God interpretation (a later pack) has already published that fact **and** this Strength pack is only describing interaction — PACK-01 itself must not determine Useful God.
7. Gender, age, and similar runtime attributes may shape grammar, never appear as leaked tokens.

---

# 8. Length Guidance

| Section | Target |
|---------|--------|
| Conclusion | 1–2 sentences |
| Why | 1 short paragraph |
| Meaning | 1 short paragraph |
| Advantages | 3–5 distinct points or 1 tight paragraph |
| Challenges | 3–5 distinct points or 1 tight paragraph |
| Influence | 1–3 sentences per domain; skip padding |
| Luck Cycles | 1 short paragraph, or Insufficient Data |
| Recommendations | Do-list + Avoid-list, each 3–6 items max |
| Executive Summary | 5–8 lines |

Verbosity may expand later by profile.

Value must not expand by repetition.

---

# 9. Pass Test

A reviewer should be able to delete all headings and still hear a consultant.

If the remaining text sounds like:

> Strong. Score high. Rules matched.

the mode has failed.

If the remaining text sounds like:

> You can carry load, but you must leave room for correction, especially when luck adds even more force.

the mode is on the standard.

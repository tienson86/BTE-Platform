# 1. Purpose

Every interpretation, in every future pack, must answer the same five customer questions.

If a section does not serve one of these questions, it is not interpretation.

---

# 2. The Five Questions

1. **WHY?**
2. **SO WHAT?**
3. **HOW DOES IT AFFECT LIFE?**
4. **WHAT SHOULD THE CUSTOMER DO?**
5. **WHAT SHOULD THE CUSTOMER AVOID?**

These questions are mandatory.

They are not optional flavor.

---

# 3. Mapping to Customer Mode (PACK-01)

| Question | Primary section | Supporting sections |
|----------|-----------------|---------------------|
| WHY? | Why | Conclusion (names what is being explained) |
| SO WHAT? | Meaning | Advantages, Challenges |
| HOW DOES IT AFFECT LIFE? | Influence | Influence during Luck Cycles |
| WHAT SHOULD THE CUSTOMER DO? | Recommendations (do) | Executive Summary |
| WHAT SHOULD THE CUSTOMER AVOID? | Recommendations (avoid) | Challenges, Luck overdrive/depletion |

Executive Summary must touch all five in compressed form.

---

# 4. Mapping to Validation Mode

Validation Mode does not answer the five questions in customer language.

It proves that Customer Mode **may** answer them.

| Question | Mode A proof |
|----------|--------------|
| WHY? | Evidence + Rule Trace |
| SO WHAT? | Final class + polarity of factors + meaning keys |
| HOW DOES IT AFFECT LIFE? | Evidence usable for domains; luck interaction facts or missing |
| DO / AVOID | Challenges/advantage polarities derived from class + conflicts; no invented actions |

If Mode A cannot prove a question, Mode B must use Insufficient Data for that question — not a generic essay.

---

# 5. Question Discipline

## WHY?

Must use this chart’s activated structure.

Must not use a textbook of Strength.

Fail: Because Thân Vượng is one of three traditional categories.

Pass: Because this Day Master is backed by season and root, not standing alone.

## SO WHAT?

Must change the customer’s self-understanding.

Fail: So you are Strong.

Pass: So you can carry load, and you may close the door on correction.

## HOW DOES IT AFFECT LIFE?

Must land in at least one real domain with a distinct effect.

Fail: It affects everything.

Pass: In leadership, people may rely on your stamina; in marriage, that same stamina can take up too much space.

## WHAT SHOULD THE CUSTOMER DO?

Must be actionable and sourced.

Fail: Be a better person.

Pass: Put stamina where feedback exists, so force does not run without a steering wheel.

## WHAT SHOULD THE CUSTOMER AVOID?

Must be the overuse of the same structure, not a random taboo.

Fail: Avoid the color red. Avoid people born in summer.

Pass: Avoid treating tiredness or disagreement as proof that you should push harder.

---

# 6. Completeness Gate

A Strength Customer Mode draft is incomplete if any question is unanswered.

Reviewers ask, in order:

1. Did I learn why this class?
2. Did I learn what it means for me?
3. Did I see life domains, not just personality adjectives?
4. Do I know what to do?
5. Do I know what to stop doing?

If the answer to any is no, the pack output fails.

---

# 7. Order Gate

Advice may not precede understanding.

Illegal customer order:

```text
Do this.
You are Strong.
```

Legal order:

```text
Standing → Why → Meaning → Life effect → Do / Avoid
```

Display may put Executive Summary first, because the summary is written last from that order.

---

# 8. Future Pack Reuse

Pattern, Useful God, Ten Gods, ShenSha, Luck, Career, Marriage, and all later packs MUST answer the same five questions.

Domain examples:

| Pack | WHY? | SO WHAT? |
|------|------|----------|
| Pattern | Why this pattern formed | What operating mode it creates |
| Useful God | Why this element is useful | What to feed in life choices |
| Ten Gods | Why this god is prominent | How it shapes roles and bonds |
| ShenSha | Why this star is active | What tendency it intensifies |
| Luck | Why this period supports/weakens | What to expand or protect now |

No pack may replace the five questions with a glossary.

---

# 9. Anti-Patterns

- Answering WHY with Rule IDs in Customer Mode
- Answering SO WHAT with a definition
- Answering LIFE with “it depends” and no domain
- Answering DO with a career invented from one label
- Answering AVOID with superstition not in evidence
- Answering all five with the same sentence rewritten five times

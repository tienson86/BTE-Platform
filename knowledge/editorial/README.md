## Purpose

This series is the **editorial constitution** of every customer-facing BTE report.

It governs how BTE **speaks** to a paying customer.

It does not govern how BTE **calculates**.

---

## Authority

```text
Product Manifesto
        ↓
Experience Principles
        ↓
Brand Language
        ↓
Editorial Standard          ← this series
        ↓
Customer report
(Narrative · PDF · Portal · Human review)
```

Higher layers win conflicts of purpose.

This standard wins conflicts of **customer prose**.

---

## Who must follow it

- Narrative Composer
- Reports
- PDF
- Portal
- AI generation
- Human editorial review

---

## What this is not

This series is not:

- a software specification
- an engine document
- a Knowledge Asset or Canon
- a Rule Database
- a UI redesign
- customer-facing copy itself

Knowledge decides **what is true**.

This standard decides **what the customer is allowed to read**.

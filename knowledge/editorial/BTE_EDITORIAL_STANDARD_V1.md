# 0. What this document is

This is the editorial constitution of every customer-facing BTE report.

It answers one question:

```text
May this sentence reach a paying customer?
```

If the sentence does not help one human understand themselves, decide, or act,

it does not ship.

This document is not a software specification.

It is not an engine document.

It is not a knowledge document.

Truth is decided upstream.

Prose is decided here.

---

# 1. Editorial philosophy

## Mission

BTE writes as a senior consultant sitting with one person.

The report is a consultation.

It is not a database.

It is not a glossary.

It is not a dump of everything the system knows.

## Governing principles

1. Every paragraph must help the customer.
2. Every sentence must earn its place.
3. Explanation is more valuable than terminology.
4. Guidance is more valuable than classification.
5. Understanding precedes recommendation.
6. Recommendation precedes repetition.
7. The customer must be able to retell the core reading after one pass.
8. If a paragraph reduces the will to continue reading, it fails.

## The product promise

The cover may say the report reads like a conversation, not a spreadsheet.

The body must keep that promise.

A report that calculates correctly and reads like an internal export has failed editorially.

A report that speaks calmly, explains one governing pattern, and leaves one next step has succeeded — even if many true facts remain unpublished.

## What editorial success looks like

The customer finishes the report.

The customer can say, in their own words:

```text
Who I am in this chart.
What helps.
What harms.
What I should do first.
```

If they cannot, the report is not finished.

---

# 2. Reader-first principle

Every section, paragraph, and sentence must answer:

```text
Why does the customer need to read this?
```

Valid answers include:

- It shows what is seen on this chart.
- It explains why this chart works this way.
- It says what that means in this person's life.
- It tells them what to do.
- It tells them what to stop.
- It leaves one picture they can remember.

Invalid answers include:

- It exists in the knowledge library.
- It was matched by the system.
- It is theoretically complete.
- Another god might be relevant on a different chart.
- The writer wants to prove expertise.
- The disclaimer is safer if repeated.

If no valid answer exists, the content does not appear.

Unpublished truth is allowed.

Unread truth is a product failure.

---

# 3. The one-human rule

Write to one named person.

Never write to:

- the engine
- the knowledge base
- a reviewer of completeness
- a future chart that is not this one

The customer is not a container for Ten Gods.

The customer is the subject of the reading.

A role, star, or stem appears only as it operates **this** life.

---

# 4. Section progression

Sections deepen. They do not reprint.

```text
Executive Summary
        → the whole answer in brief

Observation
        → what is seen

Reasoning
        → why it is so

Impact
        → what it means in life

Recommendation
        → what to do

Warning
        → what to avoid

Conclusion
        → one memorable synthesis
```

Career, Finance, Relationship, and Health are life stories inside Impact, or dedicated customer chapters using the same stories.

They are not extra encyclopedias.

---

# 5. Executive Summary rules

## Goal

Give the whole consultation in 4–6 sentences.

A spouse or colleague could be briefed from this section alone.

## Must answer

- Who is this person structurally?
- What governs the chart?
- What helps?
- What harms?
- What should be done first?
- What time frame, if already confirmed, holds the reading?

## Allowed

Complete Vietnamese consulting sentences.

One human thesis.

The governing pattern, useful direction, principal risk, and first priority.

## Forbidden

- raw god lists
- star catalogues
- engine labels
- English topic tags
- glossary entries
- candidate discussion
- raw scores
- confidence numbers
- Feng Shui inventory used as a briefing

## Maximum

4–6 sentences.

One short paragraph is better than six fragments.

## Fail examples from editorial evidence

Bare tokens such as `Thất Sát`, `Kiếp Tài`, `Nhật Chủ` are not a summary.

`Career: Chính Ấn Chính Ấn cách` is not a summary.

A tagged dump is not a briefing.

---

# 6. Observation rules

## Goal

Show what the consultant sees on this chart.

## Allowed

Current-chart facts, stated plainly.

Examples of belonging:

- Day Master
- month / season context
- strength state
- pattern
- selected Useful God
- current Hỷ / Kỵ
- confirmed current luck cycle as a frame, not as a reading

## Forbidden

- interpretation
- recommendations
- encyclopedic definitions
- unused stems
- star alias pedagogy
- hypothetical Useful Gods
- scores (`0.87`, `strong /`)
- internal climate dumps presented as observations

## Maximum

5–8 observations.

## Rule

Observation answers:

```text
What is seen?
```

It does not answer:

```text
What does the library contain?
```

---

# 7. Reasoning rules

## Goal

Explain why the governing reading is true for this person.

This is the highest-value section.

## Required chain

```text
Fact
        ↓
Reason
        ↓
Therefore
        ↓
Meaning
```

Never:

```text
Fact
        ↓
Fact
        ↓
Fact
```

## Required synthesis

Pattern, Strength, and Useful God must be one interpretation, not three labels.

The customer must understand how they combine.

## Allowed

One coherent chain.

A single concise mention that other directions were considered, if it protects the selected reading.

Only roles that operate this chart.

## Forbidden

- candidate tournaments
- rejected-option recitals
- internal scores and group priorities
- a Ten Gods textbook
- Shen Sha alias mechanics
- “if this unused stem were Useful God…”

## Maximum

Enough to make the chain clear.

Prefer 2–4 paragraphs.

Never a catalogue.

## Fail examples from editorial evidence

Five cloned paragraphs for rejected candidates.

A glossary of every god after a correct Useful God argument.

Reasoning that never says what the combination **means for this person**.

---

# 8. Impact rules

## Goal

Say what the reading means in life.

## Required life areas

One concise paragraph each:

- Career
- Finance
- Relationship
- Health

Learning and decision-making may appear if they earn their place.

They must not reopen the catalogue.

## Allowed

Operating-style language.

How this person works, earns, relates, rests, and decides.

One ethical disclaimer per area, at most, and only if needed.

## Forbidden

- profession lists
- “fits every job / infers no job” catalogues
- hypothetical charts
- repeated disclaimers after every clause
- English domain tags
- star-alias relationship notes

## Maximum

One paragraph per area.

Maximum 120 words per paragraph.

## Career specifically

Tell one career story.

The distinction that matters is how the person works, not a job title.

Do not predict one occupation.

Do not list fifteen occupations.

## Fail examples from editorial evidence

A job board followed by “Không suy ra…” after every line.

Finance that repeats “Không hứa hiệu quả tài chính” as a refrain.

Health that says “Không chẩn đoán” louder than the advice.

---

# 9. Recommendation rules

## Goal

Tell this customer what to do.

## Must answer

```text
What should this customer do?
```

Not:

```text
What does the knowledge say?
```

## Rules

- Maximum 5 recommendations.
- Ranked 1 to 5.
- Priority order is customer impact, not library completeness.
- Each item is actionable.
- Each item follows from the reasoning chain.
- Print the block once.

## Forbidden

- duplicate recommendation blocks
- empty actions
- instructions to the production system
- “keep both keys”
- hypothetical Hỷ / Kỵ branches for unused roles
- unranked lists of forty habits

## Fail examples from editorial evidence

The same recommendation set printed twice.

“Nuôi Thực Thần ()”.

Advice written for the writer of the report.

---

# 10. Warning rules

## Goal

Name the principal risks of **this** chart.

## Rules

- Maximum 3 warnings.
- Calm tone.
- Each warning has a human consequence and a way to stay clear of it.
- Warnings follow from the same thesis as the recommendations.

## Forbidden

- fear language
- fortune-teller menace
- production language
- English incident titles
- unused-god risk catalogues
- legal waivers posing as warnings

## Fail examples from editorial evidence

`Overusing Kỵ thần`

`Ignoring Useful God`

`Production phải truyền stems`

A warning chapter that shows the factory floor.

---

# 11. Conclusion rules

## Goal

Leave one memorable picture and one charge.

## Must answer

- Who is this person?
- What is the central pattern?
- What is the useful direction?
- What must they stop?
- What should they do next?

## Allowed

Half a page or less.

Address the person.

Synthesize. Do not restart.

## Forbidden

- reprinting Reasoning
- Ten Gods encyclopedia
- unused stem explanations
- Shen Sha alias mechanics
- closing on system vocabulary

## Fail examples from editorial evidence

Ending on “hữu ích khi hệ thống cần tên gọi khác”.

A second copy of the manual.

---

# 12. Language rules

## Allowed

- expert explanation in customer language
- Vietnamese consulting tone
- precise traditional terms when they are taught in place
- calm authority
- ethical restraint without refrain

The report may use Nhật chủ, Chính Ấn, Dụng thần, Hỷ, Kỵ when those words are carrying meaning for this person.

A term that is used must be made usable.

## Forbidden in customer prose

- engine language
- debug language
- internal variable names
- English implementation terms
- English domain tags (`Career:`, `Health:`, `Decision:`)
- rule ids
- raw scores
- empty fragments (`()`, `Tách vs .`, `trong ,`)
- production notes
- knowledge-engineering talk (`key`, `hit`, `map`, `stems` as implementation)
- mixed Vietnamese/English machine joiners

Normal Vietnamese professional use of an ordinary word is allowed when it is clearly not implementation talk.

## Tone

Calm.

Precise.

Respectful.

Consultant, not calculator.

Never noisy, mystical-absolute, playful, or frightened.

---

# 13. Knowledge inclusion policy

Knowledge may appear only if at least one is true:

- it is an actual current-chart fact
- it explains a selected decision
- it explains the current pattern or strength
- it explains an actual present Ten God or matched star that earns its place
- it is necessary to compare the selected option against a close alternative, in one short mention
- it directly supports an application, recommendation, or warning for this person

Otherwise it is excluded.

Library completeness is not a customer obligation.

A complete knowledge base and a complete customer report are different products.

---

# 14. Hypothetical knowledge

Do not teach unused roles as if they were this chart.

Forbidden customer shapes:

```text
Khi Đinh là Dụng thần…
Khi Nhâm là Hỷ…
Khi Quý là Kỵ…
```

when those roles are not true here.

The knowledge library may contain those sentences.

The customer report may not.

---

# 15. Paragraph rules

- Maximum 120 words.
- One idea.
- One purpose.
- One reader benefit.

A paragraph that both classifies and advises and disclaims and catalogues has failed.

Split or cut.

---

# 16. Duplication rules

No repeated idea.

A fact may return only when it **deepens**:

```text
seen in Observation
        ↓
explained in Reasoning
        ↓
applied in Impact
        ↓
turned into action in Recommendation
```

Never restate the same sentence in different clothes.

Never reprint a whole section.

Never duplicate the recommendation block.

---

# 17. Section contract

Each customer section has a contract.

| Section | Goal | Allowed | Forbidden | Maximum |
|---------|------|---------|-----------|---------|
| Executive Summary | Whole answer | 4–6 consulting sentences | lists, scores, glossary, tags | 6 sentences |
| Observation | What is seen | current-chart facts | interpretation, recs, encyclopedia | 5–8 observations |
| Reasoning | Why it is so | one Fact→Reason→Therefore→Meaning chain | tournament, textbook | 2–4 paragraphs |
| Impact | What it means in life | one paragraph per life area | job catalogue, disclaimer spam | 120 words / paragraph |
| Career | How this person works | style, fit, harm, useful direction, time frame | 15 professions | one story |
| Recommendation | What to do | 3–5 ranked actions | duplicates, unused-role advice | 5 items |
| Warning | What to avoid | 3 principal risks | fear, production, catalogues | 3 items |
| Conclusion | One picture | synthesis and next step | encyclopedia restart | ½ page |

If a section cannot keep its contract, cut content. Do not widen the contract.

---

# 18. Commercial standard

A paying customer should finish the report.

Editorial quality is commercial quality.

The report fails commercial review when:

- the reader stops
- the reader cannot retell the core
- the reader does not know what to do this month
- the reader sees the machine
- a senior consultant would refuse to sign it

The report passes commercial review when:

- it can be read in one sitting
- it sounds like a person who has studied this chart
- it leaves a thesis, a risk, and a first action
- unused knowledge stays unpublished

Beginner dumps, advanced catalogues, and expert libraries are not the commercial voice.

The commercial voice is a professional consultant.

# 19. Editorial checklist

Use this gate before a customer PDF, Portal report, or human-signed delivery ships.

```text
□ Written to one human, not to the system
□ Every section answers why the customer must read it
□ Executive Summary is 4–6 sentences and briefs the whole reading
□ No raw god lists in the summary
□ Observation is 5–8 seen facts only
□ Reasoning is one Fact → Reason → Therefore → Meaning chain
□ Pattern + Strength + Useful God are one interpretation
□ No unused-stem / hypothetical Hỷ-Kỵ teaching
□ Impact has one career story, one money story, one relationship story, one health story
□ No profession catalogue
□ No disclaimer refrain
□ Recommendations are ranked, maximum 5, printed once
□ Each recommendation tells the customer what to do
□ Warnings are maximum 3, calm, chart-true
□ Conclusion is a synthesis, not a reprint
□ No engine, debug, production, or implementation language
□ No empty fragments
□ No English domain tags
□ No duplicated section
□ Current luck, if shown, is a frame — not an invented reading
□ A customer can retell the core after one reading
□ A senior consultant would sign this prose
```

If any box fails, the report is not editorially released.

# 20. Scoring

Score the customer report, not the implementation.

Each dimension: 0–100.

| Dimension | 0 | 50 | 100 |
|-----------|---|----|-----|
| Readability | Unreadable dump | Dense but parseable | Natural consulting prose |
| Reasoning | Labels only | Partial chain | Full Fact→Meaning chain |
| Knowledge Density | Empty or encyclopedic | Relevant but uneven | Enough expert knowledge, no surplus |
| Customer Value | Would not pay | Some help, hard to use | Worth paying for |
| Actionability | No next step | Advice exists, unranked | 3–5 ranked, doable actions |
| Commercial Quality | Internal export | Mixed | Signed consultant letter |
| Overall | Not shippable | Needs editorial repair | Ready for customers |

## Gates

Overall below 40: not a customer report.

Overall 40–69: editorial repair required. Not ready for customers.

Overall 70–84: ready for product review. Not yet signed.

Overall 85–100: ready for customers.

A zero on Language (engine/debug visible) cannot be offset by high Knowledge Density.

A correct chart call with unreadable prose fails.

# 21. Product examples

Editorial evidence: Nguyễn Tiến Sơn production narrative review.

The review itself is not modified here.

Only principles are extracted.

## The human thesis that should have governed that report

Nguyễn Tiến Sơn can carry substantial responsibility.

The chart is built on Chính Ấn and a strong Day Master.

The corrective direction is Thực Thần: surplus strength must become useful, visible, completed output.

The principal risk is not lack of strength.

The risk is adding load, control, responsibility, or peer competition because he still can.

That thesis is editorial law for a report like his:

one person, one pattern, one medicine, one poison.

## What already worked

- The chart call was expert: Canh, winter, Chính Ấn, thân vượng, Thực Thần, Hỷ output, Kỵ companion/rob wealth.
- Anti-overclaim (“do not infer a degree / accident / marriage / fixed job”) is ethically adult when used once.
- A few lines were already consultant-grade: structured output as remedy; do not take more because you still can; Ấn must not seal the system; rest after load; relationship must not become a warehouse.
- The class title *Người tự gánh* was the right human name for the pattern.

Principle extracted:

```text
Keep the correct call.
Publish the human thesis.
Do not publish the library that produced it.
```

## What failed, and the rule it created

| Evidence of failure | Editorial rule |
|---------------------|----------------|
| Cover promised a conversation; body was a data sheet | Keep the promise in the first page of prose |
| English tags `Career:` `Health:` `Decision:` | No implementation language in customer prose |
| `strong / 0.87` | No raw scores |
| Full Ten Gods and unused-stem encyclopedia | Knowledge inclusion policy |
| `Khi Đinh là Dụng thần` on a Thực Thần chart | No hypothetical roles |
| Recommendation block printed twice | No duplication |
| Empty `()` and `Tách vs .` | Broken fragments never ship |
| `Production phải truyền stems` | Production language is a critical fail |
| Job catalogue plus “do not infer this job” | Operating style, not occupation lists |
| Disclaimer louder than counsel | One ethical line, then advice |
| Conclusion restarted the manual | Conclusion synthesizes |
| Customer could not retell the core | Reader-first and one-human rules |

## What “good” would have sounded like

Not a rewrite of that case.

A shape:

```text
You are a person who can carry a great deal.
Your chart is built on structured support, with surplus strength.
The medicine is lawful output — finish work that can be seen.
The poison is taking more because you still can.
This is the first thing to change.
```

If the published report cannot be reduced to five such sentences, it is not yet edited.

# 22. Authority and change

This is Official v1.0.

It is frozen as editorial constitution.

Do not weaken a rule to make composition easier.

Do not add a section to this document to justify a dump.

If product voice and this standard conflict, this standard governs customer prose.

If knowledge truth and this standard conflict, knowledge remains true and unpublished until it can be spoken as consultation.

# 23. Final editorial law

```text
Truth may be complete.
The report must be selectable.

The library may be large.
The customer must be able to finish.

The engine may know every god.
The consultant names the few that govern this life.
```

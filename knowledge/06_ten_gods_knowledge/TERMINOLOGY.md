# Ten Gods Knowledge Terminology

**Module:** Ten Gods Knowledge  
**Version:** V1.0.0  
**Status:** Frozen (Terminology Specification)

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines canonical terminology for Ten Gods knowledge.

Shared fundamental terms are referenced from Fundamental Knowledge and are not redefined here.

---

# 2. Term Contract

Every term shall include:

| Field | Requirement |
|-------|-------------|
| term_id | Stable unique identity |
| canonical_term | Canonical label |
| definition | Precise meaning in Ten Gods domain |
| scope | Ten Gods Knowledge scope |
| aliases | Alternate labels |
| relationships | Related terms / fundamental / upstream references |
| language | Locale |
| version | Module-aligned version |

---

# 4. Interaction and Life-Area Terms

## Relationship Models

Definition: Declarative models of mutual support, conflict, control, and structure among Ten Gods.

Scope: Relationship Models.

Aliases: Ten Gods Relationship Concepts.

Relationships: related to all Ten Gods Definitions.

## Strength Interaction

Definition: Interaction concept linking published Day Master strength class to Ten Gods quality and favorability.

Scope: Strength Interaction.

Aliases: Body-Strength Interaction.

Relationships: references Strength Knowledge classifications; does not redefine them.

## Pattern Interaction

Definition: Interaction concept linking published Pattern identity to Ten Gods structural and quality knowledge.

Scope: Pattern Interaction.

Aliases: Ge Ju Interaction.

Relationships: references Pattern Knowledge identities; does not redefine them.

## Useful God Interaction

---

# 6. Non-Redefinition Rule

Stem, Branch, Wu Xing, and shared Ten Gods relationship taxonomies remain owned by Fundamental Knowledge.

Strength, Pattern, and Useful God classification identities remain owned by their respective Knowledge Modules.

Ten Gods Terminology may specialize analytical and life-area labels but must reference upstream identities.

---

# 7. Acceptance Criteria

Terminology is accepted when all mandatory Ten Gods terms include definition, scope, aliases, and relationships, and remain consistent with Fundamental and upstream analytical Knowledge Modules.

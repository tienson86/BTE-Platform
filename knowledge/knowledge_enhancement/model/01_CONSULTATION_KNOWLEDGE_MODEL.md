## 1. Purpose

Model how a **professional BaZi consultant thinks** when serving a client.

This is not a Rule Database taxonomy (Five Elements, Ten Gods, Strength, …).  
Those are **analytical inputs**.

Consultation domains are **client-facing advisory topics**: what the person came to understand and decide.

```
Analytical facts (chart, scores, patterns, gods, luck)
        ↓
Consultation Knowledge (this model)
        ↓
Advice the client can use
```

---

## 2. Consultant thinking loop

A commercial consultation typically follows:

1. **Establish identity** — who is this chart in human language  
2. **Explain structure** — strengths, limits, pattern logic (non-technical)  
3. **Map to life questions** — career, relationships, health, timing, …  
4. **Prioritize** — what matters now vs later  
5. **Recommend** — concrete, ethical next steps  
6. **Warn + mitigate** — risks without fear-mongering  
7. **Settle** — one clear conclusion the client can remember  

Pack 05 Narrative grammar (Exec → Observation → Reasoning → Impact → Recommendation → Warning → Conclusion) is the **delivery shape**.  
Consultation domains below are the **topic space** that fills that shape.

## 4. Domain definitions

For each domain: **Purpose**, **Questions answered**, **Knowledge inputs**, **Expected outputs**.

### 4.1 Identity (CK-ID)

| Field | Definition |
|-------|------------|
| **Purpose** | Name the person in consultant language (day master / pattern / strength identity) without calculator jargon |
| **Questions answered** | Who is this? What is the core structure? How should we speak about them? |
| **Knowledge inputs** | Day master, yin/yang, pattern label, strength band, primary useful god (analytical facts) |
| **Expected outputs** | Identity statement; naming vocabulary; Exec Summary identity slot |

### 4.2 Personality (CK-PE)

| Field | Definition |
|-------|------------|
| **Purpose** | Describe tendencies and interaction style ethically |
| **Questions answered** | How do I typically behave under pressure? What are natural strengths of character? |
| **Knowledge inputs** | Ten gods profile, day master nature, pattern temperament signals |
| **Expected outputs** | Trait narratives; strength/weakness personality language (not clinical diagnosis) |

### 4.3 Career (CK-CA)

| Field | Definition |
|-------|------------|
| **Purpose** | Advise on work direction and role fit |
| **Questions answered** | What kinds of work suit me? When to push vs consolidate? |
| **Knowledge inputs** | Useful god, ten gods (officer/wealth/output), pattern, luck windows |
| **Expected outputs** | Role themes; timing notes; career actions / cautions |

### 4.4 Business (CK-BU)

| Field | Definition |
|-------|------------|
| **Purpose** | Advise on enterprise and partnership risk |
| **Questions answered** | Solo vs partnership? Expansion vs conservation? |
| **Knowledge inputs** | Wealth/officer structures, clash/combine, luck, useful god |
| **Expected outputs** | Business posture; partnership cautions; opportunity windows |

### 4.5 Leadership (CK-LE)

| Field | Definition |
|-------|------------|
| **Purpose** | Clarify authority style and influence conditions |
| **Questions answered** | How do I lead well? When does authority strain me? |
| **Knowledge inputs** | Officer/kill structures, strength, pattern, shensha (selected) |
| **Expected outputs** | Leadership style notes; strain risks; mitigation habits |

### 4.6 Finance (CK-FI)

| Field | Definition |
|-------|------------|
| **Purpose** | Guide money behavior and wealth pacing |
| **Questions answered** | How do I earn/keep/grow? What financial risks recur? |
| **Knowledge inputs** | Wealth gods, useful/enemy gods, luck, clash on wealth pillars |
| **Expected outputs** | Money posture; timing; risk + mitigation (no guaranteed returns) |

### 4.7 Marriage (CK-MA)

| Field | Definition |
|-------|------------|
| **Purpose** | Discuss partnership themes carefully and ethically |
| **Questions answered** | Relationship tendencies? Timing sensitivity? Caution areas? |
| **Knowledge inputs** | Spouse palace signals, relevant ten gods, clash/harm, luck |
| **Expected outputs** | Relationship themes; caution + mitigation; never fatalistic claims |

### 4.8 Relationships (CK-RE)

| Field | Definition |
|-------|------------|
| **Purpose** | Broader social/family bond patterns beyond marriage |
| **Questions answered** | Peer dynamics? Support network? Conflict patterns? |
| **Knowledge inputs** | Ten gods peer/output, combinations/clashes, shensha (selected) |
| **Expected outputs** | Bonding guidance; conflict awareness; repair actions |

### 4.9 Children (CK-CH)

| Field | Definition |
|-------|------------|
| **Purpose** | Address offspring themes with high ethical care |
| **Questions answered** | Themes around children / nurturing roles? (not medical fertility claims) |
| **Knowledge inputs** | Children palace / related gods (when available), luck |
| **Expected outputs** | Careful thematic language; disclaimers; supportive actions only |

### 4.10 Parents (CK-PA)

| Field | Definition |
|-------|------------|
| **Purpose** | Parental / elder relationship themes |
| **Questions answered** | Support vs duty dynamics with parents/elders? |
| **Knowledge inputs** | Parental palace signals, ten gods, clash/combine |

### 4.11 Health (CK-HE)

| Field | Definition |

| **Purpose** | Lifestyle balance hints only — **not medical advice** |
| **Questions answered** | Where is elemental imbalance stressing lifestyle? What pacing helps? |

| **Expected outputs** | Lifestyle suggestions; rest/pace cautions; explicit non-medical framing |

### 4.12 Education (CK-ED)

| Field | Definition |
|-------|------------|
| **Purpose** | Learning path and skill investment advice |

### 4.14 Decision Making (CK-DM)

| Field | Definition |
|-------|------------|

| Field | Definition |

| **Knowledge inputs** | Pattern path, useful god, weaknesses, luck arc |
| **Expected outputs** | Growth themes; multi-year posture; developmental actions |

## 5. Domain → Narrative delivery (summary)

Consultation domains do **not** each become a Pack 05 section.  
They **theme** the evidence that fills the seven fixed Narrative components.

| Narrative component | Typical domains contributing |
|---------------------|------------------------------|
| Executive Summary | Identity, Decision Making, Luck, + top life domain in scope |
| Observation | Identity, Personality (factual) |
| Reasoning | Analytical explanation framed for any active domain |
| Impact | Career / Finance / Relationships / Lifestyle implications |
| Recommendation | Decision Making, Career, Lifestyle, Luck actions |
| Warning | Finance, Marriage, Health (lifestyle), Luck risks + mitigation |
| Conclusion | Identity + Decision Making settle |

## 7. Anti-patterns

| Anti-pattern | Why forbidden |
|--------------|---------------|
| Treating Ten Gods as a consultation domain itself | Analytical input, not client topic |
| Inventing life answers without analytical inputs | Breaks truthfulness / G6 honesty |
| Medical diagnosis under Health | Product & ethics risk |
| Absolute fate language under Marriage/Children | Brand & ethics risk |
| One domain = one Narrative section | Violates frozen Pack 05 grammar |

# Strength Knowledge Validation Standard

**Module:** Strength Knowledge  
**Version:** V1.0.0  
**Status:** Frozen (Validation Standard)

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines validation requirements for Strength Knowledge.

---

# 2. Validation Levels

1. Structural Validation
2. Semantic Validation
3. Knowledge Completeness
4. Rule Consistency
5. Mapping Integrity
6. Terminology Consistency
7. Metadata Validation
8. Golden Dataset Validation
9. Regression Validation

---

# 3. Structural Validation

Verify Descriptor/Manifest completeness, unique IDs, declared asset population, and documentation presence.

---

# 4. Semantic Validation

Verify:

- rule category validity
- condition/action completeness
- priority consistency
- formula-model consistency with rules and tables
- no contradiction with Fundamental Knowledge identities

---

# 5. Knowledge Completeness

Mandatory V1.0 coverage includes all scoped dimensions:

Seasonal Strength, Monthly Branch Influence, Heavenly Stem Support, Hidden Stem Support, Root Strength, Five Element Support/Restriction, Combination/Clash/Harm/Punishment/Void Influence, Temperature Adjustment Influence, Growth Stage, Tong Gen, De Ling, De Di, De Shi, Special Exceptions, Weight Models, Confidence Models, Priority Concepts.

---

# 6. Rule Consistency

Validate that overlapping rules have deterministic priority/conflict resolution and compatible actions.

---

# 7. Mapping Integrity

Validate deterministic entries and alignment with domain model entities.

---

# 8. Terminology Consistency

Validate mandatory terms, aliases, relationships, and non-redefinition of Fundamental terms.

---

# 9. Metadata Validation

Validate mandatory metadata and Manifest consistency.

---

# 10. Golden and Regression Validation

Golden Datasets must pass for representative strength knowledge outcomes.

Regression Datasets must protect accepted semantics across versions.

---

# 11. Acceptance Criteria

Validation passes when all mandatory levels succeed and no path-coupled identity is required.

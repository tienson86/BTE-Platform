# Strength Knowledge Terminology

**Module:** Strength Knowledge  
**Version:** V1.0.0  
**Status:** Frozen (Terminology Specification)

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines canonical terminology for Day Master Strength knowledge.

Shared fundamental terms are referenced from Fundamental Knowledge and are not redefined here.

---

# 2. Term Contract

Every term shall include:

| Field | Requirement |
|-------|-------------|
| term_id | Stable unique identity |
| canonical_term | Canonical label |
| definition | Precise meaning in strength domain |
| scope | Strength Knowledge scope |
| aliases | Alternate labels |
| relationships | Related terms / fundamental references |
| language | Locale |
| version | Module-aligned version |

---

# 3. Core Strength Classifications

## Thân Vượng

Definition: Day Master classified as strong.

Scope: Strength classification outcome.

Aliases: Strong Day Master, Body Strong.

Relationships: opposite of Thân Nhược; may relate to Trung Hòa as adjacent class.

## Thân Nhược

Definition: Day Master classified as weak.

Scope: Strength classification outcome.

Aliases: Weak Day Master, Body Weak.

Relationships: opposite of Thân Vượng; may relate to Trung Hòa as adjacent class.

## Trung Hòa

Definition: Day Master classified as balanced / neutral strength.

Scope: Strength classification outcome.

Aliases: Balanced, Neutral Strength.

Relationships: intermediate relative to Thân Vượng and Thân Nhược.

# 4. Attainment Concepts

## Đắc Lệnh

Definition: Day Master obtains seasonal command advantage.

Scope: Seasonal attainment factor.

Aliases: De Ling, Getting the Command.

Relationships: related to SeasonCategory and Seasonal Strength.

## Đắc Địa

Definition: Day Master obtains earthly/rooting advantage.

Scope: Root / branch attainment factor.

Aliases: De Di, Getting the Ground.

Relationships: related to RootType and Tong Gen.

## Đắc Thế

Definition: Day Master obtains positional/support advantage in chart composition.

Scope: Compositional attainment factor.

Aliases: De Shi, Getting the Momentum.

Relationships: related to SupportType and stem/element support.

# 5. Root and Growth Concepts

## Thông Căn

Definition: Day Master connects to roots through hidden stems / branch composition.

Scope: Root connectivity factor.

Aliases: Tong Gen, Root Connection.

Relationships: related to RootType, Hidden Stem Support, Đắc Địa.

## Trường Sinh

Definition: Growth-stage class referenced from Fundamental Chang Sheng cycle for strength contribution.

Scope: Growth Stage factor.

Aliases: Chang Sheng, Birth Stage.

Relationships: part of growth-stage sequence with Lâm Quan, Đế Vượng, and other stages.

## Lâm Quan

Definition: Growth-stage class associated with approaching peak force in the life cycle frame.

Scope: Growth Stage factor.

Aliases: Bath / Approaching Officer stage labels as locale-defined aliases.

Relationships: adjacent stages in Chang Sheng sequence.

## Đế Vượng

Definition: Growth-stage class associated with peak prosperity in the life cycle frame.

Scope: Growth Stage factor.

Aliases: Imperial Prosperity, Peak Stage.

Relationships: adjacent stages in Chang Sheng sequence.

## Tuyệt

Definition: Growth-stage class associated with extinction / exhaustion in the life cycle frame.

Scope: Growth Stage factor.

Aliases: Extinction Stage.

Relationships: adjacent stages in Chang Sheng sequence.

## Thai

Definition: Growth-stage class associated with conception / embryonic phase in the life cycle frame.

Scope: Growth Stage factor.

Aliases: Conception Stage.

Relationships: adjacent stages in Chang Sheng sequence.

## Dưỡng

Definition: Growth-stage class associated with nourishment / nurturing phase in the life cycle frame.

Scope: Growth Stage factor.

Aliases: Nurture Stage.

Relationships: adjacent stages in Chang Sheng sequence.

# 7. Non-Redefinition Rule

Heavenly Stem, Earthly Branch, Wu Xing, and base Chang Sheng identities remain owned by Fundamental Knowledge.

Strength Terminology may specialize strength-usage labels but must reference fundamental identities.

---

# 8. Acceptance Criteria

Terminology is accepted when all mandatory strength terms include definition, scope, aliases, and relationships, and remain consistent with Fundamental Knowledge.

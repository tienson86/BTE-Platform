---

## 1. Purpose

Refine Executive Summary **quality** for Wave 1.1 cases:

- Reduce repetition  
- Improve rhythm  
- Improve readability  
- Improve consultant tone  

P0 focuses on what **Knowledge Unit wording** can fix.  
Known Narrative slot-clone issues (strengths ≡ identity) are noted as **P1 companion** — not solved by new units here.

---

## 2. Target Exec rhythm (Wave 1.1)

| Beat | Role | Primary KU | Length guide |
|------|------|------------|--------------|
| 1 | Identity | KU-ID-001 | 1–2 sentences |
| 2 | Strength **or** Weakness (+ arc) | KU-ST-001 **or** KU-WK-001 | 1–2 sentences |
| 3 | Useful-god frame (light) | KU-UG-001 | 1 sentence |
| 4 | Priority posture | KU-RC-001 (Exec slice) | 1 sentence |

Mixed charts may include both Strength and Weakness beats **if** Frame B keeps consistency (`03`).

Customer should finish Exec in **under one minute** of reading.

---

## 3. Anti-repetition rules

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **R1 — One fact, one home** | Day master + pattern named in Identity; Strength does not re-open with full identity sentence |
| **R2 — No clone beats** | Strength text must not be a prefix copy of Identity |
| **R3 — Band once** | Thân posture said once (Identity **or** Strength), not restated identically |
| **R4 — Caution once** | Weakness arc appears once in Exec weaknesses; Warning may shorten, not reprint |
| **R5 — Priority ≠ novel dump** | Exec priority is a short posture line; full Action/Reason/Next lives in Recommendation |

### Authoring implications (Wave 1.1)

| Unit | Refinement |
|------|------------|
| KU-ID-001 | Keep identity; avoid embedding long strength essay |
| KU-ST-001 | Start from điểm tựa / nền — **do not** restart “Bạn là người mang Nhật chủ…” |
| KU-WK-001 | Full mini-arc once; no duplicated sentences inside the unit |
| KU-UG-001 | One clear priority question; no repeat of identity |
| KU-RC-001 | Exec-facing slice short; detailed next step for Recommendation |

## 4. Consultant tone checklist

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Address “bạn” calmly | Calculator observation dumps |
| Name limits without shame | Doom / blame |
| Tie advice to Dụng thần | Token-only god codes as the whole message |
| Use commercial labels (`02`) | `vuong` / `nhuoc` / `can` tokens |
| End with posture | End on emptiness or fear |

---

## 5. Readability patterns

| Pattern | Guidance |
|---------|----------|
| Sentence length | Prefer ≤ 30–35 words for Exec sentences |
| Jargon | Classical terms only if immediately framed in modern meaning |
| Lists | Avoid bullet stacks inside KU body for Exec |
| Concrete time | Keep “2–4 tuần” / “tuần này” in Rec; Exec can say “gần tới” if needed |

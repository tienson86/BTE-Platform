# Logo Usage

Version: 1.0.0  
Sprint: Beta-4

## Name

Spell **BTE** in Latin capitals in UI chrome and legal lines. Product line: **BTE Product v1.0** or **BTE Platform** as context requires. Do not invent a mystical subtitle.

## Clear space

Keep empty margin around the mark at least the height of the “B” on all sides. Do not crowd with badges or seals.

## Backgrounds

Prefer quiet light or quiet dark surfaces from the Visual Language. Do not place the logo on busy photography or rainbow gradients.

## Do not

- Stretch, outline, add drop shadows, or recolor outside the Color Guide  
- Rotate or add 3D chrome  
- Combine with unauthorized partner logos without a written agreement  
- Place the logo smaller than readable at 16px cap height on screen

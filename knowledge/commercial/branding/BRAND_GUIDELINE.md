# Brand Guideline

Version: 1.0.0  
Sprint: Beta-4

Commercial brand usage for BTE Product v1.0. **Does not redesign Product UI.** Foundation remains SSOT.

## Identity

BTE is a professional analytical platform. It should feel like an experienced consultant: calm, precise, scholarly, reliable — never noisy, flashy, or playful.

Sources (do not edit in this sprint):

- `knowledge/product/BTE_PRODUCT_MANIFESTO.md`
- `knowledge/ui_reference/brand/BTE_BRAND_LANGUAGE.md`
- `knowledge/ui_reference/brand/BTE_EXPERIENCE_PRINCIPLES.md`
- `knowledge/ui_reference/visual/VISUAL_LANGUAGE_SYSTEM.md`

## Experience order

Trust → Understanding → Insight → Action → Reflection.

## Commercial applications

Website, email, sales decks, support replies, and launch posts must follow this guideline plus `VOICE_AND_TONE.md` and `WRITING_GUIDE.md`.

## Forbidden commercially

Mystical stock art as the hero, neon “destiny score” badges, invented color palettes, joke memes as primary launch creative.

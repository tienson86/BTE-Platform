# Writing Guide

Version: 1.0.0  
Sprint: Beta-4

For customer, sales, marketing, and support copy.

## Language

Prefer the customer’s language (Vietnamese or English as the surface requires). Keep proper nouns stable: **BTE**, **BaZi**, product section names as in the UI.

## Structure

Short paragraphs. One idea each. Headings that name the outcome (“Read the report”, not “Module 4b”).

## Words to prefer

Understand, pattern, evidence, report, consultant, decision, next step.

## Words to avoid

Fate locked, 100% accurate, miracle, hack, secret algorithm, guaranteed.

## Numbers

Do not invent statistics. Do not quote unpublished SLOs as legal promises.

## Legal

Do not paste disclaimer text into ads. Link to counsel-approved pages after review.

## UI strings

Do not rewrite Product UI copy in this sprint. File a product request instead.

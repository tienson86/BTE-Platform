# Voice and Tone

Version: 1.0.0  
Sprint: Beta-4

## Voice (constant)

Calm. Precise. Professional. Scholarly. Respectful. Confident without swagger.

## Tone (by context)

| Context | Tone |
|---------|------|
| First analysis / onboarding | Patient, guiding |
| Report narrative | Clear, structured, non-dramatic |
| Support | Warm, concrete, no blame |
| Sales demo | Consultative, honest about limits |
| Incident comms | Factual, short, no speculation |

## Never

Hype (“guaranteed wealth”), fear (“your chart is doomed”), slang-as-default, or mockery of traditional knowledge.

## Experience line

Help the reader trust the system, understand the structure, then act. Do not skip to a punchy prediction.

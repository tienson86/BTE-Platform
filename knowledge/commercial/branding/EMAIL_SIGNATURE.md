# Email Signature

Version: 1.0.0  
Sprint: Beta-4

## Standard (plain text + simple HTML)

```
[Full name]
[Role] · BTE
[phone — optional]
[company site URL]

BTE — professional BaZi analysis for clearer decisions.
```

## Rules

- No inspirational quotes  
- No stacked social icon rows  
- No confidential classification banners unless required  
- Logo optional; if used, follow `LOGO_USAGE.md` (small, quiet)  
- Legal entity name as counsel specifies  

## Support / sales variants

Support may add: `Support hours: [TBD per SUPPORT_SLA.md]`  
Sales may add a calendar link **after** privacy review of the booking tool.

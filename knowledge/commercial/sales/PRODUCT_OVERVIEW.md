# Product Overview

Version: 1.0.0  
Sprint: Beta-4

## BTE Product v1.0

A professional BaZi analytical platform: enter birth data, run a stable analysis pipeline, and read a consultant-quality report.

## What the buyer gets

- Product experience designed for trust and understanding  
- Structured reports, not unexplained scores  
- Optional Knowledge Center for learning terms  
- Public API v1 for approved integrations  
- Production operations model (health, backup, runbooks)

## What the buyer does not get

- A fortune-telling entertainment app  
- Custom engine rewrites per client  
- In-app payments (not in v1.0)  
- A published price in this repository  

## Fit

Independent consultants, studios, educators, and organizations that need explainable analysis.

# Consultation Flow

Version: 1.0.0  
Sprint: Beta-4

End-to-end commercial consultation (sales + practitioner session pattern).

## Sales consultation (pre-purchase)

1. Intro and ethics (not fortune-telling)  
2. Current workflow  
3. Success definition  
4. Demo or recorded walkthrough  
5. License / seats discussion (placeholder SKUs)  
6. Legal/privacy: pages under counsel review  
7. Agree pilot or go-live date  

## Practitioner consultation (using BTE with a client)

1. Intake and consent to use birth data  
2. Clarify one primary question  
3. First analysis  
4. Read report together (`customer/REPORT_READING_GUIDE.md`)  
5. Agree 1–3 human actions  
6. Optional follow-up case  

BTE supports the practitioner; it does not replace their duty of care.

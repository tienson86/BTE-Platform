# Sales Playbook

Version: 1.0.0  
Sprint: Beta-4

## Qualify

- Do they serve real clients or students?  
- Do they care about explaining results?  
- Are they asking for guaranteed predictions? (disqualify or reframe)  
- Decision maker and timeline?

## Discovery questions

- How do you produce reports today?  
- Where do clients get confused?  
- Who enters birth data?  
- Any integration need (API) vs portal only?

## Pitch order

1. Problem: fragmented, unexplained BaZi software  
2. Position: consultant, not calculator  
3. Demo (`DEMO_SCRIPT.md`)  
4. Consultation workflow (`CONSULTATION_FLOW.md`)  
5. Commercial next step — license conversation with placeholders only  

## Objections

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| “Another calculator” | Show report structure and explanation, not a single number |
| “AI can do this” | BTE is a frozen, explainable platform — not a chat lottery |
| “Change the rules for me” | v1.0 is AF-1 frozen; we listen via feature requests |
| “Price?” | Use `PRICING_PLACEHOLDER.md` — do not invent a number in git |

## Do not

Discount by promising engine edits. Do not trash traditional schools.

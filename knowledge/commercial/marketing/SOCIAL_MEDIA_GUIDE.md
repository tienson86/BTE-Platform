# Social Media Guide

Version: 1.0.0  
Sprint: Beta-4

## Channels

Choose 1–2 professional networks. Do not spam every platform with the same mystical graphic.

## Pillars

1. Education (how to read a report section)  
2. Craft (consultant ethics)  
3. Product (calm screenshots, no PII)  
4. Launch news (once)

## Cadence (placeholder)

2–4 posts / month around launch, then sustainable.

## Visuals

Quiet surfaces, Foundation type/color roles, logo clear space. No neon destiny wheels.

## Do not

- Publish client charts  
- Claim accuracy percentages  
- Argue other schools  
- Put TBD prices in captions  

## Approval

Marketing owner + brand check. Legal pages must exist before “we take your data seriously” claims.

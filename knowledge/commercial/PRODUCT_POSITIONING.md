# Product Positioning

Version: 1.0.0  
Sprint: Beta-4

## Category

Professional BaZi analytical platform and decision-support product.

## Position

BTE is the consultant, not the calculator.

It turns traditional BaZi knowledge into structured, explainable, actionable analysis that a practitioner or serious learner can trust.

## Versus alternatives

| Alternative | BTE difference |
|-------------|----------------|
| Entertainment fortune apps | No mystical theatre. Explanation before recommendation. |
| Spreadsheet / hobby calculators | Full pipeline, report contract, commercial-grade operations. |
| Opaque “AI destiny” chat | Transparent structure, Knowledge-backed interpretation, audit-friendly reports. |
| Pure academic texts | Guided product experience: trust → understanding → action. |

## Promise

Every report should help the user understand more, think more clearly, and decide with better information.

## Not this product

Fortune-telling entertainment, superstition generators, unexplained scores, or a consumer widget that hides the reasoning.

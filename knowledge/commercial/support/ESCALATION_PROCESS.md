# Escalation Process

Version: 1.0.0  
Sprint: Beta-4

## When to escalate

- S1 incident  
- Suspected personal-data breach  
- Repeated S2 after a failed fix  
- Customer requesting contractual credit or legal threat  
- Security disclosure  

## Path

1. Support owner documents the ticket and impact.  
2. Platform-ops for availability (runbooks).  
3. Product owner for defect vs expected analysis behavior.  
4. Counsel for legal threats or privacy incidents.  
5. Founder / exec only for public statements.

## Customer updates

Escalation does not mean silence. Keep the SLA update cadence. Do not promise engine patches.

## After

Close with cause class (ops / data entry / product defect / known limit) and a link to post-incident note if S1.

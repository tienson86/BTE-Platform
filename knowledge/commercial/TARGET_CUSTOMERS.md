# Target Customers

Version: 1.0.0  
Sprint: Beta-4

## Primary

**Independent BaZi consultants and small studios**  
Need a reliable analysis and report workflow for client sessions. Care about trust, clarity, and exportable reports.

## Secondary

**Educators and study groups**  
Need consistent charts and language for teaching. Care about explanation quality, not entertainment.

## Tertiary (enterprise conversation)

**Wellness, HR advisory, or cultural-services organizations** (where legally appropriate)  
Need branded, auditable decision-support — not fortune-telling. See `sales/ENTERPRISE_OVERVIEW.md`.

## Not a fit (v1.0)

- Users seeking guaranteed predictions or gambling tips
- Teams that require custom engine rule edits as a service
- Mass-market entertainment / social fortune games
- Buyers who need live payment checkout in-product (not in v1.0 scope)

## Buying triggers

New practice opening, report inconsistency across staff, client complaints about unexplained results, need for a professional portal.

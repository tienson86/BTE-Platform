# Launch Plan

Version: 1.0.0  
Sprint: Beta-4

## Goal

Commercially announce BTE Product v1.0 as a professional BaZi analytical platform, with AF-1 frozen and operational Beta-1–3 layers in place.

## Phases

| Phase | Intent |
|-------|--------|
| T-14d | Counsel review of legal templates; freeze marketing copy |
| T-7d | Internal go-live rehearsal; rollback drill |
| T-2d | Customer onboarding pack ready; support rota live |
| T-0 | Go live per `release/GO_LIVE_CHECKLIST.md` |
| T+1d | Monitor health, support queue, error budget |
| T+7d | Post-release review (`release/POST_RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md`) |
| T+30d | Success criteria check (`SUCCESS_CRITERIA.md`) |

## Launch assets

- `marketing/LAUNCH_ANNOUNCEMENT.md`
- `marketing/WEBSITE_COPY.md`
- `customer/QUICK_START.md`
- Legal pages (published only after review)

## Audience order

1. Existing beta consultants  
2. Waitlist / warm leads  
3. Public announcement

## Kill criteria (do not launch)

Unreviewed legal pages, failing `/health`, incomplete rollback plan, or any unfrozen engine/UI change sneaking into the release.

# Commercial Readiness

Version: 1.0.0  
Sprint: Beta-4

BTE Product v1.0 is commercially ready when the items below are true. Technical freeze (AF-1) is a prerequisite, not a substitute.

## Ready means

1. Positioning and value proposition are written and consistent with the Product Manifesto.
2. Legal templates exist and are marked for counsel review before publish.
3. Brand usage is documented without redesigning Product UI.
4. Non-technical customer guides cover first analysis through report reading.
5. Support process and SLA placeholders are defined.
6. Sales and marketing kits exist with **no production prices**.
7. Go-live, rollback, communication, and onboarding checklists exist.
8. Engines, pipelines, Knowledge packages, APIs, and UI remain unchanged by this sprint.

## Not required for Beta-4

- Payment processing
- Authentication implementation
- Published price list
- Signed legal opinions
- New product features

See `SUCCESS_CRITERIA.md` and `release/GO_LIVE_CHECKLIST.md`.

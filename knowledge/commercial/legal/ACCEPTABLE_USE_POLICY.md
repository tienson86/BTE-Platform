## Permitted

Lawful professional analysis, education, and internal decision-support consistent with the Product Manifesto (understanding, not coercion).

## Prohibited (draft list)

- Using outputs to harass, discriminate, or make automated decisions that applicable law forbids without human review  
- Representing BTE results as guaranteed fate, medical diagnosis, or financial advice  
- Attempting to extract, scrape, or republish Knowledge packages or engine internals  
- Overloading the Service (abuse of API, credential stuffing)  
- Uploading unlawful content or others’ personal data without a lawful basis  
- Reverse engineering except where mandatory law allows  

## Enforcement

Provider may suspend access for material breach, subject to the reviewed Terms of Service.

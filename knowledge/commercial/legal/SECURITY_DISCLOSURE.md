# Security Disclosure

**Template for legal review**

This document is a production-ready draft template. It is **not legal advice** and **not a security certification**. It does not claim SOC2, ISO 27001, or penetration-test results unless those are independently obtained and counsel approves the wording.

## Safe harbor (draft intent)

Provider intends not to pursue legal action against good-faith researchers who:

- Avoid privacy violations and service degradation  
- Give a reasonable time to remediate before public disclosure  
- Do not access data that is not their own  

**[Counsel must approve any safe-harbor statement.]**

## Our response targets (operational, not a warranty)

- Acknowledge: 2 business days  
- Severity triage: per `../support/ISSUE_TRIAGE.md`  
- Customer notification: if counsel agrees personal data was affected  

## Out of scope for this template

Bug bounties, public hall of fame, and guaranteed patch SLAs.

Related internal baseline: `deployment/SECURITY_BASELINE.md` (ops, not a customer claim).

# Success Criteria

Version: 1.0.0  
Sprint: Beta-4

## Commercial (30 days post go-live)

- Launch announcement published through approved channels  
- Onboarding pack sent to first customers  
- Support process followed for 100% of tickets (no silent Slack-only fixes)  
- No published legal page that skipped counsel review

## Product quality (unchanged AF-1)

- No emergency engine or pipeline rewrite  
- Public API v1 contract unchanged  
- Product UI not redesigned under launch pressure

## Operations

- Availability within documented SLO window (`applications/documentation/SERVICE_LEVEL_OBJECTIVES.md`)  
- Rollback unused, or used successfully if needed  
- Backup verify still green

## Customer understanding

- First-analysis guide usable without engineering help  
- FAQ covers the top “is this fortune-telling?” and “how do I read the report?” questions

## Explicit non-goals

Revenue targets, conversion funnels, and paid ads performance are out of scope for this document.

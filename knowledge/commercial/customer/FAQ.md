# FAQ

Version: 1.0.0  
Sprint: Beta-4  
Audience: non-technical users

## Is BTE fortune-telling?

No. BTE is a professional analysis and education product. It explains patterns to support better decisions. It does not lock anyone’s fate.

## Can results be 100% certain?

No. Treat reports as structured input to human judgment.

## Why does birth time matter?

Traditional BaZi uses time to place the hour pillar. An unknown time still allows useful work, but hour-related statements should stay modest.

## Solar or lunar date?

Use the calendar type that matches the record you hold. If unsure, ask a practitioner; do not switch types to “get a better result.”

## Can I analyze someone else?

Only with a lawful basis and their trust. You are responsible for the personal data you type in.

## The report looks different from an app I used before

Different schools and products emphasize different structures. BTE follows its own frozen analytical platform. Compare methods; do not assume one screenshot invalidates another without study.

## Something is slow or failed

See `TROUBLESHOOTING.md`. Share the on-screen reference number with support.

## How much does it cost?

Ask your account contact. Public price lists are not published in this documentation set.

# Troubleshooting

Version: 1.0.0  
Sprint: Beta-4  
Audience: non-technical users

## Cannot sign in

- Confirm you are on the URL your administrator sent  
- Retry once; avoid many rapid attempts  
- Ask your administrator to check that your access is still active  

This guide does not describe a self-serve password-reset product.

## Analysis does not start

- Required birth fields empty or impossible date (e.g. month 13)  
- Network interruption — wait, then open history if the case was saved  
- Do not click submit many times with the same data

## Analysis takes a long time

Complex reports can take a while on a busy system. Wait for the completion state. If it exceeds what your administrator considers normal, contact support with the time you started and the Request ID if shown.

## Report looks empty or incomplete

- Refresh once after completion  
- Confirm you opened the finished case, not a draft  
- If still empty, stop and contact support — do not re-enter private data in a public chat

## Chart “feels wrong”

First re-check birth time, timezone, and calendar type with the family record. Most mismatches start there.

## Need a human

See `../support/SUPPORT_PROCESS.md`. Include: what you tried, screenshot without unnecessary third-party data, Request ID.

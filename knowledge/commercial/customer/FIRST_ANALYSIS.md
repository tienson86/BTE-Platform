# First Analysis

Version: 1.0.0  
Sprint: Beta-4  
Audience: non-technical users

## Prepare the facts

Write down:

- Full name (for your records)  
- Date of birth  
- Time of birth (or “unknown”)  
- Timezone or city  
- Calendar type you intend (commonly solar civil date)

Wrong time or timezone is the most common source of confusion — not “the software being mystical.”

## Enter and run

1. Choose new analysis.  
2. Fill required fields. Leave optional fields empty rather than guessing.  
3. Submit once.  
4. When status shows complete, open the report.

## How to read the first time

Spend ten quiet minutes:

1. Who is this chart about? (identity line)  
2. What is the overall picture?  
3. Which two sections feel most relevant to the question you actually have?  
4. What is one practical next step — a conversation, not a superstition?

Use `REPORT_READING_GUIDE.md` for section order.

## Save your work

If the product keeps history, name the case clearly (person + date). Do not use other people’s data for practice without permission.

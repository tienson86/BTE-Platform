# Knowledge Center Guide

Version: 1.0.0  
Sprint: Beta-4  
Audience: non-technical users

The Knowledge Center (when enabled on your site) explains concepts that appear in reports. It is a learning aid, not a second fortune engine.

## How to use it

1. Finish reading the report overview first.  
2. Open the Knowledge Center for a term you do not know.  
3. Return to the report and reread that section.  
4. Do not collect concepts without connecting them to this chart.

## What you will not find

Secret “override codes,” unofficial rule dumps, or editable Knowledge packages. Those are not customer features.

## Teaching

Educators may assign one Knowledge article plus one report section per lesson.

## If an article is missing

Use `../support/FEATURE_REQUEST_TEMPLATE.md`. Do not treat a missing article as a product defect unless a report link is broken — then use the bug template.

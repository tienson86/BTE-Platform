# Best Practices

Version: 1.0.0  
Sprint: Beta-4  
Audience: consultants and serious learners

## Data quality

Verify birth records before the session. One careful analysis beats three rushed ones.

## Session design

1. Agree the question (career, timing, study — one primary question).  
2. Run BTE.  
3. Read together.  
4. Translate into actions the person can actually take.  
5. Leave space for reflection. Do not dump every section.

## Ethics

Do not frighten clients. Do not sell add-on rituals as if BTE required them. Do not present scores as moral judgment.

## Privacy

Use private devices. Lock the screen. Do not post full reports on social media.

## Teaching

When teaching, show the structure of the report first, then one example. Point learners to `KNOWLEDGE_CENTER_GUIDE.md` if that area is enabled.

## Product changes

Do not ask support to “tweak the engine for this client.” Architecture is frozen for v1.0. Feedback goes through `../support/FEATURE_REQUEST_TEMPLATE.md`.

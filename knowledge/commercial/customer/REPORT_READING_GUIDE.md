# Report Reading Guide

Version: 1.0.0  
Sprint: Beta-4  
Audience: non-technical users

Read top to bottom. Hierarchy is intentional.

## Suggested order

1. **Who and when** — confirm this is the right person and birth data.  
2. **Overview** — the calm summary. Stay here until it makes sense.  
3. **Chart structure** — pillars and basic relationships, as presented.  
4. **Analysis** — strength, pattern, and related structured findings.  
5. **Interpretation** — narrative explanation. This is where understanding should deepen.  
6. **Suggestions / next steps** — actions and cautions, not commands.  
7. **Details** — only if you need evidence for a specific question.

## How to talk about it

Prefer: “The report highlights this pattern, which may mean…”  
Avoid: “The computer says you will…”

## If two sections seem to disagree

That can happen in real charts. Note both, look at the overview, and use human judgment. Do not pick the more dramatic sentence.

## Printing or sharing

Follow your privacy rules. Remove another client’s name from screenshots.

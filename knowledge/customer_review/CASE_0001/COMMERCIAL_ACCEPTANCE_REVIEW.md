# Scoring Method

Each chapter scored **0–10** on seven dimensions:

| Dimension | Question the customer asks |
|-----------|---------------------------|
| Clarity | Do I understand this without a glossary? |
| Trust | Do I believe this consultant — and feel nothing is hidden? |
| Customer Value | Did I get something worth paying for in this chapter? |
| Emotional Resonance | Does this describe *my* life, not a textbook? |
| Actionability | Do I know what to do differently tomorrow? |
| Memory Impact | Will I remember one line from this chapter a week later? |
| Commercial Readiness | Could I receive this chapter as a paid deliverable today? |

After reading the complete set (Parts 01–06 + Part 08), can the customer answer:

| Question | Answer | Evidence |
|----------|--------|----------|
| **Who am I?** | **Yes** | Part 01 (durable inner strength) + Part 03 (long-horizon structured self) + Part 08 §1 synthesise a coherent identity: trusted carrier of responsibility with a stable frame. |
| **Why am I like this?** | **Yes** | Part 02 explains daily operating rhythm; Part 03 explains structural preference for foundation and consistency. Part 08 §2 unifies both as “frame says go steady, engine says carry.” |
| **What helps me?** | **Yes** | Part 04 (release through output), Part 05 (external support when door is open), Part 08 §3. Internal recovery layer from Part 02 included. |
| **What blocks me?** | **Yes** | Self-carry, late fatigue, thin boundaries, rigidity — across Parts 01, 02, 04 and consolidated in Part 08 §4. |

# Top Strengths

1. **One coherent story across six domains** — strength → operating system → structure → balance → external weather → life chapter → integrated consulting. Rare in automated BaZi products; reads human.

2. **Part 02 and Part 04 are commercial-grade** — high recognition (“invisible pressure”, “silent overload”) and high actionability. These are the chapters a customer would quote to a friend.

3. **Part 08 is a true synthesis, not a summary** — single consultant voice, memorable closing line, strategic focus table. Suitable as the **primary paid deliverable**.

4. **Trust through restraint** — avoids event promises, avoids cát/hung drama, avoids score worship. Sophisticated customers will pay for this tone.

5. **Cross-layer tension named honestly** — “khung nói đi chắc, máy nói làm chịu” is the intellectual and emotional spine of the whole report. Makes the set feel integrated, not modular.

---

# Top Weaknesses

1. **Part 06 under-delivers for paying expectations** — “Life Timeline” chapter with one published decade feels incomplete. Honesty preserves trust but **hurts perceived value** unless set as “current chapter focus” in product packaging.

2. **Part 03 is the driest chapter** — structurally important but lower emotional pull. Some customers will disengage mid-report unless Part 08 is positioned as the destination.

3. **Residual specialist vocabulary** — *Thân Vượng*, *Chính Ấn*, *Thất Sát*, *Thực Thần*, *Ất Tỵ* appear in customer prose. Context usually suffices, but first-time buyers may still feel “inside the system” rather than “outside with a consultant.”

4. **Part 05 is uniformly positive** — no published external risk stars means the chapter can feel like reassurance without edge. Customers in crisis may want “what external pressure looks like when it goes wrong.”

5. **Appendices in Parts 01–06 leak engineering tone** — if shipped to customers as-is (NarrativePlan, runtime limitations, cross-chapter review tables), trust tone shifts from consultant to audit. **Commercial packaging should hide appendices** or move to internal QA only.

6. **Missing Part 07** — numbering jumps 06 → 08. Minor, but a paying customer may notice a gap in the “complete journey.”

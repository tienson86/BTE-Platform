---

# 1. When

Too few eligible units (e.g. EC-07: conclusion + why only).

---

# 2. Allowed

- shorter interpretation
- `insufficient_data` shells for empty required sections (Luck, or domains with no surviving units)
- `verbosity = short` as the honest shape

---

# 3. Forbidden

- generic filler to reach a page length
- borrowing Pattern / Useful God
- inventing luck
- duplicating Why into Meaning just to fill Meaning

If Meaning has no distinct unit, Meaning may compress into a single qualified sentence from Conclusion+Why **only if** that sentence still answers So What and is traced. Prefer a short Meaning from class-level unit if eligible. If even that fails, Meaning = insufficient, not a dictionary definition.

| Field | Value |

| Document | CONFLICT_REASONING |

---

# 1. Conflict types

| Type | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| `FACT_CONFLICT` | Two published facts disagree in polarity (C1) |
| `KNOWLEDGE_CONFLICT` | Two units’ claims cannot both be true as stated |
| `DOMAIN_CONFLICT` | Same person, two domains imply opposite life policies |
| `ADVICE_CONFLICT` | Do vs do, or do vs avoid, without a condition |
| `CONFIDENCE_CONFLICT` | Claim requires high confidence; input band is low |

---

# 2. Contradiction vs nuance

**TRUE CONTRADICTION:** both claims cannot hold in the same condition.

**CONDITIONAL NUANCE:** both hold in different conditions.

Example:

- Strong → tends to be proactive  
- When Quan Sát / control is strong → may become guarded under pressure  

These are **nuance**, not contradiction. Keep both with context (`qualify`).

Do not delete one side because the outline looks cleaner.

---

# 3. Actions

| Action | When | Customer Mode |
|--------|------|----------------|
| `resolve` | Engine already resolved facts; tell the lived synthesis | one qualified weather |
| `qualify` | nuance / C1 | both sides with “and / when” |
| `defer` | cannot tell without missing data | Validation + insufficient |
| `expose` | true contradiction unresolved | Validation Mode conflict record; Customer does **not** get two unexplained opposites |

---

# 4. Advice safety (Strength)

Forbidden customer advice from Strength alone:

- “You must quit your job.”
- “You must invest.”
- Any guaranteed outcome

Allowed:

- tendency
- consideration
- suitable operating style
- do/avoid paired with a kept implication

`absolute = true` advice claims are ineligible.

---

# 5. Customer Mode rule

Two opposite assertions without a qualifier are a defect.

Correct: “You can decide and stay decided; under sitting pressure you may hear correction and still not revise.”

Incorrect: “You are decisive.” / “You hesitate under pressure.” as two unexplained headlines.

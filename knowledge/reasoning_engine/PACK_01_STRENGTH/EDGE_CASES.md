---

# EC-01 Strong sát Balanced

Boundary class. Alternative material. Conclusion `qualified`. Drop Very-Strong surplus units. Keep Alternative in Validation.

---

# EC-02 Very Strong with evidence conflict

Do not hide weakening facts. Why must include conflict. Customer qualifier required. Do not glorify.

---

# EC-03 Strong but root data missing

Root units ineligible or partial. Why without root. Missing Data lists root. Do not invent Thông căn.

---

# EC-04 Many duplicate Strong units

Duplicate pipeline + budget. One representative per synonym family.

---

# EC-05 Recommendation conflict

Two recs disagree without condition → `ADVICE_CONFLICT`: qualify, or keep the one chained to higher-salience implication, expose in Validation.

---

# EC-06 No luck data

Luck shell insufficient. No “luck has no effect”. No luck units.

---

# EC-07 Only conclusion + why

Short plan. Do not pad. Optional domains omitted with reason. Recs only if chain exists; else rec section insufficient or omitted.

---

# EC-08 Strong evidence, low interpretation confidence

Why can name present facts (`qualified`). Firm life advice dropped (`REJECTED_CONFIDENCE_TOO_LOW`). Alternative likely kept in Validation.

---

# EC-09 High knowledge priority, low customer value

Narrative / salience wins. Generic high-priority textbook unit loses to cause-specific medium-priority unit.

---

# EC-10 One domain has too many units

Domain cap (e.g. Career 1–3). Cut by salience. `REJECTED_NARRATIVE_BUDGET`.

---

# CASE-0001 mapping

EC-06 applies (luck). C1 is a live FACT_CONFLICT (not EC-01 boundary — score 0.87 is not sát 0.65). Thin root is a qualifier, not missing root (not EC-03).

# Narrative Compression

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Document | NARRATIVE_COMPRESSION |
| Version | 1.0.0 |

---

# 1. When

Eligible units exceed Narrative Budget.

---

# 2. Keep

- specific
- high evidence
- high life impact
- actionable (if chained)
- C1 both polarities in Why if they fit max 4

---

# 3. Remove

- generic class slogans
- repetition / synonym families
- low-value secondary facets
- extra domain units beyond cap
- unpaired recommendations

---

# 4. Method

Sort by salience descending inside each slot; cut from the tail.

Record `REJECTED_NARRATIVE_BUDGET` on dropped units.

Do not silently drop C1’s weakening cause to make Why “cleaner”.

---

END

# 1. Purpose

This document catalogs the fundamental domains owned by this Knowledge Module.

---

# 2. Domain Catalog

## 2.1 Yin Yang

Canonical polarity system.

Defines:

- Yin
- Yang
- polarity relationships applicable to stems, branches, and elements

---

## 2.2 Wu Xing

Five Element system.

Defines:

- Wood
- Fire
- Earth
- Metal
- Water

And their generative / controlling relationship framework.

---

## 2.3 Heavenly Stems

Canonical Heavenly Stem catalog and attributes.

Defines stem identity, polarity, and elemental affiliation.

---

## 2.4 Earthly Branches

Canonical Earthly Branch catalog and attributes.

Defines branch identity, polarity, elemental affiliation, and seasonal associations.

---

## 2.5 Hidden Stems

Canonical hidden stem composition of Earthly Branches.

Defines principal and residual hidden stem membership.

---

## 2.6 Chang Sheng

Canonical twelve-stage life cycle relative to stems and branches.

Defines ordered stage identities and structural progression.

---

## 2.7 Na Yin

Canonical Na Yin pairings of stem-branch combinations.

Defines Na Yin identity and elemental affiliation of pairs.

---

## 2.8 Ten Gods Relationships

Canonical Ten Gods relationship framework relative to Day Master.

Defines relationship classes only.

Does not define quality scoring or interpretive ranking rules.

---

## 2.9 Five Element Relationships

Canonical generation, control, and related elemental interaction definitions.

---

## 2.10 Stem Relationships

Canonical stem-to-stem relationship definitions.

---

## 2.11 Branch Relationships

Canonical branch-to-branch relationship definitions, including structural combination classes as taxonomy only.

Business activation or transformation rules belong to Combination Knowledge.

---

## 2.12 Season Definitions

Canonical seasonal frames and solar-term seasonal association definitions.

---

## 2.13 Climate Definitions

Canonical climate definition frames used as shared vocabulary for temperature/climate domains.

Does not define climate scoring or adjustment business rules.

---

## 2.14 Terminology

Shared controlled vocabulary spanning all fundamental domains.

---

# 3. Domain Boundaries

Each domain above is definitional.

Domain Knowledge Modules may reference these definitions.

They must not redefine them.

---

# 4. Non-Overlap Rule

If a concept is fundamental and shared, it belongs here.

If a concept encodes domain decision logic, it belongs in a domain Knowledge Module.

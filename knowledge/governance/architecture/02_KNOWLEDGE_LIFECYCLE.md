# Knowledge Lifecycle Architecture

## Purpose

Describe the lifecycle of every Knowledge Asset.

---

# Lifecycle

```
Idea

↓

Draft

↓

Review

↓

Approved

↓

Released

↓

Maintained

↓

Deprecated

↓

Archived
```

---

# State Definitions

Idea

Business need identified.

Draft

Content being created.

Review

Quality verification.

Approved

Accepted by Governance.

Released

Official asset.

Maintained

Regular updates.

Deprecated

Replacement planned.

Archived

Read-only.

---

# Entry Criteria

Every state has mandatory entry conditions.

---

# Exit Criteria

Every transition requires validation.

---

# Responsibilities

Author

Reviewer

Governance

Release Manager

---

# Validation Rules

No asset may skip Review.

No asset may be Released without Approval.

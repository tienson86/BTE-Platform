# 1. Purpose

This specification defines the official structure of every chapter within the BTE Knowledge Canon.

Its objectives are:

- Ensure consistency across all knowledge modules.
- Improve readability.
- Enable automated parsing.
- Support AI processing.
- Support Rule Database generation.
- Support future documentation generation.

Every chapter SHALL follow this specification.

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to all knowledge chapters under:

- knowledge/bazi/
- knowledge/numerology/
- knowledge/meihua/

---

# 3. Chapter Principles

Every chapter shall satisfy the following principles.

## 3.1 Single Topic

A chapter explains only one concept.

Example

✔ Chapter 03 - Heavenly Stems

✘ Heavenly Stems and Earthly Branches

---

## 3.2 Progressive Learning

Knowledge shall be presented from simple to complex.

Recommended order:

Definition

↓

Theory

↓

Principles

↓

Applications

↓

Examples

↓

Case Studies

---

## 3.3 Reusability

A chapter shall be reusable by:

- Humans
- AI
- Rule Engine
- Documentation Generator

---

# 4. Standard Chapter Structure

Every chapter SHALL contain the following sections.

---

## Metadata

YAML Metadata

---

## Learning Objectives

Describe what the reader will learn.

Example

- Understand Yin and Yang.
- Explain Five Elements.
- Apply the concept in Bazi analysis.

---

## Prerequisites

Knowledge required before reading.

Example

Before reading this chapter, the reader should understand:

- Yin Yang
- Five Elements

---

## Introduction

Brief overview.

Historical background.

Purpose.

---

## Definitions

Official definitions.

Every important term shall be defined.

---

## Core Principles

Explain the fundamental theories.

No interpretation.

No examples.

Pure knowledge.

---

## Detailed Explanation

Explain each principle.

Use subsections.

Example

### Principle 1

...

### Principle 2

...

---

## Relationships

Explain relationships with other concepts.

Example

This chapter relates to:

- Ten Gods
- Useful God
- Pattern

---

## Applications

Describe practical usage.

Example

How the concept is used during Bazi analysis.

---

## Rule Summary

Summarize all rules.

Do NOT include Rule IDs.

Only summarize the logic.

---

## Exceptions

List all exceptions.

Explain why.

---

## Common Mistakes

Describe common misunderstandings.

Explain the correct interpretation.

---

## Examples

Every chapter shall contain examples.

Recommended:

3–10 examples.

Structure

Background

↓

Analysis

↓

Conclusion

---

## Case Studies

Every important chapter shall contain real-world cases.

Structure

Scenario

↓

Observation

↓

Analysis

↓

Lessons Learned

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

Recommended.

List common questions.

Provide concise answers.

---

## Rule Mapping

Reference related Rule Database modules.

Example

Strength Rules

Temperature Rules

Combination Rules

---

## Knowledge Mapping

Reference related chapters.

Example

01 Fundamental

↓

02 Strength

↓

05 Useful God

---

## References

Books

Classical texts

Research

Internal references

---

## Glossary

List important terms.

Each glossary entry contains:

Term

Definition

Reference

---

## Revision History

Version history.

# 5. Chapter Length

Recommended size

| Type | Recommended Length |
|------|--------------------|
| Small | 5–10 pages |
| Medium | 10–20 pages |
| Large | 20–40 pages |

No maximum limit.

Quality is more important than length.

---

# 6. Knowledge Quality Requirements

A chapter SHALL NOT be frozen until it satisfies all requirements.

## Content

- Complete
- Accurate
- Consistent

## Examples

- Minimum three examples

## Case Studies

- Minimum one

## References

- Present

## Rule Mapping

- Completed

## Glossary

- Completed

---

# 7. Writing Style

Writing shall be:

- Clear
- Objective
- Academic
- Structured
- Consistent

Avoid:

- Emotional language
- Personal opinions
- Ambiguous statements

---

# 8. AI Compatibility

A chapter shall support:

- AI Retrieval
- Semantic Search
- Rule Extraction
- Knowledge Graph
- Future LLM Training

Each section shall remain independent whenever possible.

---

# 9. Freeze Policy

A chapter may enter Official status only after:

- Technical Review
- Knowledge Review
- Consistency Review
- Rule Mapping Review
- Final Approval

After freezing:

Content SHALL NOT be modified directly.

Any change requires a new version.

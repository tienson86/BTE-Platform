# 1. Purpose

This guide defines the editorial style for all knowledge content within the BTE Knowledge Canon.

Its objectives are:

- Maintain a consistent writing style.
- Improve readability.
- Standardize terminology.
- Support multilingual publishing.
- Support AI processing.

---

# 2. Writing Principles

Knowledge shall be:

- Accurate
- Objective
- Concise
- Consistent
- Educational
- Traceable

Avoid:

- Personal opinions
- Emotional language
- Ambiguous wording
- Marketing language

---

# 3. Language Rules

Primary language:

- Vietnamese

Future supported languages:

- English
- Simplified Chinese

---

# 4. Terminology Rules

All official terminology SHALL come from the Terminology Registry.

Do not invent new terminology inside chapters.

---

# 5. Chinese Terminology

Where applicable, include:

- Chinese characters
- Pinyin
- Official Vietnamese term
- Official English term

Example:

| Chinese | Pinyin | Vietnamese | English |
|----------|---------|------------|----------|
| 用神 | Yòng Shén | Dụng Thần | Useful God |

---

# 6. Writing Style

Prefer:

- Short paragraphs
- Active voice
- Clear explanations
- One idea per paragraph

Avoid:

- Long sentences
- Redundant explanations

---

# 7. Examples

Every major concept should include examples.

Examples shall follow the official Case Study Standard when appropriate.

---

# 8. Tables

Use tables for:

- Comparisons
- Mappings
- Structured data

Avoid tables for narrative explanations.

---

# 9. Diagrams

Preferred formats:

- Mermaid
- Flowcharts
- Decision Trees

All diagrams should include captions and IDs.

---

# 10. Cross References

Use official IDs when referring to:

- Knowledge Assets
- Rules
- Cases
- References
- Glossary Entries

---

# 11. Editorial Checklist

Before publication verify:

- [ ] Terminology compliant
- [ ] Grammar checked
- [ ] References complete
- [ ] Examples included
- [ ] Tables validated
- [ ] Diagrams validated
- [ ] IDs verified

---

# 12. Compliance

Any document that does not follow this guide SHALL NOT be published as an Official Knowledge Asset.

# 1. Purpose

This specification defines the quality requirements for every Knowledge Asset within the BTE Knowledge Canon.

Its objectives are:

- Ensure correctness.
- Ensure completeness.
- Ensure consistency.
- Ensure traceability.
- Ensure maintainability.
- Ensure AI readiness.

No Knowledge Asset may become Official unless it satisfies this standard.

---

# 2. Quality Principles

Every Knowledge Asset SHALL satisfy the following principles.

## Accuracy

Knowledge shall be factually correct.

---

## Completeness

The topic shall be covered sufficiently for its intended scope.

---

## Consistency

No contradictions with other official Knowledge Assets.

---

## Traceability

Every important conclusion shall reference authoritative sources.

---

## Clarity

Knowledge shall be understandable by its intended audience.

---

## Reusability

Knowledge shall support reuse across engines and documents.

---

## Maintainability

Knowledge shall be easy to update without introducing inconsistencies.

---

## AI Readiness

Knowledge shall be structured for automated processing.

---

# 4. Acceptance Criteria

Every Knowledge Asset SHALL:

- Have complete metadata.
- Use official terminology.
- Reference authoritative sources.
- Follow Markdown Standard.
- Follow Chapter Standard (where applicable).
- Pass review.
- Pass validation.

---

# 5. Review Levels

Level 1 — Technical Review

- Structure
- Metadata
- IDs
- Formatting

---

Level 2 — Knowledge Review

- Accuracy
- Completeness
- Logic

---

Level 3 — Editorial Review

- Language
- Style
- Terminology

---

Level 4 — Final Approval

- Governance compliance
- Release readiness

---

# 6. Quality Score

Each asset shall be evaluated using a 100-point model.

| Category | Weight |
|----------|-------:|
| Accuracy | 30 |
| Completeness | 20 |
| Consistency | 15 |
| Traceability | 15 |
| Readability | 10 |
| Maintainability | 5 |
| AI Readiness | 5 |

Minimum score for Official release: **90/100**.

---

# 7. Validation Checklist

- [ ] Metadata complete
- [ ] IDs valid
- [ ] References verified
- [ ] Terminology compliant
- [ ] No duplicate content
- [ ] Rule Mapping complete
- [ ] Traceability complete
- [ ] Peer reviewed
- [ ] Approved

---

# 8. Compliance

Any Knowledge Asset that does not satisfy this standard SHALL NOT be released as Official.

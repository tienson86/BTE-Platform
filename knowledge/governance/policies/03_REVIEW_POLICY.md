# 1. Purpose

This policy defines the official review process for all Knowledge Assets within the BTE Platform.

Its objectives are:

- Ensure quality.
- Ensure consistency.
- Prevent incorrect knowledge.
- Improve maintainability.

---

# 2. Scope

Applies to all official assets.

---

# 3. Review Principles

- Independent review.
- Evidence-based review.
- Documented review.
- Repeatable review.

---

# 4. Review Types

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Technical Review | Structure and compliance |
| Knowledge Review | Accuracy and completeness |
| Editorial Review | Language and style |
| Governance Review | Policy compliance |

---

# 5. Review Workflow

```
Draft
   ↓
Technical Review
   ↓
Knowledge Review
   ↓
Editorial Review
   ↓
Governance Review
   ↓
Approval
```

---

# 6. Review Checklist

- Metadata valid
- IDs valid
- References verified
- Traceability complete
- Terminology compliant
- Examples reviewed
- Case Studies verified
- Rule Mapping completed

---

# 7. Review Outcomes

- Approved
- Approved with Minor Revisions
- Revisions Required
- Rejected

---

# 8. Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| Author | Prepare submission |
| Reviewer | Evaluate objectively |
| Governance Team | Final verification |

---

# 9. Audit

Every review SHALL generate:

- Review ID
- Reviewer
- Date
- Result
- Comments

---

# 10. Related Standards

- Knowledge Quality Standard
- Style Guide
- Traceability Standard

---

# 11. Compliance

Assets without completed review SHALL NOT be released.

---

# 1. Purpose

This policy defines the official process for deprecating Knowledge Assets while preserving historical integrity and backward traceability.

---

# 2. Scope

Applies to:

- Knowledge Canon
- Rule Database
- Sentence Library
- Phrase Library
- Terminology Registry
- Reference Registry
- Metadata Registry
- Report Templates

---

# 3. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| Deprecated Asset | An asset no longer recommended for future use but still retained. |
| Replacement Asset | The official successor to a deprecated asset. |

---

# 4. Policy Principles

- Assets SHALL NOT be deleted directly.
- Deprecation SHALL be documented.
- A replacement SHOULD be identified whenever possible.
- Existing references SHALL remain valid.

---

# 5. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| Author | Submit deprecation request |
| Reviewer | Assess impact |
| Governance Team | Approve deprecation |
| Release Manager | Publish updated status |

---

# 6. Deprecation Requirements

A deprecation request SHALL include:

- Asset ID
- Reason
- Replacement (if applicable)
- Impact Analysis
- Effective Date
- Approval Record

---

# 7. Workflow

```
Identify Asset
      ↓
Impact Analysis
      ↓
Deprecation Request
      ↓
Review
      ↓
Approval
      ↓
Mark as Deprecated
      ↓
Notify Consumers
```

---

# 8. Exceptions

Emergency security or legal issues may require immediate deprecation.

---

# 9. Compliance

Deprecated assets SHALL remain traceable and accessible.

---

# 10. Audit Requirements

Maintain:

- Deprecation ID
- Date
- Reason
- Reviewer
- Approver

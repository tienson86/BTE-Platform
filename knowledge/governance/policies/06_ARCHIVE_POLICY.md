---

# 1. Purpose

Define the rules for archiving obsolete or inactive Knowledge Assets while preserving long-term accessibility and traceability.

---

# 2. Scope

Applies to every official Knowledge Asset.

---

# 3. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| Archive | Long-term read-only preservation of an asset. |

---

# 4. Policy Principles

- Archive SHALL preserve history.
- Archive SHALL be read-only.
- Archived assets SHALL keep their IDs.
- Archived assets SHALL remain searchable.

---

# 5. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| Governance Team | Archive approval |
| Repository Administrator | Archive execution |
| Auditor | Verify archive integrity |

---

# 6. Archive Requirements

Archived assets SHALL retain:

- Metadata
- IDs
- References
- Change History
- Review History

---

# 7. Workflow

```
Inactive Asset
      ↓
Eligibility Review
      ↓
Approval
      ↓
Archive
      ↓
Integrity Validation
```

---

# 8. Exceptions

Legal or regulatory requirements may override normal retention schedules.

---

# 9. Compliance

Archived assets SHALL NOT be modified.

---

# 10. Audit Requirements

Record:

- Archive Date
- Archive Reason
- Integrity Check
- Responsible Person

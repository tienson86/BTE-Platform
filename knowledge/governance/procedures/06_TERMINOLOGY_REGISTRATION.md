# 1. Purpose

Define the official procedure for registering, reviewing, approving and maintaining terminology used throughout the BTE Knowledge Canon.

---

# 2. Scope

Applies to:

- Official Terms
- Chinese Terms
- Pinyin
- Vietnamese Terms
- English Terms
- Synonyms
- Deprecated Terms
- Abbreviations

---

# 3. Preconditions

- Term identified
- No duplicate entry
- Reliable source available
- Classification defined

---

# 4. Inputs

- Official Name
- Chinese Characters
- Pinyin
- Definition
- References
- Related Assets

---

# 5. Roles

Registrar

Knowledge Reviewer

Editorial Reviewer

Governance Reviewer

Approver

---

# 6. Procedure Steps

1. Verify term necessity
2. Search Terminology Registry
3. Assign Glossary ID
4. Record multilingual names
5. Write definition
6. Link references
7. Link related assets
8. Review
9. Publish

---

# 7. Outputs

- Terminology Record
- Registry Entry
- Metadata
- Relationship Mapping

---

# 8. Validation

Verify:

- Unique ID
- Definition complete
- References valid
- Related Assets linked
- Translation verified

---

# 9. Exceptions

Experimental terminology SHALL remain Draft.

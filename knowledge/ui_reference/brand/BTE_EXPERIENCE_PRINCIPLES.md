# 1. Purpose

This document defines the highest-level experience principles of the BTE Platform.

It does not describe components.

It does not describe layouts.

It does not describe implementation.

Instead, it defines the experience that every user should consistently receive.

Every product decision should align with these principles.

---

# 2. The BTE Mission

BTE exists to transform traditional BaZi knowledge into a structured, explainable and actionable decision-support system.

The goal is not to predict fate.

The goal is to help users understand patterns, evaluate possibilities and make better-informed decisions.

Every feature should reinforce this mission.

---

# 3. The Experience Pyramid

Every user interaction should follow the same hierarchy.

Experience

↓

Trust

↓

Understanding

↓

Insight

↓

Action

↓

Reflection

Technology supports the experience.

It never becomes the experience.

---

# 4. First Impression Principle

Within the first ten seconds,

users should feel

Professionalism

Clarity

Order

Trust

The interface should immediately communicate

"This analysis has been carefully prepared."

Users should never feel overwhelmed by information.

---

# 5. Progressive Understanding

Knowledge should unfold gradually.

Overview

↓

Evidence

↓

Analysis

↓

Interpretation

↓

Recommendation

↓

Learning

The user should become more confident with each step.

Never present every detail immediately.

---

# 6. Explain Before Recommend

Recommendations without explanations reduce trust.

Every recommendation should answer

What

↓

Why

↓

Evidence

↓

Expected Outcome

↓

Optional Details

Explanation always precedes persuasion.

---

# 7. Teach While Analyzing

Every interaction should increase the user's understanding.

BTE is not only an analytical platform.

It is also an educational platform.

Users should leave knowing more than when they arrived.

Learning is part of the product experience.

---

# 8. Calm Over Excitement

The interface should encourage thoughtful reading.

Avoid creating urgency through design.

Avoid unnecessary visual stimulation.

The platform should feel calm,

focused,

and respectful.

Confidence is built through clarity,

not excitement.

---

# 9. Transparency Builds Trust

The system should reveal

How conclusions were reached.

What evidence was used.

Which assumptions were applied.

Where uncertainty exists.

Users trust explanations more than assertions.

---

# 10. One Decision at a Time

The interface should help users focus on the next meaningful decision.

Avoid presenting too many actions simultaneously.

Every screen should naturally answer

"What should I understand next?"

"What should I do next?"

---

# 11. Reading Before Interaction

BTE is a reading-first platform.

Interactions support understanding.

They should never interrupt it.

Scrolling,

expanding,

and navigation

must feel natural and predictable.

---

# 12. Respect the User's Attention

Attention is valuable.

Every component must justify its presence.

Every visual element must support understanding.

Remove anything that distracts from insight.

Less interface.

More meaning.

---

# 13. Structured Thinking

Every analysis should follow a repeatable structure.

Observe

↓

Explain

↓

Evaluate

↓

Recommend

↓

Learn

Users should always know where they are within the analytical journey.

---

# 14. Confidence Without Certainty

Traditional metaphysics involves interpretation.

The interface should communicate confidence in the analytical process,

not certainty about outcomes.

Avoid absolute language.

Prefer evidence-based reasoning.

---

# 15. Human Expertise Amplified

BTE should feel like working alongside an experienced consultant.

Artificial intelligence,

automation,

and calculations

exist to amplify expertise,

not replace it.

The product should always feel human-guided.

---

# 16. Consistency Creates Trust

Consistency is more important than novelty.

Users should encounter

consistent terminology,

consistent structure,

consistent interactions,

consistent recommendations.

Familiarity reduces cognitive effort.

---

# 17. Reflection Before Completion

The experience should not end after presenting recommendations.

Users should leave with

greater understanding,

greater confidence,

and a clearer perspective.

The final emotion should be

"I understand my situation better."

not

"I received a prediction."

---

# 18. Definition of Success

A successful BTE experience is achieved when users say

"I understand why."

before asking

"What should I do?"

Understanding creates confidence.

Confidence enables action.

Action becomes meaningful only after understanding.

---

# 19. Experience Anti-Patterns

The following experiences are prohibited.

Making users feel overwhelmed.

Encouraging superstition.

Creating artificial urgency.

Using mysterious language instead of explanations.

Presenting recommendations without evidence.

Treating users as passive recipients.

Allowing interface decoration to overshadow analysis.

Breaking the analytical reading journey.

---

# 20. The BTE Promise

Every interaction should reinforce one promise.

"We help you understand before you decide."

This promise is the foundation of every feature,

every screen,

every report,

and every recommendation within the BTE Platform.

---

# Experience Constitution

Whenever design,

engineering,

or product decisions conflict,

choose the option that

improves understanding,

strengthens trust,

reduces cognitive load,

and supports thoughtful decision-making.

The experience always comes first.

If season, root, support, and control are present, Why must mention the lived weather of those four — not a textbook of all causes.

# 5. Meaning / Advantages / Challenges

From class-matching units after caps and duplicate removal.

Challenges ≠ negated Advantages.

# 9. Executive Summary

Compression of 1–8. No new claims. 5–8 lines.

If Luck was insufficient, omit the luck job rather than invent it.

---

# 10. Leak ban (absolute)

Mode B must not contain:

- Rule IDs (`sea_002`, `STR-…`, `pri_level_strong`)
- Confidence / percents
- Scores (`0.87`, `season_score`)
- Raw tokens (`male`, `strong`, `success`, `hot`)
- Dumps (`{'name':'Đại Hàn','index':23}`)
- Engine / pack names
- Selected unit IDs
- Expert-disagreement internals

---

If a paragraph cannot name that job, the composer must drop it.

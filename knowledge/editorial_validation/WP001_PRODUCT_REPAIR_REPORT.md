## 2. Executive Summary improvement

Before: thesis opening plus leftover climate (`Thực Thần: Thân vượng cần tiết khí`) filling spare slots. No first action. Cover class often disagreed with the body.

After: at most six sentences covering identity, governing structure, corrective direction, principal risk, first action, and current Da Yun frame.

Sơn now reads, in one minute: Người tự gánh · surplus on Chính Ấn · finish visible work · do not add load · first move = one output channel · Ất Tỵ 2022–2031.

Huỳnh and Tân use the same slot with different theses. Climate fragments are no longer appended.

---

## 3. Reasoning improvement

Before: synthesis sentence, then independent Pattern / Strength / Useful God textbooks, then Ten Gods and Shen Sha catalogues.

After: one synthesis (Pattern + Strength → corrective direction) plus one Fact → Reason → Therefore chain from the governing Useful God. Ten God / Shen Sha encyclopedias are not rendered in Reasoning.

Remaining: the Therefore line can still echo the Useful God climate label (`Thực Thần: Thân vượng cần tiết khí`). That is copied knowledge, not a new catalogue.

---

## 4. Career improvement

Before: Pattern leftover (`mentor / không suy ra nghề giáo`) plus dumped recommendations and a luck timestamp.

After: one work-structure paragraph from the case thesis. How this person works, what structure suits them, what hurts them. No profession list. Dedicated career chapter uses only that paragraph.

Sơn: finish visible work; do not turn the job into extra load.  
Huỳnh: refine at the heat point; do not freeze-cut too early.  
Tân: cut surplus and install usable discipline; do not sprout more frames.

---

## 5. Finance improvement

Before: thesis fragment plus keep-source / disclaimer refrain.

After: one finance thesis per chart, no second fragment.

## 6. Relationship improvement

Before: case-true opener plus Ấn-generic “bọc người khác” clones.

After: one relationship thesis per chart (kho chứa việc / chỗ tính-cắt / mở thêm khung).

## 7. Health improvement

Before: recovery advice plus repeated `Không chẩn đoán`.

After: one paragraph. Repeated medical disclaimer collapsed. Sơn/Huỳnh: rest after load. Tân: keep hot-cold in bound.

## 8. Recommendation improvement

Before: five items including engine recs (`Giữ nhãn Nhật Chủ`), Hỷ-list rituals (`Đặt X làm trọng tâm`), and unranked dumps.

After: maximum five, numbered, each starting with Làm / Tránh / Xây / Củng cố / Giảm / Dùng. Generic rituals dropped.

Sơn first action is now a limiter: one output channel, then stop.  
Huỳnh first action is refine/quality.  
Tân first action is cut with scope.

Before: thesis plus Ten God / Nhật Chủ encyclopedia restart.

After: one memorable ending. No catalogue.

Example (Sơn): Người tự gánh · finish visible work · risk is leftover strength used as permission to pile load · keep this on Chính Ấn.

Do not treat those as reasons to build Story Engine, Luck Domain, or a new composer next. They are leftover selection leaks.

# Cross-case similarity review

Method: compare customer PDF prose (executive summary, reasoning spine, career, recommendations, warnings, conclusion).  
Distinguish **legitimate similarity** (shared pattern or shared Useful God *function*) from **template collapse** (same consultation with swapped tokens).

## Mandatory pair — Sơn vs Tân

| | EV-0001 Nguyễn Tiến Sơn | EV-0003 Ngô Đặng Minh Tân |
|--|-------------------------|---------------------------|
| Day Master | Canh Kim | Bính Hỏa |
| Strength | strong / Thân vượng / 0.87 | balanced / Trung hòa / 0.54 |

| Five elements | Earth 6, Water 1 | Wood 6, Fire 1, Metal 1 |

| Body thesis | Người tự gánh | Người chỉnh trục |
| Cover class | Người tự gánh | Người điều tiết *(mismatch)* |

### What legitimately stays similar

Both have **Chính Ấn** as month-command pattern. Shared *background* is allowed:

- learning / foundation / “chỗ dựa” as the Ấn axis
- “Không suy ra bằng cấp”
- mentor / study-process as *one* career hint for Ấn — **if** it is then specialised

A senior consultant could mention Ấn in both rooms without telling the same life story.

### What must differ

| Must differ | Sơn | Tân |
|-------------|-----|-----|
| Central person | Surplus bearer who finishes visible work | Young fire in thick wood who needs cutting/discipline, not more growth frames |
| Core tension | Capacity vs overload | Growth vs containment |
| Useful God job | Drain surplus into completed output | Metal cuts wood surplus; standard, not more Ấn-nurture |
| Life stage | Mid-career load | Teen / first adult choices |
| Current luck | Ất Tỵ as a period (currently unlist-interpreted) | Đinh Tỵ as a period (currently unlist-interpreted) |
| Risk | Adding load because strength remains | Adding frames because wood keeps sprouting |

### What currently remains too similar — and why

1. **Ấn career clone.** Both Impact/Career still say: *Hợp môi trường có mentor, quy trình học. Không suy ra nghề giáo.*  
   That sentence does not know Sơn is 39 and Tân is 18. It is pattern-token prose.

2. **Relationship clone.** Both: *Cần được nâng. Xung đột khi bọc người khác quá.*  
   Ấn-nurture leak. Tân’s wood-growth story should not default to “bọc người khác”.

3. **Finance clone.** Both: *Thiên giữ nguồn, ít bung. Rủi ro: trì không lưu thông.*  
   Sơn’s surplus-output and Tân’s cut-and-cap are different money lives. The sentence is a shared Ấn/keep-source fragment.

4. **Conclusion encyclopedia restart.** Both append Ten God / Nhật Chủ glossary after the thesis. Same pedagogical voice.

5. **Luck as a timestamp only.** Both name Da Yun; neither says what this decade does to *this* person.

6. **Observation skeleton** is identical by design (facts slot). Legitimate. The leak `balanced` on Tân is not.

**Editorial judgment:** Sơn vs Tân is **better than the historical collapse** at the thesis layer. Case Thesis Generator already splits *Người tự gánh* vs *Người chỉnh trục*. The remaining collapse is **domain application + pattern leftover + life stage**, not the opening thesis.

They still fail the product question in Career / Relationship / generic recs 4–5.

## Mandatory pair — Sơn vs Huỳnh

| | Sơn | Huỳnh |
|--|-----|-------|
| Pattern | Chính Ấn | Chính Tài |
| Useful God | Thực Thần | Đinh |
| Thesis (body) | Người tự gánh | Người kiến tạo |
| Cover | Người tự gánh | **Người tự gánh** (wrong) |

### Legitimate similarity

Both are **strong / Thân vượng**. Shared *surplus needs a channel* is structurally fair. Observation line `Thân vượng — sức dư cần được dẫn` is the same fact-type sentence — acceptable as observation, not as identity.

### What must differ — and does, in the body

Huỳnh’s “giữ nhiệt điểm, tinh luyện” vs Sơn’s “đưa sức dư ra thành việc hoàn thành” is a real split. Recommendations 1–3 also split (refine/quality vs one output channel).

### What remains too similar

- Cover class on Huỳnh is Sơn’s identity (`Người tự gánh`). Mapping/rendering defect.
- Conclusion still dumps Nhật Chủ / Kiếp Tài pedagogy on both.
- Strength observation doubled (`Thân vượng Thân vượng`).
- Health: both “giữ nhịp nghỉ sau pha gánh” — fair for two strong charts, weak as the only health story.

**Judgment:** Body theses are legitimately different. Cover identity is template collapse. This pair is the best evidence that Case Thesis Generator **helps the body** and **does not yet own the cover**.

## Sentence skeletons that recur (product-level)

1. `Lá số cho thấy xu hướng {strength gloss} trên nền {pattern}. Cấu trúc này phù hợp hơn khi {corrective}. Rủi ro tăng khi {risk}. Áp lực chính đi cùng hướng {UG} cần được giữ, không đảo ngược.`
2. `Sự nghiệp ổn định hơn khi vận hành theo {UG}. Hạn chế áp lực từ {Kỵ} khi nhận thêm cam kết.`
3. `Quan hệ cân bằng hơn khi duy trì không khí thuận ({Hỷ}); tránh kích hoạt Kỵ thần ({Kỵ}) trong tranh chấp.`
4. `Đặt {UG} làm trọng tâm khi cần điều tiết hệ.` / `Ưu tiên hành động gắn Hỷ thần: {list}.`
5. `Hệ vượng bị kìm / căng Hạn chế khuếch đại yếu tố bất lợi` (broken fragment; identical on child/Follow charts)
6. Conclusion: thesis (if any) + Ten God encyclopedia + Nhật Chủ system note

Skeleton 1 is **Case Thesis working**. It is a feature when the slots are case-true (Sơn, Huỳnh, Tân). It is collapse when the slots are routed only by Useful God stem (Trung, CASE-0003).

Skeletons 2–6 are **template collapse**, not legitimate pattern sharing.

## Answer to the product question

Across 10 PDFs:

- **This person** is audible in EV-0001, EV-0002, and the *opening* of EV-0003 and EV-0007.
- **Same consultation, different tokens** is the dominant mode from Reasoning’s second half through Conclusion, and is the entire report for EV-0005, EV-0006, EV-0008, EV-0009.
- Shared Chính Ấn (Sơn vs Tân) is **no longer the worst pair**. Shared Useful God stem is.

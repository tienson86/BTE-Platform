**claim**

Do not give them two lives and two manuals. Give them one standing plus a qualifier: this is not extreme; the other weather is real on the margin.

**supporting_points**

- The person has the main standing, and also a weekly experience of the neighbor.
- A Strong person who is tired like Weak every Sunday. A Weak person who can look Strong for a short, well-fed sprint.
- Name the main operating system. Name the leak or the extra feed.

**limitations**

- If the engine published a class, keep it.
- Do not say maybe the chart is wrong.
- Do not say percentages. Do not say you are both Strong and Weak. Do not give a second personality for the weekend.
- If the engine published nothing, do not invent a class.
- Edge knowledge tells what life feels like when the standing is not clean. It does not tell the engine how to decide the class.

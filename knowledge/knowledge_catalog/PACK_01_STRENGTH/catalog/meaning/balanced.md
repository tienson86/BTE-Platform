**claim**

The gift is range: you can push when the room needs push and yield when the room needs yield. The risk is waiting for the perfect middle while a real choice expires.

**supporting_points**

- You are not empty and you are not overflowing.
- People may call you easy to work with and also hard to read.
- Consultant phrasing (VI): Bạn không phải người “không có gì nổi bật”. Bạn đứng gần điểm cân. Điểm mạnh là chỉnh được nhịp. Điểm mù là chờ cân bằng hoàn hảo rồi mới chọn.

**limitations**

- Do not use when published classification is not balanced.
- Do not use to decide or upgrade the class.
- Do not dump Rule IDs, scores, or thresholds.
- Do not say Balanced people are average or have no personality.

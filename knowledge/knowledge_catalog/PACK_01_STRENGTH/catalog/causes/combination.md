**claim**

The customer may feel “I should be Strong, but I don’t feel it” or “I should be fragile, but I keep surviving”. The chart is not lying. A piece is not behaving as a still object.

**supporting_points**

- Combination can merge forces so the Day Master is no longer standing alone — or so a needed piece is pulled into another story.
- Clash can shake the ground. What looked like root becomes weather.
- Void can empty a piece that looked present on paper.

**limitations**

- If combination / clash / void is not published, do not narrate it.
- This is not a Pattern lecture.
- Do not invent a second class.
